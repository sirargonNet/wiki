Stuck? The game itself can help: `/guide` opens the guide, `/bug` reports a bug and `/report <player>` reports someone breaking the rules. For questions and news, head to the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net). If you can't get onto the server at all, see [Cannot connect?](/getting-started/help#cannot-connect).

## In-game guide

`/guide` or `/help` opens the **Guide**, which has these pages:

- Getting started
- Game modes
- Together
- Settings
- Help

So you don't forget it, the in-game sidebar reminds you: “New here? /guide”.

## What's new?

`/changelog` opens the **Changelog**. Pick a version to see its changes, sorted into groups such as Added, Changed and Fixed. Versions you haven't seen yet are marked **Unread**, and when there's something new, the chat reminds you as you join.

## Report a bug

Found a bug? Type `/bug`.

1. Choose a category: gameplay, connection, performance, interface, security or other.
2. Enter a title and a description. The game adds your position and version on its own. Keep passwords and other secrets out of it.
3. You get a code such as `BUG-123`.

If sending doesn't work straight away, the game tries again. Changed your mind? Type `/bug` again and click **Discard**.

`/bugs` lists your reports and their status: New, Confirmed, In progress, Resolved, Duplicate or Closed. Click one to read your description and the team's replies. Whenever the team replies, you get a message under `/account messages`. Your reports are on the website too.

Until you've seen a reply, the team can still correct or withdraw it. Once you've seen it or marked it as read, it stays as it is, and any correction comes as a new reply.

If one of your reports is marked **Duplicate**, the game and your web account show the original ticket's number and its current status. You don't get to see another player’s description or replies that way.

Helpful reports can earn a reward, but it's never automatic: the team decides case by case.

## Report a player

Someone is breaking the [rules](/reference/rules)? Type `/report <player>`, or click their message in chat. When you hover over it, it says “Click to report …”.

1. Under **Choose what happened**, pick the offence, for example Spam, Insult, Griefing or Inappropriate skin.
2. Under **Describe the incident**, tick the messages of theirs you saw, from chat and direct messages in the last 24 hours. The team also sees the conversation around them.
3. Add a description if you like.
4. Click **Send report**.

Afterwards the chat says: “Thank you. Your report about … has reached the team.” You won't be told what the team decides.

- If it's about a clan, such as an offensive clan name, pick the clan offence. The report then names the clan and its founder.
- In the waiting room, no form opens. Put the offence straight into the command instead: `/report <player> <offence> [description]`.
- If you send a lot of reports within an hour, you'll have to wait a while.

Please only report real rule breaks. False reports are against the rules.

## Recover your game state

Once in a while, the server can't confirm your game state straight away when you switch. **Recover game state** then opens, and your inventory stays protected in the meantime.

- **Wait (recommended):** The game keeps checking your state. This is usually the best choice.
- **Continue with the last saved state now:** You carry on playing right away, but items you got since the last save may be missing.
- **Report missing items:** The team can then check your game state.

Closed the window? `/profileinventory` brings it back, and `/profileinventory missing` reports missing items.

If an inventory edit was interrupted and an item is still being held, it won't get lost. Make room in your inventory and wait for it to come back. If the game reports a problem, try again later.

## Discord

The [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net) has news, help with getting started and people to play with.

Please don't DM individual team members with requests. Suggestions go in **#vorschläge** and questions in **#user-helfen-user**, while bugs are reported with `/bug`. To appeal, use the routes under [Team and reports](/reference/rules).

## Cannot connect?

- Check your Minecraft version: 26.3 or 26.2.
- Enable server resource packs, because you can't join without the network's resource pack.
- If the network is under maintenance, the game tells you. If the end is already set, it also shows you when the network reopens.
- If all lobbies are full, you wait in a queue. `/leavequeue` takes you out of it.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/guide` or `/help`: open the guide
- `/rules`: show the rules
- `/wiki`: open the wiki
- `/changelog` or `/changes`: show changes
- `/confirm`: open a pending confirmation
- `/resourcepack`: load a pending resource pack update now, or load a failed one again
- `/bug`: report a bug
- `/bugs`: your bug reports
- `/report <player> [offence] [description]`: report a player
- `/profileinventory`: recover your game state
- `/profileinventory missing`: report missing items

## Frequently asked questions

### I found a bug.

Report it with `/bug`. [Report a bug](/getting-started/help#report-a-bug) explains how.

### Someone is breaking the rules.

Report them with `/report <player>`. [Report a player](/getting-started/help#report-a-player) explains how.

### Where do I get help?

In game with `/guide` and on the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).
