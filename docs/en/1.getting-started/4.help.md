This page helps you in game and outside it. `/guide` opens the guide, `/bug` reports a bug and `/report <player>` reports a rule break. You find questions and news on the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net). If you cannot connect, read [Cannot connect?](/getting-started/help#cannot-connect).

## In-game guide

`/guide` or `/help` opens the **Guide**. It has these pages:

- Getting started
- Game modes
- Together
- Settings
- Help

The in-game sidebar reminds you: “New here? /guide”.

## What's new?

`/changelog` opens the **Changelog**. Pick a version. Its changes are sorted, for example into Added, Changed and Fixed. Versions you have not seen yet are marked **Unread**. When there is something new, the chat reminds you when you join.

## Report a bug

Found a bug? Type `/bug`.

1. Choose a category: gameplay, connection, performance, interface, security or other.
2. Enter a title and a description. The game adds your position and version itself. Do not include passwords or other secrets.
3. You get a code such as `BUG-123`.

If sending does not work right away, the game tries again. If you no longer want to send it, type `/bug` again and click **Discard**.

`/bugs` shows your reports with their status: New, Confirmed, In progress, Resolved, Duplicate or Closed. Click a report to read your description and the team's replies. When the team replies, you get a message under `/account messages`. You also see your reports on the website.

If one of your reports is a **Duplicate**, the game and web account show the original ticket number and its current status. This does not reveal another player’s description or replies.

Helpful reports can earn a reward. The team decides case by case. There is no automatic reward.

## Report a player

Someone is breaking the [rules](/reference/rules)? Type `/report <player>`. Or click the player's message in chat. Hovering over it shows “Click to report …”.

1. Under **Choose what happened**, pick the offence, for example Spam, Insult, Griefing or Inappropriate skin.
2. Under **Describe the incident**, tick the player's messages you saw. This covers chat and direct messages from the last 24 hours. The team also sees the conversation around them.
3. Optionally add a description.
4. Click **Send report**.

Afterwards the chat says: “Thank you. Your report about … has reached the team.” You are not told how the team decides.

- If it is about a clan, for example an offensive clan name, pick the clan offence. The report then names the clan and its founder.
- In the waiting room no form opens. Add the offence directly there: `/report <player> <offence> [description]`.
- If you report very often within an hour, you have to wait a while.

Only report real rule breaks. False reports are forbidden.

## Recover your game state

Rarely, the server cannot confirm your game state right away when you switch. Then **Recover game state** opens. Your inventory stays protected meanwhile.

- **Wait (recommended):** The game keeps checking your state. This is usually the best choice.
- **Continue with the last saved state now:** You play on at once. Items you got since the last save may be missing.
- **Report missing items:** The team can check your game state.

If you closed the window, `/profileinventory` opens it again. `/profileinventory missing` reports missing items.

If an item is still held after an interrupted inventory edit, it stays safe. Make room in your inventory and wait for its return. If the game reports a problem, try again later.

## Discord

The [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net) has news, help with getting started and people to play with.

Do not send requests to individual team members by direct message. Put suggestions in **#vorschläge** and questions in **#user-helfen-user**. Report bugs with `/bug`. For appeals, use the routes under [Team and reports](/reference/rules).

## Cannot connect?

- Check your Minecraft version: 26.3 or 26.2.
- Enable server resource packs. Without the network's resource pack you cannot join.
- If the network is under maintenance, the game tells you. When the end is set, you also see when it reopens.
- If all lobbies are full, you wait in a queue. `/leavequeue` leaves it.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/guide` or `/help`: open the guide
- `/rules`: show the rules
- `/wiki`: open the wiki
- `/changelog` or `/changes`: show changes
- `/confirm`: open a pending confirmation
- `/resourcepack`: load a pending resource pack update now, or load a failed one again
- `/bug`: report a bug
- `/bugs`: your bug reports
- `/report <player> [offence] [description]`: report a player
- `/profileinventory`: recover your game state
- `/profileinventory missing`: report missing items

## Frequently asked questions

### I found a bug.

Report it with `/bug`. More under [Report a bug](/getting-started/help#report-a-bug).

### Someone is breaking the rules.

Report them with `/report <player>`. More under [Report a player](/getting-started/help#report-a-player).

### Where do I get help?

In game with `/guide` and on the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).
