You land in the lobby after joining. From here you choose your game mode, meet friends and collect your streak rewards. There are several lobbies, and the Nether star in your hotbar switches between them. If all are full, you wait in a [queue](/getting-started/lobby#queue-and-afk).

## Hotbar

You cannot move or drop the hotbar items. Right-click uses them.

### Compass – Games

Opens the game modes. For each mode you see:

- how many are playing
- whether it is starting, restarting or under maintenance

Click a mode to connect. If it is full, you wait in a [queue](/getting-started/lobby#queue-and-afk).

### Firework star – Player visibility

Right-click switches between:

- **Players visible:** you see everyone
- **Selected players only:** you see friends, party and clan
- **Players hidden:** you see nobody

### Nether star – Lobbies

Shows all lobbies. Your current lobby is marked. Click another one to switch.

### Clock – Streak

Opens your [streak calendar](/guides/currencies#streak).

### Chest – Inventory

Opens your [inventory](/guides/inventory): network, SMP and Clan XP boosters, effects such as auras, trails and fireworks, and gadgets. Buy and activate boosters there for Argonite. The [price table](/guides/inventory#boosters) lists all three kinds.

You can activate network and Clan boosters in the lobby, even without a clan of your own. SMP XP boosters can only be activated in the SMP.

#### Gadget in the third slot

Equip a [gadget](/guides/inventory#gadgets) in `/inventory`. It appears in the third hotbar slot. Right-click to use it. The confetti cannon costs 200 Argonite once and shoots confetti with a bang every 1.5 seconds.

### Your head – My account

Opens your [account](/guides/account).

## NPCs

Right-click the NPCs:

- **SMP Season 10** and **CREATIVE** connect you directly.
- **GUIDE** opens the Guide.
- **Bugs** opens the bug report.
- **Changelog** shows the changes.
- **Wiki** opens this wiki.
- **CLAN** opens your clan.

### Double jump

Jump once more in mid-air. You can turn double jump off under `/settings lobby`.

### Pick up

- Right-click a player to lift them onto your head.
- Left-click throws them.
- Sneak to get down.

Whoever is carried can pick someone up too. This builds a tower of up to 5 players on top of the bottom one.

Who may pick you up is set under `/settings lobby`: **Everyone**, **Friends** or **Nobody**. With **Nobody**, you cannot pick anyone up either.

### Fell into the void?

If you fall out of the lobby, you land back at spawn.

## Lobby settings

Under `/settings lobby` you choose:

- which players you see
- where you land when you return
- whether you double jump
- who may pick you up

## Return

When you come back to the lobby, you land at your last position or at spawn. Set this under `/settings lobby`. On your next login you return to your last mode or to the lobby. Set this under `/settings network`.

## Flying

With a rank you can fly in the lobby: Prime, Elite, Legend, VIP or Media. Type `/fly`. The chat tells you “Flight is on.” or “Flight is off.” The game remembers your choice. You also find it under `/settings lobby` as **Lobby flight**.

## Queue and AFK

### Queue

If all lobbies or a mode are full, you wait in a queue. The game shows your position. `/leavequeue` leaves it.

### Maintenance

If a mode or the network is under maintenance, the game tells you. When the end is set, you also see when it reopens. Before maintenance or a restart you get a warning.

### Server switch during updates

Sometimes the server you are connected through is replaced. Then the chat says “This proxy is being replaced.” with a countdown. Afterwards you are moved to a new one by yourself. If you do not want to wait, click **Switch now** or type `/switch`. This does not work during a round; afterwards you switch by yourself.

### AFK

If you do not move for a while, you are AFK. `/afk` marks you yourself. Simply standing AFK is allowed, automated play is not. [AFK fishing](/smp/mechanics#fishing) is allowed too; after a long time you find fewer treasures.

### Waiting room

When it is busy, AFK players go to the waiting room. You also wait there when no lobby is free. `/lobby` brings you back. In the waiting room direct messages work, public chat is paused.

## Commands

- `/lobby`, `/l` or `/hub`: go to the lobby
- `/spawn`: go to the lobby spawn
- `/fly`: turn flight on or off, rank only
- `/leavequeue`: leave a queue
- `/afk`: mark yourself as AFK
- `/switch` or `/wechseln`: move to the new server at once when yours is being replaced

## Frequently asked questions

### All lobbies are full. What now?

You wait in a queue. The game shows your position. `/leavequeue` leaves it.

### Why am I in the waiting room?

You were AFK while it was busy, or no lobby was free. `/lobby` brings you back.
