When you join the network, you land in the lobby. This is where you pick a game mode, meet up with friends and collect your streak rewards. There are several lobbies, and the Nether star in your hotbar takes you between them. If they are all full, you wait in a [queue](/getting-started/lobby#queue-and-afk).

## Hotbar

You use the items in your hotbar with right-click. You can't move them around or drop them.

### Compass – Games

The compass opens the game modes. For each one you can see:

- how many people are playing it right now
- whether it is starting, restarting or under maintenance

Click a mode and you're connected. If it's full, you go into the [queue](/getting-started/lobby#queue-and-afk).

### Firework star – Player visibility

Right-click to cycle through:

- **Players visible:** you see everyone
- **Selected players only:** you see friends, party and clan
- **Players hidden:** you see nobody

### Nether star – Lobbies

This shows every lobby, with your current one marked. Click another one to move there.

### Clock – Streak

The clock opens your [streak calendar](/guides/currencies#streak).

### Chest – Inventory

The chest opens your [inventory](/guides/inventory). That's where your network, SMP and Clan XP boosters live, along with effects such as auras, trails and fireworks, and your gadgets. You buy boosters there for Argonite and activate them there too. The [price table](/guides/inventory#boosters) shows what all three kinds cost.

You can activate network and Clan boosters in the lobby, even if you don't have a clan of your own. SMP XP boosters, on the other hand, can only be activated in the SMP.

#### Gadget in the third slot

Equip a [gadget](/guides/inventory#gadgets) in `/inventory` and it shows up in your third hotbar slot, ready to use with right-click. The confetti cannon is a one-off 200 Argonite and fires confetti with a bang every 1.5 seconds.

### Your head – My account

Your head opens your [account](/guides/account).

## NPCs

Right-click an NPC:

- **SMP Season 10** and **CREATIVE** connect you directly.
- **GUIDE** opens the Guide.
- **Bugs** opens the bug report.
- **Changelog** shows the changes.
- **Wiki** opens this wiki.
- **CLAN** opens your clan.

### Double jump

Press jump again while you're in mid-air to jump a second time. If you'd rather not, turn double jump off under `/settings lobby`.

### Pick up

- Right-click a player to lift them onto your head.
- Left-click throws them.
- Sneak to get down.

Whoever is being carried can pick someone up as well, so you can build a tower of up to 5 players on top of the one at the bottom.

You decide who may pick you up under `/settings lobby`: **Everyone**, **Friends** or **Nobody**. Choose **Nobody**, though, and you can't pick anyone up yourself either.

### Fell into the void?

No worries: if you fall out of the lobby, you land back at spawn.

## Lobby settings

Under `/settings lobby` you choose:

- which players you see
- where you land when you return
- whether double jump is on
- who may pick you up

## Return

Under `/settings lobby` you choose whether you come back to the lobby at your last position or at spawn. And under `/settings network` you decide whether your next login takes you to your last mode or to the lobby.

## Flying

If you have the Prime, Elite, Legend, VIP or Media rank, you can fly in the lobby. Type `/fly` and the chat tells you “Flight is on.” or “Flight is off.” The game remembers your choice, and you'll also find it under `/settings lobby` as **Lobby flight**.

## Chaos mode

Now and then the team starts chaos mode in one lobby. "Chaos mode" appears in big letters on your screen, you're switched to creative, and everyone in that lobby may build and break whatever they like. TNT, fire, lava and redstone all work, and your lobby hotbar is gone until it ends.

The chaos lobby stays in the lobby selection, so you can join in by clicking it there. When you log in, or when the game picks a lobby for you, you never end up in it.

Once the mode ends, the game sends you to another lobby, where your usual hotbar is back. The chaos lobby then restarts as good as new. If the last player leaves before that, the mode ends on its own.

There are two limits even here: spawn eggs never work in the lobby, and no more than 32 lit TNT can burn at once. Light more and the action bar tells you to wait until some of it has gone off.

## Queue and AFK

### Queue

If every lobby is full, or a mode is, you end up in a queue and the game shows you your place in it. Had enough of waiting? `/leavequeue` takes you out.

### Maintenance

If a mode or the whole network is down for maintenance, the game tells you. When the end time is already set, you also see when it reopens. You get a warning ahead of any maintenance or restart.

### Server switch during updates

Sometimes an update moves your connection to a new server. The game announces this in chat, and you can finish a round you're in the middle of first.

Once the switch is ready, chat first offers you **Switch now**. Whether you click it is up to you. Instead of clicking, you can also use `/switch` or `/wechseln` to move earlier. Otherwise you move automatically at your next server change, or when the countdown ends at the latest.

### AFK

If you don't move for a while, you count as AFK. You can also mark yourself with `/afk`. Standing around AFK is fine, but automated play is not. [AFK fishing](/smp/mechanics#fishing) is allowed too, although after a long time you'll find fewer treasures.

### Waiting room

If you're AFK while things are busy, you're moved to the waiting room. You also wait there when no lobby is free. Direct messages still work in the waiting room, but public chat is paused. `/lobby` brings you back.

## Commands

- `/lobby`, `/l` or `/hub`: go to the lobby
- `/spawn`: go to the lobby spawn
- `/fly`: turn flight on or off, rank only
- `/leavequeue`: leave a queue
- `/afk`: mark yourself as AFK
- `/switch` or `/wechseln`: switch earlier during an update; return to the lobby while the handover is not ready

## Frequently asked questions

### All lobbies are full. What now?

Then you wait in a [queue](/getting-started/lobby#queue) and can see your place in it. `/leavequeue` takes you out again.

### Why am I in the waiting room?

Either you were AFK while things were busy, or no lobby was free at the time. `/lobby` brings you back.

### The server switch is taking a while. What happens?

If preparation takes longer, you get a message within ten seconds and can carry on playing. While the handover isn't ready yet, `/lobby`, `/l`, `/hub` and `/switch` take you to the lobby as usual. The queue still applies.

### Why am I suddenly in creative?

Your lobby is running [chaos mode](/getting-started/lobby#chaos-mode). Once it ends, you're moved to another lobby and everything is back to normal.
