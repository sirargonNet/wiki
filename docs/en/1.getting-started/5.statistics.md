The website has public statistics for SMP and Creative. Open the [statistics](https://sirargon.net/stats): the start page shows the whole network, and below it you pick a mode. At the top of every statistics page, **Search for a player or clan** finds any profile or clan.

The numbers update about every 30 seconds and trail the game by 30 to 60 seconds.

## Network

The start page shows tiles with network-wide values and the **Players online** history of the last 24 hours, ending with the count right now. Three comparison cards make the big totals tangible, for example how long one person would have to play without a break. Below them, one card leads to each mode.

## A mode

Each mode has its own tabs: **Overview** and **Leaderboards**, and in the SMP also **Advancements**, **World Quests** and **Market**. At the top right you switch between **Players** and **Clans**. The switch applies to the overview, the leaderboards and the World Quests; on the other tabs it is greyed out.

- **Overview:** tiles, the **Players online** history with the **Just now** list beside it, the **Highlights** and the top three of every leaderboard. **Show all** opens the full list.
- **Leaderboards:** the selected leaderboards are on the left. **Search statistics** finds any other published statistic, such as a block or an item. The chosen list is on the right.
- **Advancements:** on the left you narrow the list by category and by status, **All** or **Awarded**. **Search advancements** finds an advancement, and you sort awarded ones with **Newest first** or **Oldest first**. Each row names the first player and how long it took; **Show more players** lists the places up to 10. **Latest discovery** and **Most firsts** sit below.
- **World Quests:** progress and contributions of every quest, see [World Quest](/smp/quests/world-quest#website-ranking).
- **Market:** prices and histories of sold items, see [Market data](/smp/silver#market-data-on-the-website).

In the **Clans** view a clan only gets what its members achieved while they were members. A clan only appears once at least three of its current members have contributed.

## Just now

In a mode's **Overview**, **Just now** sits next to **Players online** and shows what happened over the last seven days. You see the five newest events there, the latest at the top:

- someone was first to an advancement
- someone set a new record, for example on **Survivor**
- someone is the new number 1 on one of the main leaderboards such as **Miner**, **Marathon player** or **Most SMP money**
- a World Quest was completed together

Hidden players never show up in it, not even anonymously. If nothing happened in that time, the list doesn't appear.

## Players

Click a name to open the profile. A card at the top shows what holds across all modes:

- whether the player is online right now, if they show their online status
- **Playing since**: the day of their first join
- their network level
- **Total play time**, as a bar split into SMP and Creative

Below it you pick a mode. Each tab shows tiles, the **Best places** among the top five of the leaderboards and the advancements per area. An area card shows the progress and opens into the list; a **First** badge marks every advancement the player reached first. Under **Values** there is one card per category with its three largest values, and **Show all …** opens the whole category.

## Clans

Click a clan to open its page. The card at the top shows tag, members, clan level, founding day and the play time of all members who show theirs. Each mode lists the **Clan places** and the members by play time, see [Clans](/guides/clans).

## My statistics

Sign in with `/web` and open **My statistics**, see [Website](/guides/account#website). The page looks like your profile but also shows what you hide. **Your places** lists every place you hold, and every value shows your rank. A link above leads to your public page.

## Hidden values

In `/settings privacy` you decide what others see, see [Settings](/guides/settings):

- With **Statistics** set to friends or only you, there is no public profile.
- Without **Show online status** your profile shows no online status.
- Without **Show first join**, **Playing since** is missing.
- Hidden **Play time** shows as **Hidden**.
- With **Leaderboards** off your place stays anonymous, without name or head, and your profile shows no **First** badge.

The website never shows when you were last online.
