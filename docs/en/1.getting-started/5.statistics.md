On the website you can browse public statistics from SMP and Creative. Open the [statistics](https://sirargon.net/stats) and choose **SMP** or **Creative**. There you search statistics, narrow them down by category and sort them, and the SMP page also shows who reached an advancement first.

## Explore statistics

1. On the mode's page, go to **Explore statistics**.
2. Search for a statistic or choose a category.
3. Sort by **Highest totals** or **Name**.

Each entry shows its name and icon, the **Leader**, a **Last 14 days** mini-history and the **Total**. The page controls below the list take you further, with 20 entries on each page.

Hidden players appear anonymously and without a link to their profile, and their values don't count towards the **Total**. You choose whether you're hidden yourself in the [settings](/guides/settings), under `/settings privacy`.

## The three cards

Above the search sit three cards, and what they show changes every few seconds:

- **Trending today:** what grew faster in the last 24 hours than the previous week's daily mean
- **Curious:** rare values, for example when only a few players have a value above 0, or unusual ratios between two statistics
- **Random:** a used statistic of the selected mode

The cards are there for browsing. Search, categories and sorting work independently of them.

## Just now

Next to **Players online**, a mode's page shows what happened over the last seven days under **Just now**. You see the five newest events there, the latest at the top:

- someone was first to an advancement
- someone set a new record, for example on **Survivor**
- someone is the new number 1 on one of the main leaderboards such as **Miner**, **Marathon player** or **Most SMP money**
- the players completed a World Quest together

The list arrives with the same short delay as the other statistics. Hidden players do not show up in it at all, not even anonymously. If nothing happened in the last seven days, the list simply isn't there.

## Who got there first?

On the SMP page, **Who got there first?** shows who reached an advancement first.

- The top shows how many advancements are still waiting for someone to get them first.
- **Search advancements** finds an advancement, and categories narrow the list.
- Sort advancements that already have a first by **Newest first** or **Oldest first**.
- **Latest discovery** shows the newest first, **Most firsts** the three players with the most first advancements.
- Each row names the first player and how long it took. **Show more players** reveals the next ones, up to place 10.

Hidden players appear anonymously here too.
