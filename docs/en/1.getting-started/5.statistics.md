The website has public statistics for SMP and Creative. Open the [statistics](https://sirargon.net/stats) and choose **SMP** or **Creative**.

## Explore statistics

1. On the mode's page, go to **Explore statistics**.
2. Search for a statistic or choose a category.
3. Sort by **Highest totals** or **Name**.

Each entry shows its name and icon, the **Leader**, a **Last 14 days** mini-history and the **Total**. Use the page controls below the list to browse further, 20 entries per page.

Hidden players appear anonymously and without a profile link. Their values do not count towards the **Total**. Set whether you are hidden in `/settings privacy`, see [Settings](/guides/settings).

## The three cards

Three cards sit above the search. Their content changes every few seconds:

- **Trending today:** what grew faster in the last 24 hours than the previous week's daily mean
- **Curious:** rare values, for example when only a few players have a value above 0, or unusual ratios between two statistics
- **Random:** a used statistic of the selected mode

The cards help you browse. Search, categories and sorting work independently of them.

## Who got there first?

On the SMP page, **Who got there first?** shows who reached an advancement first.

- At the top you see how many advancements still await their first.
- **Search advancements** finds an advancement. Categories narrow the list.
- Sort awarded advancements with **Newest first** or **Oldest first**.
- **Latest discovery** shows the newest first, **Most firsts** the three players with the most first advancements.
- Each row names the first player and how long it took. **Show more players** shows the next ones up to place 10.

Hidden players appear anonymously here too.
