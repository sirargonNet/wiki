sirargon.net has two game modes to play: the survival [SMP](/smp) and [Creative](/creative). Both are in beta, and minigames are still in development. You pick a mode in the [lobby](/getting-started/lobby) with the compass or at the NPCs, and `/lobby` takes you back there at any time. Before you enter a mode for the first time, you confirm its [rules](/reference/rules).

::wiki-cards{paths="/smp,/creative" images="/brand/modes/smp.png,/brand/modes/creative.png"}
::

## SMP

**SMP Season 10** is one survival world for everyone. Find your spot, build a home and trade with your neighbours. On top of that there are camps, cities, quests and skills. You'll find the rest under [SMP](/smp).

## Creative

Your own plots, every block and WorldEdit on top. Try out ideas here, or build the things you don't have the materials for in survival. You'll find the rest under [Creative](/creative).
