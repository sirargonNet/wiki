sirargon.net has two game modes: the survival [SMP](/smp) and [Creative](/creative). Both are in beta. Minigames are still in development. You choose your game mode in the [lobby](/getting-started/lobby) with the compass or at the NPCs, and `/lobby` takes you back at any time. Before you enter a mode for the first time, you confirm its [rules](/reference/rules).

::wiki-cards{paths="/smp,/creative" images="/brand/modes/smp.png,/brand/modes/creative.png"}
::

## SMP

**SMP Season 10** is one survival world for everyone: find your place, build your home and trade with your neighbours. It has camps, cities, trading, quests and skills. Everything about the SMP is under [SMP](/smp).

## Creative

Your plot, every block. Try out ideas or build what you lack the materials for in survival. It has your own plots, every block and WorldEdit. Everything about Creative is under [Creative](/creative).
