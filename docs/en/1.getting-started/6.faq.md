These are the most important questions across the whole network. Each article answers more of them in its own **Frequently asked questions** section. Can't find yours? In game, `/guide` can help, and otherwise there's the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).

## Network

### Which address do I use?

`sirargon.net`. [Connect](/getting-started/first-steps#connect) walks you through it.

### Which Minecraft version do I need?

Java Edition 26.3, and 26.2 is supported too.

### Do I need mods?

No. If you want voice chat, use the official [modpack](https://sirargon.net/modpack). You'll find the allowed mods under [Mods & clients](/reference/mods).

### Why can I not enter an area?

Accept the resource packs: in the server list, set **Server Resource Packs** to **Enabled**. Without the network's pack you can't join at all, and without the SMP packs you can't get into the SMP. More under [Resource packs](/reference/resource-packs).

### All lobbies are full. What now?

You wait in a queue, and the game shows your place in it. `/leavequeue` takes you out of it. More under [Queue and AFK](/getting-started/lobby#queue-and-afk).

### Is AFK allowed?

Standing around AFK is fine. Automated play with macros, autoclickers or bots is not.

### Where are the rules?

In game with `/rules`, and here in the wiki under [Rules](/reference/rules).

### I found a bug.

Report it with `/bug`. [Report a bug](/getting-started/help#report-a-bug) explains how.

### Someone is breaking the rules.

Report them with `/report <player>`. [Report a player](/getting-started/help#report-a-player) explains how.

### Where do I get help?

In game with `/guide` and on the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).

## Community

### How do I link Discord?

Type `/minecraft link` on the Discord and `/discord link <code>` in game. More under [Discord](/guides/account#discord).

### Where are my balance and my level?

Open `/account`. More under [Currencies & streak](/guides/currencies).

### How do I play with friends?

Manage your [friends](/guides/friends) with `/friends` and your [party](/guides/friends#party) with `/party`.

### How do I change the language?

Change it under `/settings language`. More under [Language](/guides/settings#language).

## Clans

### How do I found a clan?

With `/clan found <tag> <name>`. You need a certain network level. More under [Clans](/guides/clans#found-a-clan).

### How do I find a clan?

`/clans` opens the directory, and `/clans search <text>` searches it. More under [Find clans](/guides/clans#find-clans).

## SMP

### Why can I not join the SMP?

You need a [Discord link](/guides/account#discord) and have to be a member of the Discord. You also need to accept the resource pack and confirm the rules. More under [SMP](/smp#join).

### How do I protect my builds?

With a [camp](/smp/camps) or in a [city](/smp/cities). Nothing is protected in the wilderness.

### I died. Where are my items?

In your gravestone. `/graves` shows you where it is. More under [PvP and death](/smp/death#gravestone).

### How do I get silver?

By playing actively, completing quests, reaching milestones and trading. More under [Silver & trading](/smp/silver), [Quests](/smp/quests) and [Milestones](/smp/milestones).

## Creative

### How do I get a plot?

With `/plot auto`. More under [Plots](/creative/plots).

### May I use WorldEdit?

Yes, on your own plot. More under [WorldEdit](/creative/worldedit).
