These are the most important questions for the whole network. Every article answers more questions in its **Frequently asked questions** section. Your question is not here? In game, `/guide` helps, otherwise the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).

## Network

### Which address do I use?

`sirargon.net`. The steps are under [Connect](/getting-started/first-steps#connect).

### Which Minecraft version do I need?

Java Edition 26.3. 26.2 is supported as well.

### Do I need mods?

No. For voice chat, use the official [modpack](https://sirargon.net/modpack). Allowed mods are listed under [Mods & clients](/reference/mods).

### Why can I not enter an area?

Accept the resource packs. Set **Server Resource Packs** to **Enabled** in the server list. Without the network's pack you cannot join, without the SMP packs you cannot enter the SMP. More under [Resource packs](/reference/resource-packs).

### All lobbies are full. What now?

You wait in a queue. The game shows your position. `/leavequeue` leaves it. More under [Queue and AFK](/getting-started/lobby#queue-and-afk).

### Is AFK allowed?

Simply standing AFK, yes. Automated play with macros, autoclickers or bots, no.

### Where are the rules?

In game with `/rules` and under [Rules](/reference/rules).

### I found a bug.

Report it with `/bug`. More under [Report a bug](/getting-started/help#report-a-bug).

### Someone is breaking the rules.

Report them with `/report <player>`. More under [Report a player](/getting-started/help#report-a-player).

### Where do I get help?

In game with `/guide` and on the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).

## Community

### How do I link Discord?

Type `/minecraft link` on the Discord and `/discord link <code>` in game. More under [Discord](/guides/account#discord).

### Where are my balance and my level?

Open `/account`. More under [Currencies & streak](/guides/currencies).

### How do I play with friends?

`/friends` manages your [friends](/guides/friends), `/party` your [party](/guides/friends#party).

### How do I change the language?

Under `/settings language`. More under [Language](/guides/settings#language).

## Clans

### How do I found a clan?

With `/clan found <tag> <name>`. You need a certain network level. More under [Clans](/guides/clans#found-a-clan).

### How do I find a clan?

`/clans` opens the directory. `/clans search <text>` searches. More under [Find clans](/guides/clans#find-clans).

## SMP

### Why can I not join the SMP?

You need a [Discord link](/guides/account#discord) and Discord membership. Also accept the resource pack and confirm the rules. More under [SMP](/smp#join).

### How do I protect my builds?

With a [camp](/smp/camps) or in a [city](/smp/cities). The wilderness has no protection.

### I died. Where are my items?

In your gravestone. `/graves` shows where it is. More under [PvP and death](/smp/death#gravestone).

### How do I get silver?

Play actively, complete quests, reach milestones and trade. More under [Silver & trading](/smp/silver), [Quests](/smp/quests) and [Milestones](/smp/milestones).

## Creative

### How do I get a plot?

With `/plot auto`. More under [Plots](/creative/plots).

### May I use WorldEdit?

Yes, on your own plot. More under [WorldEdit](/creative/worldedit).
