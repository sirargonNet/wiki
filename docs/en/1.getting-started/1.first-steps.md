How to join sirargon.net and find your way around in your first minutes. You need Minecraft: Java Edition 26.3 or 26.2 and connect to `sirargon.net`. On your first join you confirm the privacy notice, the terms of use and the rules. Then you land in the lobby and choose SMP or Creative there.

## Connect

1. Start Minecraft: Java Edition **26.3**. **26.2** is supported as well.
2. Open **Multiplayer** and add a server.
3. Enter `sirargon.net` as the address.
4. Set **Server Resource Packs** to **Enabled**.
5. Connect.

### Resource packs

Some areas use their own resource packs. Accept them, or you cannot enter those areas. While a pack loads, you are protected.

### With or without mods

You can play without mods. For voice chat and extra comfort, use the official modpack [Sirargons Mod-Mische](https://sirargon.net/modpack). Simple Voice Chat is already included.

The allowed mods are listed under [Mods & clients](/reference/mods).

### Checklist

- [ ] Java Edition 26.3 or 26.2 started
- [ ] `sirargon.net` added
- [ ] Server resource packs enabled
- [ ] Optional: official modpack set up

## When you first join

When you first join, you confirm:

- the **privacy notice**
- the **terms of use**
- the network **rules**

If you decline, you leave the network. Before you enter a game mode for the first time, you also confirm its rules. When the rules change, you confirm them again in the lobby. `/confirm` reopens a pending confirmation.

## In the lobby

After joining, you land in the lobby. Your hotbar:

- **Compass – Games:** choose a game mode
- **Firework star – Player visibility:** see all, selected or no players
- **Nether star – Lobbies:** switch lobby
- **Clock – Streak:** your daily streak
- **Chest – Inventory:** boosters, auras, trails and gadgets, also for sale for Argonite
- **Your head – My account:** profile, friends, settings

More about the [lobby](/getting-started/lobby).

## Start playing

Open the compass and choose [SMP](/smp) or [Creative](/creative). You can also click the NPCs in the lobby. `/lobby` takes you back at any time.

The SMP needs a [Discord link](/guides/account#discord).

## Commands

- `/confirm`: reopen a pending confirmation
- `/lobby`: go back to the lobby
- `/guide`: open the in-game Guide
- `/rules`: show the rules
- `/wiki`: open this wiki
- `/changelog`: show what has changed
- `/settings`: open your [settings](/guides/settings)

## Frequently asked questions

### Which address do I use?

`sirargon.net`. The steps are under [Connect](/getting-started/first-steps#connect).

### Which Minecraft version do I need?

Java Edition 26.3. 26.2 is supported as well.

### Do I need mods?

No. For voice chat, use the official [modpack](https://sirargon.net/modpack). Allowed mods are listed under [Mods & clients](/reference/mods#allowed-mods).
