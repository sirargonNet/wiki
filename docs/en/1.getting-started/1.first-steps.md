Here's how to get onto sirargon.net and find your feet in your first few minutes, from connecting to picking your first game mode.

## Connect

1. Start Minecraft: Java Edition **26.3**. **26.2** works too.
2. Open **Multiplayer** and add a server.
3. Enter `sirargon.net` as the address.
4. Set **Server Resource Packs** to **Enabled**.
5. Connect.

### Resource packs

The network has its own resource pack, and you have to accept it to join. The SMP loads more packs once you enter it, and if you decline those, you're sent back to the lobby. Until they've loaded, you can't move in the SMP, but nothing can hurt you either. More under [Resource packs](/reference/resource-packs).

### With or without mods

You don't need any mods. If you want voice chat and a bit more comfort, grab the official modpack [Sirargons Mod-Mische](https://sirargon.net/modpack), which already comes with Simple Voice Chat.

You can see which mods are allowed under [Mods & clients](/reference/mods).

### Checklist

- [ ] Java Edition 26.3 or 26.2 started
- [ ] `sirargon.net` added
- [ ] Server resource packs enabled
- [ ] Optional: official modpack set up

## When you first join

When you first join, you confirm three things:

- the **privacy notice**
- the **terms of use**
- the network **rules**

If you decline, you leave the network. Each game mode has its own rules too, and you confirm them before you enter it for the first time. When the rules change, you confirm them again in the lobby. If a confirmation is still pending, `/confirm` brings it back up.

## In the lobby

After joining, you land in the lobby. Here's what's in your hotbar:

- **Compass – Games:** choose a game mode
- **Firework star – Player visibility:** see all, selected or no players
- **Nether star – Lobbies:** switch lobby
- **Clock – Streak:** your daily streak
- **Chest – Inventory:** boosters, effects and gadgets, many of which you can also buy with Argonite
- **Your head – My account:** profile, friends, settings

Everything else you can do there is under [Lobby](/getting-started/lobby).

## Start playing

Open the compass and pick [SMP](/smp) or [Creative](/creative), or click one of the NPCs in the lobby. `/lobby` brings you back whenever you like.

To play on the SMP, you need to link your account to Discord. The [Discord link](/guides/account#discord) section shows you how.

## Commands

- `/confirm`: reopen a pending confirmation
- `/lobby`: go back to the lobby
- `/guide`: open the in-game Guide
- `/rules`: show the rules
- `/wiki`: open this wiki
- `/changelog`: show what has changed
- `/settings`: open your [settings](/guides/settings)

## Frequently asked questions

### Which address do I use?

`sirargon.net`. The steps are under [Connect](/getting-started/first-steps#connect).

### Which Minecraft version do I need?

Java Edition 26.3, though 26.2 works too.

### Do I need mods?

No. If you want voice chat, use the official [modpack](https://sirargon.net/modpack). Allowed mods are listed under [Mods & clients](/reference/mods#allowed-mods).
