`/replays` opens **My recordings**. Start a replay from the lobby with **Watch in game**. While watching, `/replay` opens the controls. When it ends, you are back in the lobby.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/replays`: your recordings
- `/replays cancel`: cancel the start
- `/replay`: controls while watching
- `/replay pause` and `/replay resume`: pause and continue
- `/replay speed <value>`: speed
- `/replay free`: free camera
- `/replay follow`: a player's view
- `/replay end`: end
