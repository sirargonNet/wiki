You cannot move or drop the hotbar items. Right-click uses them.

## Compass – Games

Opens the game modes. For each mode you see:

- how many are playing
- whether it is starting, restarting or under maintenance

Click a mode to connect. If it is full, you wait in a [queue](/getting-started/lobby/queue).

## Firework star – Player visibility

Right-click switches between:

- **Players visible:** you see everyone
- **Selected players only:** you see friends, party and clan
- **Players hidden:** you see nobody

## Nether star – Lobbies

Shows all lobbies. Your current lobby is marked. Click another one to switch.

## Clock – Streak

Opens your [streak calendar](/guides/account#streak).

## Chest – Inventory

Opens your boosters. Activate a booster whenever you like. It works for everyone. If one of the same kind is already running, yours joins the queue.

You can also buy boosters for Argonite:

- **2× Network XP** or **2× SMP XP**
- **30 min** for 300 Argonite or **60 min** for 550 Argonite

Before anything is charged, you see the price and your balance once more. SMP XP boosters can only be activated in the SMP.

## Your head – My account

Opens your [account](/guides/account).
