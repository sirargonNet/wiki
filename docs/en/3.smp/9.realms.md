The SMP has three realms: the **Overworld**, the **Nether** and the **End**. Each realm has its own quests, its own milestones and its own skill tree. Here you see where to find the content of each realm.

## Discovering a realm

You only see a realm's content once you have entered it. Before that, `/quests`, `/milestones` and the skill tree do not show the realm. When you first enter it, the chat tells you what is now unlocked.

## Overworld

[Camps](/smp/camps) and [cities](/smp/cities) exist only in the Overworld.

- [Quests](/smp/quests#overworld)
- [Milestones](/smp/milestones#overworld)
- [Skill tree](/smp/skill-tree#overworld)
- [Items](/smp/items#overworld)
- [Enchantments](/smp/enchantments#overworld)
- [Advancements](/smp/advancements#overworld)
- [Spawners](/smp/spawners#prices) for the Overworld
- [Outposts](/smp/cities/outposts) of your city

## Nether

The Nether is open. Build a Nether portal and step through.

Once you have been to the Nether for the first time, you get:

- your own [Nether quests](/smp/quests#nether), on top of your Overworld quests
- a separate group of [Nether milestones](/smp/milestones#nether)
- the [Nether skill tree](/smp/skill-tree#nether): in the skill tree, **To the Nether skill tree** switches over and **To the Overworld** switches back

The Nether also has:

- [Items](/smp/items#nether)
- [Enchantments](/smp/enchantments#nether)
- [Advancements](/smp/advancements#nether)
- [Spawners](/smp/spawners#prices) for the Nether
- [Outposts](/smp/cities/outposts#nether): there they are the only kind of territory.

## End

The server opens access to the End at its announced stage.

### The first dragon

Its strength depends on the players online in the SMP when someone first enters the End. That roster stays fixed for the fight. Later logins and logouts do not change its strength.

PvP is off in the End during the fight. It stays off while the defeated dragon fades away. Only when the [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg) then appears on the exit portal does PvP turn on.

Player numbers do not change the strength of summoned dragons after the first victory. PvP in the End stays on during their fights.

Victory grants [SMP XP](/smp/skills#dragon-victories). While a dragon is alive, special [grave rules](/smp/death#end) apply near the centre.
