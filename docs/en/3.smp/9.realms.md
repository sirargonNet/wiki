The SMP has three realms: the **Overworld**, the **Nether** and the **End**. Each realm has its own quests, its own milestones and its own skill tree. Here you see where to find the content of each realm.

## Discovering a realm

You unlock a realm's content the first time you actively play there, for example by mining a block or fighting. Just entering or walking around is not enough. Before that, `/quests`, `/milestones` and the skill tree do not show the realm. Once it is unlocked, the chat tells you once what is new.

## Overworld

[Camps](/smp/camps) and [cities](/smp/cities) exist only in the Overworld.

## Nether

The Nether is open. Build a Nether portal and step through.

Once you have been to the Nether for the first time, you get:

- your own [Nether quests](/smp/quests#nether), on top of your Overworld quests
- a separate group of [Nether milestones](/smp/milestones#nether)
- the [Nether skill tree](/smp/skill-tree#nether): in the skill tree, **To the Nether skill tree** switches over and **To the Overworld** switches back

The Nether also has:

- [Items](/smp/items#nether)
- [Enchantments](/smp/enchantments#nether)
- [Advancements](/smp/advancements#nether)
- [Spawners](/smp/spawners#prices) for the Nether
- [Outposts](/smp/cities/outposts#nether): there they are the only kind of territory.

## End

The server opens access to the End at its announced stage.

### Elytra stage

You can glide with elytra everywhere, as in vanilla. Rocket boosts in flight only work in the End until the Elytra stage. In the Overworld and the Nether the game cancels the boost and shows in the action bar: "Elytra rocket boosts are only available in the End until the release." The rocket is not used up.

Normal fireworks and rockets shot from a crossbow are not affected. Before the release a countdown runs, then the game announces **Elytra boosts unlocked**. From then on, rocket boosts work everywhere.

### The dragon fight

PvP is off in the End during the fight. It stays off until the [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg) appears on the exit portal after the victory. Only then does PvP turn on.

PvP in the End stays on during fights against dragons summoned later.

Victory grants [SMP XP](/smp/skills#dragon-victories). During the fight, special [grave rules](/smp/death#end) apply near the centre.
