The SMP has three realms: the **Overworld**, the **Nether** and the **End**. Each one has its own quests, its own milestones and its own skill tree.

## Discovering a realm

You unlock a realm's content the first time you actively play there, for example by mining a block or fighting. Entering the realm or walking around doesn't count. Until then, the realm doesn't show up in `/quests`, `/milestones` or the skill tree. Once it's unlocked, the chat tells you once what's new.

## Nether

The Nether is open. Build a Nether portal and step through.

Once you have discovered the Nether, you get:

- your own [Nether quests](/smp/quests#nether), on top of your Overworld quests
- a separate group of [Nether milestones](/smp/milestones#nether)
- the [Nether skill tree](/smp/skill-tree#nether): in the skill tree, **To the Nether skill tree** switches over and **To the Overworld** switches back

The Nether also has:

- [Items](/smp/items#nether)
- [Enchantments](/smp/enchantments#nether)
- [Advancements](/smp/advancements#nether)
- [Spawners](/smp/spawners#prices) for the Nether
- [Outposts](/smp/cities/outposts#nether): there they are the only kind of territory.

## End

The server opens access to the End at its announced stage.

### Elytra stage

You can glide with elytra everywhere, as in vanilla. Until the Elytra stage, though, rocket boosts in flight only work in the End. In the Overworld and the Nether the game cancels the boost and shows this in the action bar: "Elytra rocket boosts are only available in the End until the release." The rocket is not used up.

Normal fireworks and rockets shot from a crossbow are not affected. A countdown runs before the release. Then the game announces **Elytra boosts unlocked**, and from then on, rocket boosts work everywhere.

### The dragon fight

::callout{type="warning" title="Careful at the End event"}
If you don't want to die, stay away from the End event. Once PvP is on there, it's often unclear who is going for the egg and who isn't, so players who aren't fighting for the egg can get hit too.

Your clan or city can also make you a target: if your allies fight for the egg, you can be attacked as their ally. To stay safe, keep away from the centre of the End after the victory, that is from the area around the exit portal where the egg appears.
::

The End event's dragon is stronger and fights differently than in vanilla.

For the first dragon, a border limits the End to **300 × 300 blocks around 0/0**. You can't leave it, and if you stand outside, you take damage as at any vanilla world border. The border disappears with the victory. Dragons summoned again later have no border.

For the first dragon, PvP in the End is off until the [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg) appears on the exit portal. From then on, PvP in the End is on. For all dragons summoned again later, it is on the whole time.

The dragon egg also counts as an [RP reason](/smp/death#pvp) for PvP. What that means for whoever carries or chases it is explained under [Fighting for the egg](/smp/dragon-egg#fighting-for-the-egg).

Victory grants [SMP XP](/smp/skills#dragon-victories). While a dragon lives and the Elytra stage is still closed, special [grave rules](/smp/death#end) apply near the centre.
