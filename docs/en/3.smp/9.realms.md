The SMP has three realms: the **Overworld**, the **Nether** and the **End**. Each realm has its own quests, its own milestones and its own skill tree.

## Nether

The Nether is open. Build a Nether portal and step through.

## Discovering a realm

You only see a realm's content once you have entered it. Before that, `/quests`, `/milestones` and the skill tree do not show the realm. When you first enter it, the chat tells you what is now unlocked.

Once you have been to the Nether for the first time, you get:

- your own [Nether quests](/smp/quests/nether), on top of your Overworld quests
- a separate group of [Nether milestones](/smp/milestones/nether)
- the [Nether skill tree](/smp/skills/nether): in the skill tree, **To the Nether skill tree** switches over and **To the Overworld** switches back

## End

The End follows later in the same way. We will announce in good time when it opens.
