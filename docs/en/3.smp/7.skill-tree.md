In the skill tree you spend your skill points on abilities. Every SMP level gives one point, including level 1. All realms share this pool. How stars, skill points and mana work is under [Skills & levels](/smp/skills#skill-tree).

The values run from tier I to the highest tier. "Points" names the points in the category that the higher tiers need.

Hover over an unpurchased star to see its tier’s skill point cost. You can also see it while the star is locked or you lack free points. The required category points remain visible alongside it.

## Overworld

### Mining

| Ability      | Values per tier                    | Points |
| :----------- | :--------------------------------- | :----- |
| Vein Miner   | 4, 8, 16, 24, 32 ore blocks        | 6, 10  |
| Tunnel Borer | tier I to V, more stone types      | 6, 10  |
| Mining XP    | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP | 6, 10  |
| Mining Mana  | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana       | 6, 10  |

- Vein Miner: sneak with a pickaxe, natural ores within 32 blocks, 3 mana per extra block. No ancient debris.
- Tunnel Borer: sneak and press F with a pickaxe, up to 9 natural stone blocks in a 3 × 3 plane, aim up to 5 blocks away, 2 mana per block. Your tool loses one point of durability per block. Unbreaking lowers that as usual.

| Tier | New stone types                |
| :--- | :----------------------------- |
| I    | stone, deepslate               |
| II   | granite, diorite, andesite     |
| III  | tuff, calcite, dripstone block |
| IV   | netherrack, basalt, blackstone |
| V    | end stone                      |

Every tier keeps the stone types of the tiers before it.

### Forestry

| Ability       | Values per tier                    | Points |
| :------------ | :--------------------------------- | :----- |
| Tree Feller   | 8, 16, 40, 80, 140 logs            | 6, 10  |
| Replanting    | 1 sapling, all 4 for 2 × 2 trees   | –      |
| Leaf Fall     | one tier                           | –      |
| Forestry XP   | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP | 6, 10  |
| Forestry Mana | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana       | 6, 10  |

- Tree Feller: sneak with an axe, natural logs within 32 blocks, 2 mana per extra log. You pay the mana before each log. Sneaking only triggers it; after that you no longer need to sneak. When a felling ends is under [Skills & levels](/smp/skills#tree-feller). Larger trees remain partly standing.
- Replanting: after you fell a natural tree, plants a sapling from your inventory on its lowest log. With Tree Feller it does not matter which log you click. If you break a single log without Tree Feller, it only plants when that was the lowest one. The sapling takes every ground Minecraft allows for it, such as dirt, grass, podzol, coarse dirt and moss. For mangroves it is a mangrove propagule, for crimson and warped stems the matching fungus. Tier II plants all four saplings on the four lowest logs of 2 × 2 spruce, jungle, dark oak and pale oak trees. You need four saplings in your inventory for that.
- Leaf Fall: the natural leaves around the felled logs decay at once, like normal leaf decay. Saplings, sticks and apples drop as usual. Your tool does not wear down. Placed leaves remain.

### Farming

| Ability        | Values per tier                           | Points |
| :------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Harvest Scythe | 3 × 3, 5 × 5, 7 × 7, 7 × 7, 9 × 9         | 6, 10  |
| Reseeding      | one tier                                  | –      |
| Farming XP     | 20, 40, 60, 80, 100% more SMP XP          | 6, 10  |
| Breeder        | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% chance of twins        | 6, 10  |
| Rich Harvest   | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% chance of double crops | 6, 10  |

- Harvest Scythe: sneak with a hoe, 0.5 mana per ripe crop. From tier II also nether wart, from tier IV also pumpkins, melons, sugar cane and cocoa.
- Breeder: A successful breeding can produce a second baby animal. This costs no mana.
- Rich Harvest: May double ripe crop drops harvested by hand or with Harvest Scythe. Automated harvests do not count.
- Reseeding: replants using a seed from your inventory. This works for ripe wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots and nether wart, also without Harvest Scythe.

### Building, exploration and combat

| Ability      | Values per tier                     | Points |
| :----------- | :---------------------------------- | :----- |
| Long Arms    | +1, +2 blocks of reach              | –      |
| Sprint Boost | 10, 15 seconds of Speed II          | –      |
| Soft Fall    | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less fall damage | 4, 6   |
| Deep Diver   | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% more air        | 4, 6   |
| Mob Expert   | 10, 20% more damage against mobs    | –      |
| Second Wind  | Regeneration I, II                  | –      |
| Combat XP    | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP  | 3, 5   |

- Long Arms: only works while you stand in your own territory. With the extra reach you only place and break blocks in your own territory. You cannot use chests, doors or other blocks with it. Buckets and TNT do not work with it either.
- Sprint Boost: sneak twice while sprinting, both within one second. Costs 20 mana.
- Mob Expert: does not apply against players.
- Second Wind: when damage takes you below three hearts, you get five seconds of Regeneration. Then the ability waits ten minutes. Never during player combat.

### Community, spirit and crafting

| Ability         | Values per tier                                        | Points |
| :-------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Team Aura       | 10, 20, 30% more SMP XP                                | –      |
| Campfire Rest   | one tier                                               | –      |
| Mana Storage    | 75, 100, 125, 150, 175, 200, 225, 250 total mana       | 5, 8   |

- Team Aura: works with at least two active players within 16 blocks.
- Campfire Rest: if you stay for ten seconds within 5 blocks of a lit campfire you placed yourself, you get Regeneration I. It is renewed every second while you stay. If you take or deal damage, the rest pauses for ten seconds.
- Mana Storage and Mana Flow: without stars you have 50 mana and refill 0.5 mana per second.

### Fishing

| Ability      | Values per tier                        | Points |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Fishing XP   | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP     | 6, 10  |
| Fishing Mana | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana           | 6, 10  |
| Fast Bite    | 10, 20, 30% shorter wait               | 6      |
| Double Catch | 4, 8, 12, 16, 20% chance of a 2nd fish | 6, 10  |
| Cook Catch   | one tier                               | –      |

- Fishing XP: more SMP XP per catch. Fish and treasure give XP, junk does not. Minecraft XP stays separate.
- Fast Bite: shorter wait until a bite. Costs 2 mana when you cast. Without enough mana it has no effect.
- Double Catch: chance of a second fish of the same kind, never treasure or junk. Costs 3 mana only on a hit. The second fish gives no extra XP.
- Cook Catch: cod and salmon arrive cooked. Costs 1 mana per cooked fish. Without mana the fish stays raw.
- Fishing Mana lowers the costs of Fast Bite, Double Catch and Cook Catch.
- No fishing ability needs sneaking. Turn off Fast Bite, Double Catch and Cook Catch with `/skills toggle`.

### Mastery

Once every skill of an area is at its highest tier, you unlock three **Diamond tiers** there. They go in order:

| Tier      | Cost           |
| :-------- | :------------- |
| Diamond 1 | 3 skill points |
| Diamond 2 | 4 skill points |
| Diamond 3 | 5 skill points |

A full mastery costs 12 skill points. In the skill tree the diamonds sit on the outside of their area. In the constellation view they form a triangle beyond the area; in the column view they have their own row beneath it.

- Diamond tiers do not count towards the points that higher tiers of other skills need.
- A skill reset also removes mastery. You get its points back.
- Masteries cannot be turned off. Like all skills, they only work in Survival mode and not while you fight players.
- For Overworld masteries, enchantment bonuses only work when the enchantment is at its highest vanilla level. Your item stays unchanged. With Silk Touch there is no Fortune bonus.

Building, community and crafting have no mastery. The [Nether masteries](/smp/skill-tree#nether-masteries) are listed below.

## Nether

You get the Nether skill tree once you have entered the Nether. Before that, the skill tree does not offer it. Afterwards you switch at the top with **To the Nether skill tree** and **To the Overworld**. It uses the same skill points as the Overworld. All Nether abilities only work in the Nether.

| Category  | Ability         | Values per tier                 | Points |
| :-------- | :-------------- | :------------------------------ | :----- |
| Gathering | Nether Mining   | 20, 40, 60, 80, 100% faster     | 3, 6   |
| Gathering | Nether Forestry | 8, 16, 32, 48, 64 blocks        | 3, 6   |
| Fighting  | Nether Combat   | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% more damage | 3, 6   |
| Fighting  | Fire Ward       | 4, 8, 12, 16, 20% less damage   | 3, 6   |
| Roaming   | Nether Wanderer | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% faster       | 3, 6   |
| Roaming   | Bartering       | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% chance      | 3, 6   |
| Roaming   | Piglin Friend   | one tier                        | –      |

All Nether abilities only work in Survival mode and never while you fight other players.

### Nether Mining

You mine faster while you dig nether quartz ore, nether gold ore, ancient debris or gilded blackstone. When you let go, the bonus is gone.

Natural nether quartz ore has a 5, 10, 15, 20 or 25% chance to give twice as much quartz. The chance rises with your tier.

### Nether Forestry

- When you fell a natural crimson or warped stem with an axe while sneaking, the wart blocks and shroomlights of its fungus fall too.
- It searches 5 blocks in every direction and up to 16 blocks above the felled stem. The nearest blocks fall first, up to the value of your tier.
- It costs no mana. You do not need Tree Feller for it.
- Placed wart blocks and shroomlights remain.

### Nether Combat

- More damage against blazes, wither skeletons, ghasts, magma cubes, hoglins, zoglins and piglin brutes.
- Only your own melee hits and your own projectiles count, for example arrows. Pets and TNT do not count.
- It does not work against bosses of public events.

### Fire Ward

Less damage when you stand in fire, burn, are in lava, walk on magma blocks or touch a campfire.

### Nether Wanderer

You walk faster on every floor in the Nether. This stacks with the Soul Speed enchantment. You get one shared optional free reset for the whole skill revision. There is no separate transfer or refund for Soul Speed.

### Bartering

With the chance of your tier, a piglin gives you twice as much for your gold. This is how it works:

- You give an adult piglin a gold ingot. You throw it or hand it over with a right-click.
- The piglin trades within 15 seconds after that.
- When it trades, you are at most 16 blocks away from it.
- Gold in the trade is never doubled: you get gold ingots, gold blocks, gold nuggets and raw gold only once.
- Your gold ingot does not come back.

### Piglin Friend

Piglins stay neutral even without gold armour. Attacking them or opening their containers still provokes them. Piglin brutes remain dangerous.

### Nether masteries

Max out every ordinary skill in a Nether area first. Then buy its Diamond tiers in order for 3, 4 and 5 skill points. Each tier keeps the earlier bonuses. They only work in the Nether, in Survival mode and never during player combat.

| Mastery                  | Diamond 1                                                                                                                                | Diamond 2                                                                                        | Diamond 3                                                                   |
| :----------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nether Gathering Mastery | **Quartz Luck:** Nether quartz and Nether gold drop as with at least Fortune IV, even without Fortune on your tool. Not with Silk Touch. | **Wart Garden:** Sneaking has a 10% chance to grow Nether wart and Nether fungi within 3 blocks. | **Debris Find:** Natural ancient debris has a 10% chance to drop one extra. |
| Nether Combat Mastery    | **Witherproof:** Wither lasts half as long.                                                                                              | **Blaze Hunter:** Blazes have a 25% chance to drop an extra blaze rod.                           | **Fire Walker:** No afterburn after fire or lava.                           |
| Nether Roaming Mastery   | **Strider Rider:** Ridden striders move 50% faster.                                                                                      | **Lava Breath:** Fire Resistance lasts 50% longer in the Nether.                                 | **No Junk:** Piglins never barter gravel, blackstone or Nether bricks.      |

Territory rights also apply to Quartz Luck, Debris Find and Wart Garden. Placed ores give no extra drops. Your tool stays unchanged.

The End gets its own skill tree later in the same way.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

Instead of `/skills` you can use `/skill`, instead of `/skilltree` also `/skillstree`.

- `/skills tree` or `/skilltree`: skill tree
- `/skills toggle <ability>`: turn an ability on or off
- `/skills reset`: reset stars
