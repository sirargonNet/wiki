The skill tree is where you spend your skill points on abilities. Every SMP level gives you one point, including level 1, and all realms share the same pool. How stars, skill points and mana work is explained under [Skills & levels](/smp/skills#skill-tree).

The tables list the values from tier I up to the highest tier. The "Points" column shows how many points in the category you need for the higher tiers.

Hover over a star you haven't bought yet to see how many skill points its tier costs. This also works while the star is locked or you lack free points, and the required category points stay visible next to it.

## Overworld

### Mining

| Ability      | Values per tier                    | Points |
| :----------- | :--------------------------------- | :----- |
| Vein Miner   | 4, 8, 16, 24, 32 ore blocks        | 6, 10  |
| Tunnel Borer | tier I to V, more stone types      | 6, 10  |
| Mining XP    | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP | 6, 10  |
| Mining Mana  | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana       | 6, 10  |

- Vein Miner: sneak with a pickaxe to mine natural ores within a 32-block reach. Each extra block costs 3 mana. Ancient debris isn't included.
- Tunnel Borer: sneak with a pickaxe and press F to break up to 9 natural stone blocks in a 3 × 3 plane, aiming up to 5 blocks away. It costs 2 mana per block, and your tool loses one point of durability per block. Unbreaking lowers that as usual.

| Tier | New stone types                |
| :--- | :----------------------------- |
| I    | stone, deepslate               |
| II   | granite, diorite, andesite     |
| III  | tuff, calcite, dripstone block |
| IV   | netherrack, basalt, blackstone |
| V    | end stone                      |

Every tier keeps the stone types of the tiers before it.

### Forestry

| Ability       | Values per tier                    | Points |
| :------------ | :--------------------------------- | :----- |
| Tree Feller   | 8, 16, 40, 80, 140 logs            | 6, 10  |
| Replanting    | 1 sapling, all 4 for 2 × 2 trees   | –      |
| Leaf Fall     | one tier                           | –      |
| Forestry XP   | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP | 6, 10  |
| Forestry Mana | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana       | 6, 10  |

- Tree Feller: sneak with an axe to fell natural logs within a 32-block reach. Each extra log costs 2 mana, paid before each log. You only need to sneak to trigger it, not afterwards. When a felling ends is under [Skills & levels](/smp/skills#tree-feller). Larger trees remain partly standing.
- Replanting: when you fell a natural tree, it plants a sapling from your inventory on the lowest log. With Tree Feller it does not matter which log you click. If you break a single log without Tree Feller, it only plants when that was the lowest one. The sapling takes every ground Minecraft allows for it, such as dirt, grass, podzol, coarse dirt and moss. For mangroves it is a mangrove propagule, for crimson and warped stems the matching fungus. Tier II plants all four saplings on the four lowest logs of 2 × 2 spruce, jungle, dark oak and pale oak trees. You need four saplings in your inventory for that.
- Leaf Fall: the natural leaves around the felled logs decay at once, the same way as normal leaf decay. Saplings, sticks and apples drop as usual, and your tool doesn't wear down. Placed leaves remain.

### Farming

| Ability        | Values per tier                           | Points |
| :------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Harvest Scythe | 3 × 3, 5 × 5, 7 × 7, 7 × 7, 9 × 9         | 6, 10  |
| Reseeding      | one tier                                  | –      |
| Farming XP     | 20, 40, 60, 80, 100% more SMP XP          | 6, 10  |
| Breeder        | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% chance of twins        | 6, 10  |
| Rich Harvest   | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% chance of double crops | 6, 10  |

- Harvest Scythe: sneak with a hoe. Each ripe crop costs 0.5 mana. From tier II it also takes nether wart, from tier IV also pumpkins, melons, sugar cane and cocoa.
- Breeder: a successful breeding can produce a second baby animal. This costs no mana.
- Rich Harvest: with your tier's chance, ripe crops you harvest by hand or with Harvest Scythe drop twice as much. Automated harvests don't count.
- Reseeding: when you harvest ripe wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots or nether wart, it replants using a seed from your inventory, even without Harvest Scythe.

### Excavation

| Ability       | Values per tier                                                       | Points |
| :------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Excavator     | tier I to V, more soil types                                          | 6, 10  |
| Sieve         | 2, 4, 6% chance of a nugget                                           | 6      |
| Excavation XP | 22, 44, 66, 88, 110% more SMP XP                                      | 6, 10  |
| Quick Shovel  | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% faster digging                                    | 6, 10  |
| Archaeologist | 20, 40, 60, 80, 100% faster brushing; 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% double finds | 6, 10  |

- Excavator: sneak with a shovel to dig 3 × 3 natural soil for 1 mana per block. From tier III it goes two blocks deep.

| Tier | New soil types                 |
| :--- | :----------------------------- |
| I    | dirt, grass block, coarse dirt |
| II   | sand, red sand, gravel         |
| III  | clay, mud, rooted dirt         |
| IV   | soul sand, soul soil           |
| V    | snow block, mycelium, podzol   |

Every tier keeps the soil types of the tiers before it.

- Sieve: natural gravel, sand and red sand dug with a shovel can give an extra iron or gold nugget, on top of the ordinary drops. This doesn't work with Silk Touch.
- Quick Shovel: only speeds up natural soil while you dig with a shovel, and costs no mana. Excavator still costs 1 mana per block.
- Archaeologist: you brush suspicious sand or gravel faster and sometimes find the same item twice. Ordinary protection, brush wear and loot rules still apply.

### Building, exploration and combat

| Ability      | Values per tier                     | Points |
| :----------- | :---------------------------------- | :----- |
| Long Arms    | +1, +2 blocks of reach              | –      |
| Sprint Boost | 10, 15 seconds of Speed II          | –      |
| Soft Fall    | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less fall damage | 4, 6   |
| Deep Diver   | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% more air        | 4, 6   |
| Mob Expert   | 10, 20% more damage against mobs    | –      |
| Second Wind  | Regeneration I, II                  | –      |
| Combat XP    | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP  | 3, 5   |

- Long Arms: only works while you stand in your own territory. With the extra reach you only place and break blocks in your own territory. You can't use chests, doors or other blocks with it, and buckets and TNT don't work with it either.
- Sprint Boost: sneak twice while sprinting, both within one second. It costs 20 mana.
- Mob Expert: doesn't apply against players.
- Second Wind: when damage takes you below three hearts, you get five seconds of Regeneration. Then the ability waits ten minutes. It never triggers during player combat.

### Playing together, spirit and crafting

| Ability         | Values per tier                                        | Points |
| :-------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Team Aura       | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% more SMP XP                        | –      |
| Back Cover      | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mob damage for teammates       | –      |
| Shared Loot     | 25, 50, 100% additional combat XP for teammates        | –      |
| Campfire Circle | one tier: Regeneration I after 10 seconds              | –      |
| Mana Storage    | 75, 100, 125, 150, 175, 200, 225, 250 total mana       | 5, 8   |
| Mana Flow       | 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 1, 1.2, 1.5, 2, 2.5 mana per second     | 5, 8   |
| Smelting Master | 10, 20, 30% more SMP XP when collecting furnace output | –      |

Every member of your party or clan is a teammate, and someone in both counts once. The four Playing Together skills work only in the Overworld and never during player combat.

- Team Aura: at least one teammate must be within 32 blocks. If several auras are nearby, the strongest applies; they don't stack.
- Back Cover: teammates within 32 blocks of its owner take less mob damage. The strongest cover applies. It doesn't protect against players, their pets or environmental damage.
- Shared Loot: teammates within 32 blocks also get the stated share of the combat XP from your kills. You keep all your own XP. Mining and other activities aren't shared.
- Campfire Circle: place and light a campfire and stay within 32 blocks of it yourself. After 10 seconds, you and any teammates within 32 blocks of the fire get Regeneration I. Existing potion effects stay intact. Anyone who leaves the circle or gets into player combat loses the campfire effect, and when they come back, the wait starts again.
- Mana Storage and Mana Flow: without stars you have 50 mana and refill 0.5 mana per second.

### Fishing

| Ability      | Values per tier                                       | Points |
| :----------- | :---------------------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Fishing XP   | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% more SMP XP                       | 6, 10  |
| Treasure Eye | 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 percentage points more treasure chance | 6, 10  |
| Fast Bite    | 15, 30, 45% shorter wait                              | 6      |
| Double Catch | 8, 16, 24, 32, 40% chance of a second fish            | 6, 10  |
| Cook Catch   | one tier                                              | –      |

- Fishing XP: more SMP XP per catch. Fish and treasure give XP, junk doesn't. Your Minecraft XP stays separate.
- Treasure Eye raises your treasure chance in open water. The AFK rule still applies: after a while without your own input, you get fewer treasures.
- Fast Bite shortens the wait until a bite.
- Double Catch can give you a second fish of the same kind with your tier's chance, never treasure or junk. The second fish gives no extra XP.
- With Cook Catch, cod and salmon arrive already cooked.
- No fishing ability costs mana or needs sneaking. You can turn off Fast Bite, Double Catch and Cook Catch with `/skills toggle`.

### Mastery

Once every skill of an area is at its highest tier, you unlock three **Diamond tiers** there. You buy them strictly in order:

| Tier      | Cost           |
| :-------- | :------------- |
| Diamond 1 | 3 skill points |
| Diamond 2 | 4 skill points |
| Diamond 3 | 5 skill points |

So a full mastery costs 12 skill points. In the skill tree the diamonds sit on the outside of their area. In the constellation view they form a triangle beyond the area; in the column view they have their own row beneath it.

- Diamond tiers do not count towards the points that higher tiers of other skills need.
- A skill reset removes mastery as well, and you get its points back.
- Masteries cannot be turned off. Like all skills, they only work in Survival mode and not while you fight players.
- For Overworld masteries, enchantment bonuses only work when the enchantment is at its highest vanilla level. Your item stays unchanged. With Silk Touch there is no Fortune bonus.
- Water Walker doesn't need a helmet enchantment, and having real Aqua Affinity doesn't speed up mining any further.

| :------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mining Mastery      | Iron, gold and copper ore drop ingots directly.                                                   | With Fortune III on your pickaxe, mining works as if it had Fortune IV.                                     | With Efficiency V on your pickaxe, mine as if it had Efficiency VI.                                                              |
| Combat Mastery      | With Looting III on your weapon, mob drops work as if it had Looting IV.                          | Zombies, skeletons, creepers and piglins rarely drop their head.                                            | With Looting III on your weapon, mob drops work as if it had Looting V.                                                          |
| Fishing Mastery     | With Luck of the Sea III, fish as if it had Luck of the Sea IV.                                   | With Lure III, fish bite as if it had Lure IV.                                                              | No More Junk: fishing never yields junk.                                                                                         |
| Forestry Mastery    | With Efficiency V on your axe, chop as if it had Efficiency VI.                                   | A felled tree rarely gives a golden apple.                                                                  | Sneaking has a 10% chance to grow saplings within 3 blocks as if bone-mealed. At most one attempt every 0.5 s.                   |
| Farming Mastery     | With Fortune III, harvest ripe crops as if it had Fortune IV.                                     | Sneaking has a 10% chance to grow crops within 3 blocks as if bone-mealed. At most one attempt every 0.5 s. | A harvested carrot rarely turns into a golden carrot.                                                                            |
| Excavation Mastery  | **Glassblower:** natural sand and red sand drop glass directly.                                   | Natural gravel and sand rarely give a pottery sherd.                                                        | Natural soil very rarely gives a sniffer egg.                                                                                    |

Sneaking does not interrupt it. | Active skills have a 10% chance to cost no mana.                                                            | Meditation fills mana to 125%. The skill tree shows the exact chances of rare finds.

Building, Playing Together and crafting have no mastery. You'll find the [Nether masteries](/smp/skill-tree#nether-masteries) further down.

## Nether

You get the Nether skill tree the first time you actively play in the Nether, for example by mining a block or fighting. Entering the Nether isn't enough on its own, and until then the skill tree doesn't offer it. After that you switch back and forth at the top with **To the Nether skill tree** and **To the Overworld**. It uses the same skill points as the Overworld, and all Nether abilities only work in the Nether.

| Category  | Ability         | Values per tier                 | Points |
| :-------- | :-------------- | :------------------------------ | :----- |
| Gathering | Nether Mining   | 20, 40, 60, 80, 100% faster     | 3, 6   |
| Gathering | Nether Forestry | 8, 16, 32, 48, 64 blocks        | 3, 6   |
| Fighting  | Nether Combat   | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% more damage | 3, 6   |
| Fighting  | Fire Ward       | 4, 8, 12, 16, 20% less damage   | 3, 6   |
| Roaming   | Nether Wanderer | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% faster       | 3, 6   |
| Roaming   | Bartering       | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% chance      | 3, 6   |
| Roaming   | Piglin Friend   | one tier                        | –      |

All Nether abilities only work in Survival mode and never while you fight other players.

### Nether Mining

You mine faster while you dig nether quartz ore, nether gold ore, ancient debris or gilded blackstone. When you let go, the bonus is gone.

On top of that, natural nether quartz ore has a 5, 10, 15, 20 or 25% chance to give twice as much quartz. The chance rises with your tier.

### Nether Forestry

- When you fell a natural crimson or warped stem with an axe while sneaking, the wart blocks and shroomlights of its fungus fall too.
- It searches 5 blocks in every direction and up to 16 blocks above the felled stem. The nearest blocks fall first, up to the value of your tier.
- It costs no mana, and you don't need Tree Feller for it.
- Placed wart blocks and shroomlights remain.

### Nether Combat

- More damage against blazes, wither skeletons, ghasts, magma cubes, hoglins, zoglins and piglin brutes.
- Only your own melee hits and your own projectiles count, such as arrows. Pets and TNT don't.
- It does not work against bosses of public events.

### Fire Ward

You take less damage when you stand in fire, burn, are in lava, walk on magma blocks or touch a campfire.

### Nether Wanderer

You walk faster on every floor in the Nether, on top of the Soul Speed enchantment. For the whole skill revision you get one shared free reset, which you can use but don't have to. There is no separate transfer or refund for Soul Speed.

### Bartering

With the chance of your tier, a piglin gives you twice as much for your gold. This is how it works:

- You give an adult piglin a gold ingot, either by throwing it or by handing it over with a right-click.
- The piglin trades within 15 seconds after that.
- When it trades, you are at most 16 blocks away from it.
- Gold in the trade is never doubled: you get gold ingots, gold blocks, gold nuggets and raw gold only once.
- Your gold ingot does not come back.

### Piglin Friend

Piglins stay neutral even without gold armour. Attacking them or opening their containers still makes them hostile. Piglin brutes remain dangerous.

### Nether masteries

Once you've maxed out every ordinary skill in a Nether area, you can buy its Diamond tiers in order for 3, 4 and 5 skill points. Each tier keeps the bonuses of the ones before it. They only work in the Nether and in Survival mode, never during player combat.

| Mastery                  | Diamond 1                                                                                                                                | Diamond 2                                                                                        | Diamond 3                                                                   |
| :----------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nether Gathering Mastery | **Quartz Luck:** Nether quartz and Nether gold drop as with at least Fortune IV, even without Fortune on your tool. Not with Silk Touch. | **Wart Garden:** Sneaking has a 10% chance to grow Nether wart and Nether fungi within 3 blocks. | **Debris Find:** Natural ancient debris has a 10% chance to drop one extra. |
| Nether Combat Mastery    | **Witherproof:** Wither lasts half as long.                                                                                              | **Blaze Hunter:** Blazes have a 25% chance to drop an extra blaze rod.                           | **Fire Walker:** No afterburn after fire or lava.                           |
| Nether Roaming Mastery   | **Strider Rider:** Ridden striders move 50% faster.                                                                                      | **Lava Breath:** Fire Resistance lasts 50% longer in the Nether.                                 | **No Junk:** Piglins never barter gravel, blackstone or Nether bricks.      |

Territory rights also apply to Quartz Luck, Debris Find and Wart Garden. Placed ores give no extra drops. Your tool stays unchanged.

## End

The End skill tree appears once you've entered the open End for the first time. You choose the realm and layout at the top, and all realms share your skill points.

End skills only work in the End and in Survival mode. They never apply in player combat or against the Ender Dragon.

### Gathering

| Ability         | Values per tier                                                       | Points |
| :-------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Chorus Harvest  | 8, 16, 24, 32, 48 plant blocks; 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% extra chorus fruit | 3, 6   |
| Shell Collector | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% chance of an extra shulker shell                   | 3, 6   |

Sneak while breaking chorus to harvest the whole natural plant, at no mana cost. If the plant is too large, placed or protected, the harvest doesn't start. Your territory rights apply to every further block, and you have to keep sneaking and holding the same tool. Use `/skills toggle chorus-harvest` to turn Chorus Harvest off.

### Combat

| Ability          | Values per tier                                                           | Points |
| :--------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----- |
| End Combat       | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% more damage against endermen, shulkers and endermites | 3, 6   |
| Levitation Guard | 20, 40, 60, 80, 100% shorter levitation from shulkers                     | 3, 6   |
| Enderman Calm    | looking at endermen does not provoke them; one tier                       | –      |

End Combat counts your own melee attacks and projectiles. Attacking endermen still makes them hostile.

### Travel

| Ability     | Values per tier                                                           | Points |
| :---------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----- |
| Pearl Throw | 20, 40, 60, 80, 100% less damage from ender pearls                        | 3, 6   |
| Void Saver  | last safe position instead of void death, once every 30 minutes; one tier | –      |

Void Saver takes you back to your last safe position within the same world, as long as that position is still safe and available. It never rescues you while you carry the dragon egg. If there's no safe way back, the usual [death rules](/smp/death) apply.

### End masteries

Each area has three diamond tiers. Once you've maxed out every ordinary skill in the area, you can buy them in order for 3, 4 and 5 skill points.

| Area      | Diamond 1                                                                             | Diamond 2                                                                             | Diamond 3                                                         |
| :-------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gathering | **End Stone Breaker:** mine end stone and purpur with a pickaxe as with Efficiency VI | **Chorus Gardener:** sneaking has a 10% chance to grow chorus flowers within 3 blocks | **Shell Rain:** shulkers always drop at least one shell           |
| Combat    | **Pearl Hunter:** endermen always drop at least one ender pearl                       | **Teleport Ban:** hit endermen cannot teleport for 3 seconds                          | **Armour Breaker:** closed shulkers take full damage              |
| Travel    | **Pearl Flight:** ender pearls fly 25% farther                                        | **Soft Landing:** no fall damage up to 20 blocks                                      | **Void Master:** Void Saver recharges in 10 instead of 30 minutes |

End masteries also only work in the End, never in player combat or against the Ender Dragon. End Stone Breaker leaves your pickaxe unchanged.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

You can also type `/skill` instead of `/skills`, and `/skillstree` instead of `/skilltree`.

- `/skills tree` or `/skilltree`: skill tree
- `/skills toggle <ability>`: turn an ability on or off
- `/skills reset`: reset stars
