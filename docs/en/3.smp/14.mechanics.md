To keep the server running smoothly even with lots of players, some values on the SMP differ from vanilla. Two things stand out most: you can't trade with villagers, and the night isn't skipped but runs faster once enough players sleep. Mobs, items, hoppers and redstone also behave differently in places.

## Cushions

Cushions are vanilla furniture you can sit on, and they work the same way on the SMP.

- **Crafting:** put three wool slabs of the same colour in a row at a crafting table to get a cushion, for example a **White Cushion**. Use a dye to recolour a cushion.
- **Placing:** put the cushion on top of a block.
- **Sitting:** right-click a free cushion. Only one player can sit on a cushion at a time. Sneak to stand up again.
- **Removing:** hit the cushion and you get it back as an item.

If you log out while sitting on a cushion, you stand up and the cushion stays where it is. The same goes for a normal server restart.

## Villagers

Villagers and wandering traders live their normal lives in their villages. They spawn, breed, take jobs, harvest fields and sleep at night. **But you can't trade with them.** Clicking one doesn't open a trading window.

You can still use a name tag on villagers.

### Why?

Without villager trading, good finds keep their value, and trading between players matters more. If you're good at exploring, enchanting or combining books at the anvil, you have something others need.

### Where do I get …?

- **Enchanted books and gear:** enchanting table at level 30, anvil, chests in structures, fishing
- **Top levels such as Sharpness V or Protection IV:** enchanting table at level 30, or combine two equal levels at the anvil
- **Mending:** fishing, chests, pots and archaeology in structures, such as ancient cities, Nether fortresses, towers and ruins
- **Frost Walker:** fishing, chests in structures
- **Soul Speed:** bastions, bartering with piglins
- **Swift Sneak:** ancient cities
- **Wind Burst:** ominous vaults in trial chambers
- **Bottles o' enchanting:** chests in many structures
- **Explorer maps:** chests in structures
- **Globe banner pattern:** chests in structures
- **Ender pearls:** endermen

You still find emeralds, and you can use them for beacons and building.

## Sleeping

The night isn't skipped; instead it runs faster once enough players in the Overworld are asleep. A boss bar shows everyone how many are still missing. If someone gets up and there are no longer enough, time goes back to normal speed.

Only active players count: anyone who does nothing for 5 minutes drops out. Even with `/afk`, you only drop out of the count after 5 minutes. Anyone lying in bed always counts, though.

How many have to sleep:

- 1 player: 1
- 2 players: 1
- 3 players: 2
- 4 players: 2
- 6 players: 3
- 10 players: 4
- 20 players: 5
- 30 players: 6
- 50 players: 9
- 75 players: 11

## Locator bar and minecarts

- The locator bar is off in every realm.
- Minecarts go up to 16 blocks per second, so twice as fast as in vanilla.

## Mob spawning

Fewer mobs spawn on the SMP than in vanilla:

| Category             | Vanilla | SMP |
| :------------------- | ------: | --: |
| Ambient (bats)       |      15 |   4 |
| Axolotls             |       5 |   3 |
| Monsters             |      70 |  25 |
| Water ambient (fish) |      20 |  10 |

This is how often the game tries to spawn mobs, in ticks (20 ticks = 1 second):

| Category          | Vanilla | SMP |
| :---------------- | ------: | --: |
| Ambient           |       1 | 400 |
| Animals           |     400 | 300 |
| Axolotls          |       1 | 400 |
| Monsters          |       1 |  10 |
| Water ambient     |       1 | 200 |
| Water animals     |       1 | 100 |
| Underground water |       1 | 200 |

## Activation range

The server only rarely processes mobs that are further away from the nearest player, so AFK farms only work if you stand close enough.

| Type     | Vanilla | SMP |
| :------- | ------: | --: |
| Animals  |      32 |  16 |
| Monsters |      32 |  24 |
| Other    |      16 |  16 |

## Despawn distance

| Distance                   | Vanilla | SMP |
| :------------------------- | ------: | --: |
| Mob _may_ despawn randomly |      32 |  28 |
| Mob _always_ despawns      |     128 |  72 |

The distance is measured horizontally from the nearest player. This applies to all mobs except named ones, which never despawn, as in vanilla.

## Items and XP merge

Items and XP orbs on the ground merge into one stack faster, sometimes even through thin walls.

| What  | Vanilla |        SMP |
| :---- | ------: | ---------: |
| Items |     0.5 | 3.5 blocks |
| XP    |       – |   5 blocks |

## Item despawn

In vanilla, an item despawns after 5 minutes. On the SMP, typical bulk drops vanish sooner. **This also applies to items you lose.**

- **30 seconds:** arrows, cobblestone, cobbled deepslate, andesite, diorite, granite, tuff, dirt, netherrack, red sand, leaves, grass, twisting vines, weeping vines, golden swords
- **40 seconds:** bamboo, cactus, kelp, melons, melon slices, pumpkins, sugar cane
- **45 seconds:** scaffolding
- **2 minutes:** sand, gravel
- **everything else:** 5 minutes, as in vanilla

## Hoppers, containers, grass and spawners

| What                        | Vanilla       | SMP           |
| :-------------------------- | :------------ | :------------ |
| Hoppers check for new items | every tick    | every tick    |
| Hopper transfer             | every 8 ticks | every 8 ticks |
| Container updates           | every tick    | every 3 ticks |
| Grass spreading             | every tick    | every 4 ticks |
| Mob spawners                | every tick    | every 2 ticks |

Item sorters and clocks from vanilla tutorials may therefore need small adjustments.

## Fishing

AFK fishing is still allowed. If you fish for a long time without any input of your own, though, you find treasure less often, while fish and junk stay unchanged. Automating it with mods, macros or bots is still forbidden.

## Redstone

For redstone, the server uses the “Alternate Current” implementation, so redstone dust works out its power for a whole network at once. That behaves almost exactly like vanilla, except that the order of updates no longer depends on position. Because of that, a handful of circuits that rely on random vanilla behaviour act differently.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do villagers not trade?

So that good finds keep their value. Where to get everything instead is listed under [Villagers](/smp/mechanics#villagers).

### My farm runs slower than in vanilla.

Some values differ, for example for [mob spawning](/smp/mechanics#mob-spawning), the [activation range](/smp/mechanics#activation-range) and [hoppers, containers, grass and spawners](/smp/mechanics#hoppers-containers-grass-and-spawners).

### May I fish while AFK?

Yes. After a long time without any input of your own, though, you find treasure less often. Fish and junk stay the same. Mods, macros and bots for automatic fishing are forbidden. See [Fishing](/smp/mechanics#fishing).
