So that the server runs smoothly with many players, some values on the SMP differ from vanilla. You notice two things most: you cannot trade with villagers, and the night is not skipped but runs faster once enough players sleep. Mobs, items, hoppers and redstone also behave differently in places.

## Cushions

Cushions are vanilla furniture you can sit on. On the SMP they work as in vanilla.

- **Crafting:** three wool slabs of the same colour in a row at a crafting table make a cushion, for example a **White Cushion**. Use a dye to recolour a cushion.
- **Placing:** put the cushion on top of a block.
- **Sitting:** right-click a free cushion. Only one player can sit on a cushion. Sneak to stand up again.
- **Removing:** hit the cushion. You get it back as an item.

An empty cushion stays where it is. If you log out while sitting on it, the cushion disappears from the world. The same happens if you are sitting on it during a server restart.

::callout{type="info"}
Preview of game update 0.26.0, not released yet: if you are sitting on a cushion when you log out, you stand up. The cushion then stays where it is, also during a normal server restart.
::

## Villagers

Villagers and wandering traders live normally in their villages. They spawn, breed, take jobs, harvest fields and sleep at night. **But you cannot trade with them.** Clicking them opens no trading window.

You can still use a name tag on villagers.

### Why?

Without villager trading, good finds keep their value. Trading between players matters more: whoever explores, enchants or combines books at the anvil well has something others need.

### Where do I get …?

- **Enchanted books and gear:** enchanting table at level 30, anvil, chests in structures, fishing
- **Top levels such as Sharpness V or Protection IV:** enchanting table at level 30, or combine two equal levels at the anvil
- **Mending:** fishing, chests, pots and archaeology in structures, such as ancient cities, Nether fortresses, towers and ruins
- **Frost Walker:** fishing, chests in structures
- **Soul Speed:** bastions, bartering with piglins
- **Swift Sneak:** ancient cities
- **Wind Burst:** ominous vaults in trial chambers
- **Bottles o' enchanting:** chests in many structures
- **Explorer maps:** chests in structures
- **Globe banner pattern:** chests in structures
- **Ender pearls:** endermen

You still find emeralds. Use them for beacons and building.

## Sleeping

The night is not skipped; it runs faster once enough players in the Overworld sleep. Only active players count. Whoever does nothing for 5 minutes does not count. With `/afk` you also only drop out of the count after 5 minutes. Whoever lies in bed always counts. A boss bar shows everyone how many are still missing. If someone gets up and it is no longer enough, time runs normally again.

This many must sleep:

- 1 player: 1
- 2 players: 1
- 3 players: 2
- 4 players: 2
- 6 players: 3
- 10 players: 4
- 20 players: 5
- 30 players: 6
- 50 players: 9
- 75 players: 11

## Locator bar and minecarts

- The locator bar is off in every realm.
- Minecarts go up to 16 blocks per second, twice as fast as in vanilla.

## Mob spawning

Fewer mobs spawn than in vanilla:

| Category             | Vanilla | SMP |
| :------------------- | ------: | --: |
| Ambient (bats)       |      15 |   4 |
| Axolotls             |       5 |   3 |
| Monsters             |      70 |  25 |
| Water ambient (fish) |      20 |  10 |

How often the game tries to spawn mobs, in ticks (20 ticks = 1 second):

| Category          | Vanilla | SMP |
| :---------------- | ------: | --: |
| Ambient           |       1 | 400 |
| Animals           |     400 | 300 |
| Axolotls          |       1 | 400 |
| Monsters          |       1 |  10 |
| Water ambient     |       1 | 200 |
| Water animals     |       1 | 100 |
| Underground water |       1 | 200 |

## Activation range

Mobs further from the nearest player are only rarely processed. AFK farms only work if you stand close enough.

| Type     | Vanilla | SMP |
| :------- | ------: | --: |
| Animals  |      32 |  16 |
| Monsters |      32 |  24 |
| Other    |      16 |  16 |

## Despawn distance

| Distance                   | Vanilla | SMP |
| :------------------------- | ------: | --: |
| Mob _may_ despawn randomly |      32 |  28 |
| Mob _always_ despawns      |     128 |  72 |

This applies to all mobs, measured horizontally from the nearest player. Named mobs never despawn, as in vanilla.

## Items and XP merge

Items and XP orbs on the ground merge into one stack faster, sometimes even through thin walls.

| What  | Vanilla |        SMP |
| :---- | ------: | ---------: |
| Items |     0.5 | 3.5 blocks |
| XP    |       – |   5 blocks |

## Item despawn

In vanilla an item despawns after 5 minutes. In the SMP typical bulk drops despawn faster. **This also applies to items you lose.**

- **30 seconds:** arrows, cobblestone, cobbled deepslate, andesite, diorite, granite, tuff, dirt, netherrack, red sand, leaves, grass, twisting vines, weeping vines, golden swords
- **40 seconds:** bamboo, cactus, kelp, melons, melon slices, pumpkins, sugar cane
- **45 seconds:** scaffolding
- **2 minutes:** sand, gravel
- **everything else:** 5 minutes, as in vanilla

## Hoppers, containers, grass and spawners

| What                        | Vanilla       | SMP           |
| :-------------------------- | :------------ | :------------ |
| Hoppers check for new items | every tick    | every tick    |
| Hopper transfer             | every 8 ticks | every 8 ticks |
| Container updates           | every tick    | every 3 ticks |
| Grass spreading             | every tick    | every 4 ticks |
| Mob spawners                | every tick    | every 2 ticks |

Item sorters and clocks from vanilla tutorials may therefore need small adjustments.

## Fishing

AFK fishing remains allowed. Fishing for a long time without your own input yields fewer treasures. Fish and junk stay unchanged. Automation with mods, macros or bots remains forbidden.

## Redstone

The server uses the “Alternate Current” redstone implementation. Redstone dust calculates its power for a whole network at once. It behaves almost exactly like vanilla. Only the order of updates no longer depends on position. A very few circuits that rely on random vanilla behaviour therefore act differently.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do villagers not trade?

So that good finds keep their value. Where you get everything instead is under [Villagers](/smp/mechanics#villagers).

### My farm runs slower than in vanilla.

Some values differ, for example for mobs, hoppers and the activation range. More under [Mob spawning](/smp/mechanics#mob-spawning), [Activation range](/smp/mechanics#activation-range) and [Hoppers, containers, grass and spawners](/smp/mechanics#hoppers-containers-grass-and-spawners).

### May I fish while AFK?

Yes. After a long time without your own input you find fewer treasures. Fish and junk stay the same. Mods, macros and bots for automatic fishing are forbidden. See [Fishing](/smp/mechanics#fishing).
