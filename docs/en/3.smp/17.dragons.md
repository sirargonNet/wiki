The Ender Dragon on the SMP fights differently from vanilla. Every newly summoned dragon gets a **tier**, which sets its health, and a **type**, which decides its abilities and its loot. The End crystals on the pillars gain powers of their own as well.

That goes for every dragon you summon after the End event. The End event's own dragon stays a surprise. How PvP, graves and SMP XP work during the fight is explained under [The dragon fight](/smp/realms#the-dragon-fight), and the [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg) has its own page.

## How a dragon is made

The fight area is the square of **200 × 200 blocks around 0/0** in the End. Everyone standing there when the dragon starts has a say in how strong it gets.

You have your own dragon counter, which goes up by 1 whenever a dragon starts while you are in the fight area in Survival or Adventure mode. The server averages the counters of everyone there and rounds down, and that is the **fight number**: two players with 3 and 6 go up to 4 and 7 as the dragon starts, which makes a fight number of 5. The fight number sets the tier, and the type is picked at random from that tier's types.

From fight number **4**, the crystals on the pillars have powers right away. Below that, only crystals that [rebuild](#rebuilding) get powers.

## Tiers

| Tier       | Fight number       | Health | Possible types                         |
| :--------- | :----------------- | -----: | :------------------------------------- |
| Fledgeling | 1 to 4             |    250 | Zombified, Mossy, Warped, Sculk, Frost |
| Powerful   | 5 to 9             |    350 | Sculk, Frost, Tyrant, Skeletal         |
| Cruel      | 10 to 15           |    500 | Tyrant, Skeletal, Blazing, Elder       |
| Immortal   | 16 to 20           |    750 | Blazing, Elder, Wight                  |
| Ferocious  | 21 to 30           |    850 | all from Zombified to Thunder          |
| Primordial | 31 and up          |  1,000 | all from Zombified to Artillery        |
| Ascended   | only with Bad Omen |  1,250 | all from Zombified to Artillery        |

One point of health is half a heart, and for comparison, a vanilla dragon has 200. "From … to" follows the order under [Dragon types](#dragon-types).

From Ferocious on, the weaker types turn up less often. If the pick lands on Zombified to Wight, there is a 20 % chance of a new pick from just these types:

- **Ferocious:** Elder, Wight, Beacon, Desert and Thunder
- **Primordial:** Beacon, Desert, Thunder and Artillery
- **Ascended:** Thunder and Artillery

The name above the health bar shows tier and type, for example "Cruel Elder Dragon".

### Special fights

The server counts every dragon fight on the SMP, separately from your own counter. The 20th and 30th fights bring a dragon you never meet otherwise.

| Fight | Dragon          | Tier      | Health |
| :---- | :-------------- | :-------- | -----: |
| 20th  | The Last Dragon | Immortal  |    750 |
| 30th  | 10,000 Dragon   | Ferocious |    850 |

These two dragons only appear in these fights.

### Bad Omen

If anyone in the fight area has the **Bad Omen** effect when the dragon starts, the dragon becomes **Ominous**. The omen is then removed from everyone in the fight area.

- The tier goes up by one. Primordial becomes Ascended this way.
- The name starts with "Ominous", for example "Ominous Frost Dragon".
- The dragon attacks more often.
- It drops **twice as much** loot.

You can get Bad Omen from an Ominous Bottle, for example.

## What every dragon does

All damage numbers here are without armour. With armour and protective effects you lose less.

- **Healing:** While End crystals stand, the dragon heals itself on top of the crystals' own healing, and faster the higher its tier.
- **Beds and respawn anchors:** They vanish as soon as the dragon comes close, and so does falling water below it.
- **General attacks:** Every now and then the dragon picks one of these:
  - **Dive:** It dives down to the ground.
  - **End rod spikes:** When it lands, End rods shoot out of the ground. A hit costs 1.5 hearts.
  - **Shulker bullets:** 13 bullets fly at random players.
  - **Meteor shower:** Large dragon fireballs fall around the centre. The meteor shower only comes once all crystals are destroyed.
- **Forced landing:** Once all crystals are destroyed, the dragon can pull you out of elytra flight. You then fall fast for 20 seconds but take no fall damage. The Ominous dragon can do this even while crystals still stand.

Behind end stone, obsidian or bedrock you are safe from lasers and sonic booms.

## End crystals

### Rebuilding

When a crystal on a pillar is destroyed, it rebuilds itself after **5 to 15 seconds**. The chat says: "An End Crystal has regenerated!"

| Tier            | Rebuilds per pillar |
| :-------------- | :------------------ |
| Fledgeling      | 1 or 2 times        |
| Powerful        | 2 times             |
| Cruel           | 3 times             |
| Immortal and up | 4 times             |

When the dragon dies, all remaining crystals destroy themselves.

### Crystal powers

You can tell a crystal with powers by a visible sign, for example a hat or a cage. It has up to three powers, and the higher the tier, the more of them and the nastier they get.

| Power       | From tier  | What it does                                                                        |
| :---------- | :--------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cage        | Fledgeling | A cage protects the crystal.                                                        |
| Fire        | Fledgeling | Whoever comes closer than 3.5 blocks keeps taking 1.5 hearts of fire damage.        |
| Force field | Fledgeling | Destroys arrows and other projectiles within 5.5 blocks. Tridents bounce back.      |
| Laser       | Fledgeling | Aims at a player on the ground for 3 seconds, then deals 2.5 hearts.                |
| Disarm      | Powerful   | A random player drops the items in their main hand and off hand.                    |
| Witch       | Powerful   | Gives a player without effects a bad effect, for example Blindness or Poison.       |
| Weightless  | Powerful   | Whoever stands in a 9 × 9 column around the crystal floats upwards.                 |
| Curse       | Cruel      | When it is destroyed, the nearest player takes 7.5 hearts of damage.                |
| Launcher    | Ferocious  | Shoots Eyes of Ender at players with elytra within 40 blocks. A hit costs 5 hearts. |
| Portal      | Ferocious  | Teleports up to three players to another pillar.                                    |

Curse, Disarm and Portal only exist on one crystal at a time.

Against the 10,000 Dragon, a rebuilt crystal can get a crown with a 20 % chance. The chat then says: "A 10,000 Crystal has been created!" While this crystal stands, all other crystals are invulnerable.

## Dragon types

Many dragons call servants, usually three each at up to three random players, and many change the ground beneath them.

::callout{type="warning"}
After the victory, the ground in the fight area turns back into end stone. That includes sand, snow, moss and similar blocks you placed there yourself.
::

### Zombified Dragon

- Turns the ground below it into mud.
- Calls Zombie Servants: husks with 18 health.
- Loot: golden apples, bottles o' enchanting, iron ingots

### Mossy Dragon

- Covers the ground below it with moss.
- Throws every player on the ground into the air. That costs 1.5 hearts.
- Calls Mossy Servants: slimes with 18 health.
- Loot: slimeballs, glow berries, moss blocks

### Warped Dragon

- Covers the ground below it with warped wart blocks and warped hyphae.
- Lands more often.
- Teleports to a random crystal pillar.
- Leaves no dragon's breath clouds.
- Loot: ender pearls, shulker shells, ghast tears

### Sculk Dragon

- Covers the ground below it with sculk.
- Charges a sonic boom and fires it at the nearest player on the ground. Anyone within 4 blocks of the impact loses 3.5 hearts.
- Loot: echo shards, sculk catalysts, reinforced deepslate

### Frost Dragon

- Turns the ground below it into snow, powder snow and blue ice.
- Places igloos of ice: around up to three players, on the fountain in the centre or around up to three crystals. After 15 seconds an igloo shatters, and every player within 5.5 blocks of it loses 3 hearts to freezing.
- Loot: a goat horn, a book with Frost Walker I, blue ice, snow blocks, emeralds

### Dragon Tyrant

- Turns the ground below it into soul sand.
- Calls Tyrant's Servants: wither skeletons with 18 health.
- Loot: a Nether Star, 1 to 3 wither skeleton skulls, netherite scrap, wither roses, soul sand, coal

### Skeletal Dragon

- Lands often.
- When it lands, it calls four Skeletal Servants around the exit portal: skeletons with 20 health. This costs it 40 health itself.
- While its servants live, it is invulnerable.
- Once all crystals are destroyed and no servants are left, it slowly heals itself.
- Loot: bones, ghast tears, arrows

### Blazing Dragon

- Sets the ground below it alight with fire and soul fire.
- Often attacks with fireballs.
- Calls Blazing Servants: blazes with 18 health.
- Loot: blaze rods, netherite scrap, gunpowder

### Elder Dragon

- Glows and can be seen through walls.
- Casts one of three spells:
  - Elder Servants: vexes with 5 health
  - Rings of fangs around up to three players
  - Up to three players on the ground float upwards for 2 seconds.
- Loot: bottles o' enchanting, enchanted golden apples, totems of undying

### Dragon Wight

- Makes no sounds.
- Never lands.
- Often attacks with fireballs and dives.
- Loot: diamond blocks, dragon's breath, netherite upgrade smithing templates, shulker boxes

### Beacon Dragon

- Glows and can be seen through walls.
- Fires a laser forwards, downwards or at the nearest player. Every hit costs half a heart. The laser reaches 75 blocks.
- Loot: a beacon, diamond, emerald, iron and gold blocks

### Desert Dragon

- Covers the ground below it with sand and cacti.
- Shoots fireballs at the nearest player. A hit costs 2.5 hearts.
- Calls Desert Servants: hoglins with 18 health.
- Loot: netherite scrap, gold blocks, enchanted golden apples

### Thunder Dragon

- Six lightning pillars circle the centre. Touching one costs 3 hearts. They come back every 30 seconds.
- Lightning strikes instead of dragon's breath.
- Rarely lands.
- Loot: a trident, a creeper head, skeleton skull or zombie head, wind charges, redstone blocks

### Artillery Dragon

- Lays five glowing landmines around the centre. After 5 seconds they are armed. If a player or projectile comes closer than 5 blocks, they explode, and everyone within 7 blocks loses 5 hearts.
- Fires missiles. One of them chases players with elytra. A hit costs 5 hearts.
- Loot: firework rockets, TNT, iron blocks

### The Last Dragon

- Covers the ground below it with amethyst.
- Shoots amethyst projectiles at up to five players. A hit costs 4 hearts.
- Forces players with elytra to land.
- Loot: netherite ingots, enchanted golden apples, budding amethyst

### 10,000 Dragon

- Turns the ground below it into gold.
- Fires lasers at the five nearest players. A hit costs 1.5 hearts.
- Sends shockwaves out from the centre. They cost 2.5 hearts.
- Forces players with elytra to land.
- Crowns End crystals, see [Crystal powers](#crystal-powers).
- Loot: netherite ingots, enchanted golden apples, gold blocks

The Last Dragon and the 10,000 Dragon drop a dragon egg too, but it vanishes straight away, because the SMP has only one [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg).

## Loot

When the dragon dies, the loot appears above the exit portal. Every dragon drops three parts:

1. **General loot:** 3 to 4 stacks of diamonds, obsidian, phantom membranes, amethyst shards, gold ingots and rarely End crystals. On top, a dragon head with a 15 % chance and an Ominous Bottle with a 35 % chance.
2. **Type loot:** see [Dragon types](#dragon-types).
3. **One dragon item:** one of the twelve items below.

The higher the tier, the larger the stacks.

### Dragon items

Whoever stands closest to the exit portal when the dragon dies decides the dragon item: it is always one they do not have in their inventory yet. If they already have all twelve, they get a book with a random dragon enchantment instead.

## Advancements

The dragon advancements are in the advancement menu on the **Dragonkind Evolved** tab. You get the tab as soon as a dragon starts while you are in the fight area.

Only the player the game credits with the kill gets an advancement for a defeated dragon. For the tiers, the fight number counts, not the extra tier from Bad Omen, and Next Gen only starts at fight number 2.

### Tiers

| Advancement                     | How to get it                                         |
| :------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------- |
| Next Gen                        | Kill a dragon at fight number 2 to 4 (Fledgeling)     |
| Getting Tougher                 | Kill a dragon at fight number 5 to 9 (Powerful)       |
| Unusual Punishment              | Kill a dragon at fight number 10 to 15 (Cruel)        |
| Dragonhearted                   | Kill a dragon at fight number 16 to 20 (Immortal)     |
| There's More?!                  | Kill a dragon at fight number 21 to 30 (Ferocious)    |
| Return of the Dragon Lords      | Kill a dragon at fight number 31 or more (Primordial) |
| I Have a Bad Feeling About This | Kill an Ominous dragon                                |
| Feel the Power                  | Collect all twelve dragon items                       |

| Necromancer's Nightmare      | Kill a Zombified Dragon  |
| Overgrown Terror             | Kill a Mossy Dragon      |
| Corrupted Beyond Recognition | Kill a Warped Dragon     |
| The Prisoner                 | Kill a Sculk Dragon      |
| Pillar of Ice                | Kill a Frost Dragon      |
| I Understood That Reference! | Kill a Dragon Tyrant     |

## Commands

You do not need a command for the dragons.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is our dragon so strong?

The tier depends on the fight number. Players who have fought many dragons raise the average. New players in the fight area lower it.

### Can we choose the type?

No. The type is drawn at random from the tier's types. Only the 20th and the 30th fight have a fixed dragon.

### Why did the crystal rebuild itself?

Crystals on the pillars rebuild themselves several times, depending on the tier. Keep destroying them until they stop coming back.

### Why did I not get a dragon item?

Someone else was probably closer to the exit portal when the dragon died. Stand near the portal as the fight ends.
