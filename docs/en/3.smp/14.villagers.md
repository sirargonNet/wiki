Villagers and wandering traders live normally in their villages. They spawn, breed, take jobs, harvest fields and sleep at night. **But you cannot trade with them.** Clicking them opens no trading window.

You can still use a name tag on villagers.

## Why?

Without villager trading, good finds keep their value. Trading between players matters more: whoever explores, enchants or combines books at the anvil well has something others need.

## Where do I get …?

- **Enchanted books and gear:** enchanting table at level 30, anvil, chests in structures, fishing
- **Top levels such as Sharpness V or Protection IV:** enchanting table at level 30, or combine two equal levels at the anvil
- **Mending:** fishing, chests, pots and archaeology in structures, such as ancient cities, Nether fortresses, towers and ruins
- **Frost Walker:** fishing, chests in structures
- **Soul Speed:** bastions, bartering with piglins
- **Swift Sneak:** ancient cities
- **Wind Burst:** ominous vaults in trial chambers
- **Bottles o' enchanting:** chests in many structures
- **Explorer maps:** chests in structures
- **Globe banner pattern:** chests in structures
- **Ender pearls:** endermen

You still find emeralds. Use them for beacons and building.
