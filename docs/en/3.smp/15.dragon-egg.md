After the first dragon victory, the dragon egg appears on top of the exit portal in the centre of the End. Anything players built there disappears without dropping. Whoever then stands alone in the circle around the egg for five seconds gets it, and can place it or pass it on. When the End opens is explained under [Realms](/smp/realms#end).

## Claim the egg

The egg is shown at twice its normal size, with a purple particle circle around it. The display above it shows how many players are standing in the circle. When you are the only one inside, the circle turns green.

1. Free up a slot in your inventory.
2. Step into the circle on your own, in survival mode.
3. Stay there until the **5, 4, 3, 2, 1** countdown is over, and the egg is yours.

If someone else steps in, the countdown stops. Once you are alone again, the five seconds start over. SMP chat announces who claimed the egg.

| Rule                     | Value                                 |
| :----------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| Circle radius            | 5 blocks horizontally                 |
| Height for taking part   | Up to 3 blocks above or below the egg |
| Time alone in the circle | 5 seconds                             |

If the display says **Paused**, the countdown is on hold for now.

## Place and pass on the egg

You can place the egg like a block in any realm. To give it up, drop it with your drop key, and it is up for grabs again.

Anyone can right-click a placed egg, even in other players' camps and cities. It then floats above a circle right where it is, and the countdown decides who gets it. Outsiders still can't break the block.

## Open egg

The egg is **open** while someone carries it, or while it is placed but not yet secured. While it is open:

- Whoever carries the egg glows, and everyone can see them, even through walls.
- After every full hour someone carries the egg, SMP chat tells everyone the realm and a spot no more than 100 blocks from the egg. It doesn't name the carrier.
- If the egg is open in a camp or city, PvP is on for everyone across the whole area, whatever its PvP setting. A city's plots are part of it; an outpost counts as an area of its own.
- Looting is still forbidden. Chests and other containers stay protected.

### Building area around the open egg

Within **16 blocks** of an open egg in a camp or city, outsiders may build and break blocks too. Nothing drops and nobody gets XP. TNT and other explosions only have an effect inside that area there, and nothing drops from them either.

Some things stay protected: chests and other containers, the egg itself, the block under it, and any block something hangs on. Pistons don't move anything here, not even the members' own.

Once the egg is no longer open there and no outsiders are left, the area repairs itself after about **5 minutes**. Whatever members built or broke themselves stays as it is.

## Secure a placed egg

If you place the egg in a camp or city, it stays open there for **6 hours**. Every hour, SMP chat says how long it stays open and roughly where it is: the realm and a spot no more than 100 blocks from the egg.

After that, the egg is secured for that area. PvP caused by the egg ends, and only players who may break blocks there can pick it up. Everyone else sees: "This dragon egg is secured. Only the people of this claim can take it now."

| Case                                      | What happens                   |
| :---------------------------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| Secured egg placed again in the same area | It stays secured.              |
| Egg placed in another camp or city        | The 6 hours start again.       |
| Egg placed in the wilderness              | It is never secured.           |
| Egg taken before the 6 hours are over     | It does not get secured there. |

## Store the egg

The egg only fits in your inventory. Chests, barrels, shulker boxes, bundles, ender chests and other containers won't take it, and hoppers can't move it.

If an egg is still sitting in a chest or barrel, it pops out next to it and can be claimed again.

## Fighting for the egg

For the first dragon, PvP in the End stays off until the egg appears on the exit portal, and is on after that.

The dragon egg is an [RP reason](/smp/death#pvp) for PvP: whoever carries it, fights for it or helps its carrier may be attacked. The egg and its carrier must always stay reachable, so you may not wall them in, lock them up or bunker them. More under [PvP and death](/smp/death#pvp) and in the [rules](/reference/rules).

## Protection and death

The egg never despawns, and neither fire nor explosions can destroy it. If water or another block takes the place of a placed egg, it can be claimed again right next to it.

If you die while carrying it, it does not go into your [grave](/smp/death). Instead it can be claimed again, and SMP chat names the realm and a spot no more than **100 blocks** from the egg.

## Logging out and changing servers

If you leave the SMP or go to the lobby while carrying the egg, it can be claimed again where you last were. Chat tells everyone roughly where, as above.

If you log out mid-fight, your combat-log NPC holds on to the egg at first. Come back while the NPC is still alive and combat is still running, and you keep it. If the NPC is killed or combat ends before you're back, the egg can be claimed again.

If the server crashes unexpectedly, the egg is not lost: it stays reserved for you for **one hour**. Return to the SMP within that hour to keep it; otherwise it can be claimed again. Further restarts do not extend the hour.

## Commands

The dragon egg needs no command of its own.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can't I claim the egg?

You need a free inventory slot and must be in survival mode. Then stand alone in the circle for five seconds. If the display says **Paused**, wait for the countdown to continue.

### Why can't I place the egg right now?

Right after a server start, the server is still loading camps and cities. Wait a moment and try again.

### Do I get the egg straight away when I break it?

No. A broken egg can be claimed again, and whoever stands alone in the circle for five seconds gets it.

### Can I keep the egg safe in a chest?

No, the egg only fits in your inventory. Place it in your camp or city instead, and after 6 hours it is secured there.

### Why is PvP suddenly on in my city?

Someone is carrying the dragon egg in your city, or it is placed there and not secured yet. See [Open egg](/smp/dragon-egg#open-egg).
