The dragon egg is a special item on the SMP. After the first dragon victory, it appears right on top of the exit portal in the centre of the End. Blocks that players built there disappear without dropping. You claim it through a pickup contest, then place it or pass it on. The server opens access to the End at the announced stage, see [Realms](/smp/realms#end).

## Claim the egg

The egg is shown at twice its normal size. A purple particle circle surrounds it. The display above it counts the players in the circle. When exactly one player stands inside, the circle turns green.

1. Stand alone in the circle, alive and in survival mode.
2. Stay until the **5, 4, 3, 2, 1** countdown finishes.
3. Keep one inventory slot free. You then receive the egg.

If another player joins you, the countdown stops. Once exactly one player stands alone again, the five seconds start over. SMP chat announces who claimed the egg.

| Rule                     | Value                                 |
| :----------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| Circle radius            | 5 blocks horizontally                 |
| Height for taking part   | Up to 3 blocks above or below the egg |
| Time alone in the circle | 5 seconds                             |

When the display says **Paused**, the countdown is not advancing.

## Place and pass on the egg

You can place the egg like a block, in every realm. Shortly after a server start, while camps and cities are still loading, this does not work. Try again a moment later. Using your drop key releases the egg for the pickup contest.

Anyone can right-click a placed egg, also in other players' camps and cities. It then floats above a circle again, and anyone can claim it through the normal countdown. Outsiders may not break the block.

## Open egg

The egg is **open** while someone carries it, or while it is placed and not yet secured.

- Whoever carries the egg glows. Everyone sees them, even through walls.
- While the egg is open in a camp or city, PvP is on for everyone in the whole area, whatever its PvP setting. A city's plots belong to the city. An outpost counts as its own area.
- Looting stays forbidden: chests and other containers stay protected.

### Building area around the open egg

Within **16 blocks** of an open egg in a camp or city, outsiders may also build and break blocks. Nothing drops, and there is no XP. TNT and other explosions only act inside that area and drop nothing.

Chests and other containers, the egg itself, the block beneath it and blocks something hangs on stay protected. Pistons move nothing in this area, members' pistons included.

About **5 minutes** after the egg is no longer open there and no outsiders are left, the area restores itself. What members built or broke themselves stays.

## Secure a placed egg

If you place the egg in a camp or city, it stays open there for **6 hours**. Every hour the SMP chat tells everyone the realm, a spot within 100 blocks and how long the egg stays open.

After that the egg is secured for that area. PvP there is no longer on because of the egg, and only players who may break blocks there can take it. Everyone else sees: "This dragon egg is secured. Only the people of this claim can take it now."

| Case                                       | What happens                    |
| :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------ |
| Secured egg placed again in the same area  | It stays secured.               |
| Egg placed in another camp or city         | The 6 hours start again.        |
| Egg placed in the wilderness               | It is never secured.            |
| Egg taken before the 6 hours are over      | The area has not secured it.    |

## Store the egg

The egg only fits in your inventory. You cannot put it in a chest, barrel, shulker box, bundle, ender chest or other container. Hoppers cannot transport it.

If the egg was still in a chest or barrel, it is released beside it for the pickup contest.

## Fighting for the egg

For the first dragon, PvP in the End is off until the egg appears on the exit portal. From then on, PvP is on.

The dragon egg counts as an RP reason for PvP. Whoever carries the egg, fights for it or helps its carrier may be attacked. The egg and whoever carries it must always stay reachable. Walling in, locking up or bunkering them is forbidden. More under [PvP and death](/smp/death#pvp) and in the [rules](/reference/rules).

## Protection and death

The egg does not despawn. Fire and explosions cannot destroy it.

If you die while carrying it, it does not stay in your [grave](/smp/death). It returns to the pickup contest. SMP chat names the realm and a location within **100 blocks** of the egg.

## Logging out and changing servers

If you leave the SMP while carrying the egg or go to the lobby, a new pickup contest starts at your last location. Chat names the realm and a location within 100 blocks again.

If you log out during combat, your combat-log NPC keeps the egg at first. If it dies or combat ends without your return, the egg is released. Return to your living character in time to keep it.

An unexpected server outage cannot destroy the egg either. Once the technical interruption has been detected, your ownership stays reserved for **one hour**. Return to the SMP within that window to keep the egg. Otherwise, it becomes available to claim again. Further restarts do not extend that window.

## Commands

The dragon egg needs no command of its own.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can I not claim the egg?

You must be alive, in survival mode and have a free inventory slot. Stand alone in the circle for five seconds. When the display says **Paused**, wait until the countdown resumes.

### Do I get the egg straight away when I break it?

No. Breaking the egg returns it to the pickup contest. Whoever stands alone in the circle for five seconds can then claim it.

### Can I keep the egg safe in a chest?

No. The egg only fits in your inventory. Place it in your camp or city. After 6 hours it is secured there.

### Why is PvP suddenly on in my city?

Someone carries the dragon egg in your city, or it stands there unsecured. While the egg is open, PvP is on for everyone in the whole area.
