The dragon egg is a special item on the SMP. After the first dragon victory, it appears right on top of the exit portal in the centre of the End. Blocks that players built there disappear without dropping. You claim it through a pickup contest, then place it or pass it on. The End opens later, see [Realms](/smp/realms).

## Claim the egg

The egg is shown at twice its normal size. A particle circle surrounds it. The display above it counts the players in the circle.

1. Stand alone in the circle, alive and in survival mode.
2. Stay until the **3, 2, 1** countdown finishes.
3. Keep one inventory slot free. You then receive the egg.

If another player joins you, the countdown stops. Once exactly one player stands alone again, the three seconds start over. SMP chat announces who claimed the egg.

| Rule                     | Value                                 |
| :----------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| Circle radius            | 5 blocks horizontally                 |
| Height for taking part   | Up to 3 blocks above or below the egg |
| Time alone in the circle | 3 seconds                             |

When the display says **Paused**, the countdown is not advancing.

## Place and pass on the egg

You can place the egg like a block. Breaking it makes it float above a circle again. Anyone can then claim it through the normal countdown. Right-clicking the placed egg does not teleport it.

Using your drop key also releases the egg for the pickup contest.

## Store the egg

The egg fits in your inventory, a placed chest or a barrel. Use a single click or Shift to move it between them.

You cannot put it in a shulker box, bundle, ender chest or other container. Hoppers cannot transport it.

## Protection and death

The egg does not despawn. Fire and explosions cannot destroy it.

If you die while carrying it, it does not stay in your [grave](/smp/death). It returns to the pickup contest. SMP chat names the realm and a location within **100 blocks** of the egg.

## Commands

The dragon egg needs no command of its own.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can I not claim the egg?

You must be alive, in survival mode and have a free inventory slot. Stand alone in the circle for three seconds. When the display says **Paused**, wait until the countdown resumes.

### Do I get the egg straight away when I break it?

No. Breaking the egg returns it to the pickup contest. Whoever stands alone in the circle for three seconds can then claim it.

### Can I keep it safe in my ender chest?

No. You can only use your inventory, a placed chest or a barrel.
