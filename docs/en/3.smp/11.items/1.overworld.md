You get almost all of these additional items and building mechanics the normal way in survival.

## Fried egg

Cook an egg, whether white, blue or brown:

- furnace: 10 seconds
- smoker: 5 seconds
- campfire: 30 seconds

You get no experience for it. Fried eggs stack up to 64. You can eat them, but they do not fill you up.

Eating a fried egg earns you the advancement [Sunny Side Up](/smp/advancements/overworld#sirargon).

## Throwing bricks

Hold the right mouse button with a brick in your hand. The brick charges in five stages, the action bar shows the progress as **Charging brick 1/5** up to **5/5**. Let go and it flies. The longer you charge, the further it flies.

- If it hits a creature, it deals half a heart of damage and knocks it back. You only hit players where PvP is on.
- If it hits glass or a glass pane, it breaks it. In protected areas the area's permissions apply.
- Afterwards the brick lies on the ground again. Only in lava is it gone.

## Mini blocks

At the stonecutter you turn many blocks and items into small decorative heads. Put a block or an item in and choose the mini block.

## Plants and pots

- Hold a plant and crouch on a flower pot or a decorated pot: the plant goes into the pot. This works with almost all plants.
- Two-tall plants become one-tall when you place them while crouching.
- Hold a stick and crouch on a plant placed this way to rotate it.
- At the stonecutter you turn a composter, a cauldron or a mossy cobblestone slab into half-pots. Right-click a half-pot to change the soil inside.
- Lily pads, farmland, barrels and player heads also work as pots.

## Banners

- **Flags:** Crouch-place a banner on a fence, a wall, a glass pane, iron bars, a copper rod or an end rod. Crouch next to the flag with a diamond to make it bigger. A stick flips it, a stick in your off-hand rotates it and a feather makes it wave.
- **Bedsheets:** Crouch-place a banner at the foot of a bed.

## Fences and glass panes

Fences, walls, glass panes and iron bars also connect to signs, banners, heads, rods, chains, leaves and trapdoors. If you place the block from your off-hand, it does not connect.

## Armour stands

Armour stands have arms as soon as you place them. Crouch and right-click an armour stand with an empty hand to hide or show its arms.

## Dyeing harnesses

You can re-dye harnesses.
