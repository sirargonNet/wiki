The values run from tier I to the highest tier. "Points" names the points in the category that the higher tiers need.

### Mining

| Ability      | Values per tier                    | Points |
| :----------- | :--------------------------------- | :----- |
| Vein Miner   | 4, 8, 16, 24, 32 ore blocks        | 6, 10  |
| Tunnel Borer | tier I to V, more stone types      | 6, 10  |
| Mining XP    | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP | 6, 10  |
| Mining Mana  | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana       | 6, 10  |

- Vein Miner: sneak with a pickaxe, natural ores within 32 blocks, 3 mana per extra block. No ancient debris.
- Tunnel Borer: sneak and press F with a pickaxe, up to 9 natural stone blocks in a 3 × 3 plane, aim up to 5 blocks away, 2 mana per block. Your tool loses one point of durability per block. Unbreaking lowers that as usual.

| Tier | New stone types                |
| :--- | :----------------------------- |
| I    | stone, deepslate               |
| II   | granite, diorite, andesite     |
| III  | tuff, calcite, dripstone block |
| IV   | netherrack, basalt, blackstone |
| V    | end stone                      |

Every tier keeps the stone types of the tiers before it.

### Forestry

| Ability       | Values per tier                    | Points |
| :------------ | :--------------------------------- | :----- |
| Tree Feller   | 8, 16, 40, 80, 140 logs            | 6, 10  |
| Replanting    | 1 sapling, all 4 for 2 × 2 trees   | –      |
| Leaf Fall     | one tier                           | –      |
| Forestry XP   | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP | 6, 10  |
| Forestry Mana | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana       | 6, 10  |

- Tree Feller: sneak with an axe, natural logs within 32 blocks, 2 mana per extra log. Larger trees remain partly standing.
- Replanting: after you fell a natural log, plants a sapling from your inventory in its place. For mangroves it is a mangrove propagule, for crimson and warped stems the matching fungus. Tier II plants all four saplings for 2 × 2 spruce, jungle, dark oak and pale oak trees. You need four saplings in your inventory for that.
- Leaf Fall: the natural leaves around the felled logs decay at once, like normal leaf decay. Saplings, sticks and apples drop as usual. Your tool does not wear down. Placed leaves remain.

### Farming

| Ability        | Values per tier                    | Points |
| :------------- | :--------------------------------- | :----- |
| Harvest Scythe | 3 × 3, 5 × 5, 7 × 7, 7 × 7, 9 × 9  | 6, 10  |
| Reseeding      | one tier                           | –      |
| Farming XP     | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP | 6, 10  |
| Farming Mana   | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana       | 6, 10  |

- Harvest Scythe: sneak with a hoe, 1 mana per ripe crop. From tier II also nether wart, from tier IV also pumpkins, melons, sugar cane and cocoa.
- Reseeding: replants using a seed from your inventory. This works for ripe wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots and nether wart, also without Harvest Scythe.

### Excavation

| Ability         | Values per tier                    | Points |
| :-------------- | :--------------------------------- | :----- |
| Excavator       | tier I to V, more soil types       | 6, 10  |
| Gravel Sifter   | one tier                           | –      |
| Excavation XP   | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP | 6, 10  |
| Excavation Mana | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less mana       | 6, 10  |

- Excavator: sneak with a shovel, 3 × 3 natural soil, 1 mana per block. From tier III two blocks deep.

| Tier | New soil types                 |
| :--- | :----------------------------- |
| I    | dirt, grass block, coarse dirt |
| II   | sand, red sand, gravel         |
| III  | clay, mud, rooted dirt         |
| IV   | soul sand, soul soil           |
| V    | snow block, mycelium, podzol   |

Every tier keeps the soil types of the tiers before it.

- Gravel Sifter: natural gravel dug with a shovel always drops flint, not with Silk Touch.

### Building, exploration and combat

| Ability      | Values per tier                     | Points |
| :----------- | :---------------------------------- | :----- |
| Long Arms    | +1, +2 blocks of reach              | –      |
| Sprint Boost | 10, 15 seconds of Speed II          | –      |
| Soft Fall    | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25% less fall damage | 4, 6   |
| Deep Diver   | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50% more air        | 4, 6   |
| Mob Expert   | 10, 20% more damage against mobs    | –      |
| Second Wind  | Regeneration I, II                  | –      |
| Combat XP    | 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5% more SMP XP  | 3, 5   |

- Long Arms: only works while you stand in your own territory. With the extra reach you only place and break blocks in your own territory. You cannot use chests, doors or other blocks with it. Buckets and TNT do not work with it either.
- Sprint Boost: sneak twice while sprinting, both within one second. Costs 20 mana.
- Mob Expert: does not apply against players.
- Second Wind: when damage takes you below three hearts, you get five seconds of Regeneration. Then the ability waits ten minutes. Never during player combat.

### Community, spirit and crafting

| Ability         | Values per tier                                        | Points |
| :-------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Team Aura       | 10, 20, 30% more SMP XP                                | –      |
| Campfire Rest   | one tier                                               | –      |
| Mana Storage    | 75, 100, 125, 150, 175, 200, 225, 250 total mana       | 5, 8   |
| Mana Flow       | 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 1, 1.2, 1.5, 2, 2.5 mana per second     | 5, 8   |
| Smelting Master | 10, 20, 30% more SMP XP when collecting furnace output | –      |

- Team Aura: works with at least two active players within 16 blocks.
- Campfire Rest: if you stay for ten seconds within 5 blocks of a lit campfire you placed yourself, you get Regeneration I. It is renewed every second while you stay. If you take or deal damage, the rest pauses for ten seconds.
- Mana Storage and Mana Flow: without stars you have 50 mana and refill 0.5 mana per second.

The skill tree already shows **Fishing**. You cannot unlock its stars yet.
