A camp is your own small territory. Inside it, the server protects your builds; out in the wilderness, nothing is protected. Camps only exist in the Overworld.

## Size

You can have one camp. It's free and covers four chunks, that is 32 × 32 blocks, over the full height.

## Expand your camp

You can grow your camp chunk by chunk, to at most 16 chunks, or up to 64 × 64 blocks.

1. Stand in a chunk that shares an edge with your camp.
2. In `/camp` choose **Expand**.
3. Check the preview and confirm the price.

Diagonal chunks and gaps are not possible, and new chunks follow the same distances as your camp.

Each additional chunk costs 1,000 silver more than the previous one:

| Additional chunk | Price         | Total         |
| ---------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| 1st              | 1,000 silver  | 1,000 silver  |
| 2nd              | 2,000 silver  | 3,000 silver  |
| 3rd              | 3,000 silver  | 6,000 silver  |
| 4th              | 4,000 silver  | 10,000 silver |
| 6th              | 6,000 silver  | 21,000 silver |
| 12th             | 12,000 silver | 78,000 silver |

::callout{type="info"}
**If you give up your camp, you get no silver back.**
::

## Create a camp

1. Stand where your camp should be.
2. Type `/camp` and choose **Create camp**.
3. Check the border preview.
4. Choose **Create** or **Cancel**.

You can't create a camp everywhere. If your spot doesn't work, the game tells you.

## Distances

Distances are measured horizontally, from border to border:

- to spawn: at least 512 blocks
- to other camps: no minimum, camps may border each other directly
- to cities, outposts and sold plots: at least 64 blocks

Strongholds belong to everyone. Nobody can claim anything on a stronghold or in the chunk around it, whether a camp, a city or an outpost. If your land was already there, it was released and you got back the price you paid. Built blocks stay where they are and now belong to the wilderness.

## Show borders

- `/camp view` shows the borders.
- `/camp off` hides the borders.

Only you see the borders, and they fade out once you move more than 64 blocks away.

## Uncertain and declined changes

Sometimes it's not clear whether a change went through. The game then tells you the result is uncertain. Don't start a new purchase in that case; repeat the same change with `/camp retry` instead.

If you start a different change anyway, the game settles the open one first and then says: "Your earlier unresolved territory request has been completed. Repeat your new action." or "Your earlier unresolved territory request changed nothing. Repeat your new action."

If the game declines a change, nothing has changed. The message usually gives the reason, for example "Your camp has reached its chunk limit." If the game doesn't know the reason, it still tells you clearly that the change was declined: "The change was declined for an unknown reason. Please report this to the team."

## Co-residents

`/camp add <player>` adds a co-resident, and you manage them under **Residents** in `/camp`. A camp has at most 10 co-residents.

## Rights

Under **Rights** you set what each group may do. The groups are:

- co-residents
- friends
- party members
- clan members
- visitors

The rights themselves are sorted into right groups, for example Building, Farming, Containers, Doors, Redstone, Animals and Spawners. For each right group and each single right you choose **Standard**, **Allow** or **Deny**. In a camp, Standard means denied. You'll find every right listed under [Rights & plots](/smp/cities/rights).

- If several groups match, one with **Allow** is enough. So a co-resident keeps their rights even if they are also a friend and friends are denied.
- Under **Personal rights** you give single players their own rights or take them away. This takes priority over the groups, both for **Allow** and for **Deny**.
- Under **Co-management** you add co-managers. They may edit rights and residents.
- Manage foreign graves on your land in the separate **Graves** menu.
- Your changes only apply once you save and confirm them.

## Land settings

Under **Land settings** you set TNT, PvP, environment and cosmetics. Only the owner may do this.

- **Allow TNT** and **Allow PvP:** both are off until you turn them on. TNT damage stays inside your camp. PvP across a border only works if both sides allow fights. While the [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg#open-egg) is open in your camp, PvP is on there for everyone.
- **Environment:** whether bees, sheep, villagers and snow golems work, whether fire, lava and mobs hurt your animals, whether disruptive mobs change blocks, and whether redstone, hoppers, pistons, water and lava act across your border
- **Cosmetics:** whether auras, trails, pets and fireworks are visible on your land

The defaults are listed under [Rights & plots](/smp/cities/rights#land-settings).

## Animals and golems defend

In camps, cities and plots, wolves, iron and snow golems, axolotls and frogs fight monsters, even if they belong to nobody.

Piglins, piglin brutes and hoglins are protected there. But if they attack you, you may hit them on other players' land too, and your tamed animal may then defend you against them.

## Turn your camp into a city

Choose **Found city** in `/camp`. Your camp becomes a city, and whatever you paid for camp expansions counts towards the founding price. See [Cities](/smp/cities#from-your-camp).

## Stuck

If you are stuck in someone else's camp, `/unstuck` takes you to the nearest free block after five seconds of standing still. See [Cities](/smp/cities#stuck).

## Abandon your camp

Choose **Abandon camp** in `/camp`, and the land becomes wilderness again.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/camp`: open your camp
- `/camp add <player>`: add a co-resident
- `/camp view`: show borders
- `/camp off`: hide borders
- `/camp retry`: repeat a change whose result is uncertain
- `/unstuck`: free yourself from someone else's land

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I protect my builds?

With a [camp](/smp/camps) or in a [city](/smp/cities). The wilderness has no protection.
