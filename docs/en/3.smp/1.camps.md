A camp is your own small territory in the Overworld. There the server protects your builds; the wilderness has no protection. You create it for free with `/camp`, and it covers four chunks. For silver you expand it to up to 16 chunks.

## Size

A camp covers four chunks, that is 32 × 32 blocks, over the full height. The camp is free. You have one camp.

## Expand your camp

You can grow your camp chunk by chunk. A camp has at most 16 chunks, up to 64 × 64 blocks.

1. Stand in a chunk that shares an edge with your camp.
2. In `/camp` choose **Expand**.
3. Check the preview and confirm the price.

Diagonal chunks and gaps are not possible. New chunks follow the same distances as your camp.

Each additional chunk costs 1,000 silver more than the previous one:

| Additional chunk | Price         | Total         |
| ---------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| 1st              | 1,000 silver  | 1,000 silver  |
| 2nd              | 2,000 silver  | 3,000 silver  |
| 3rd              | 3,000 silver  | 6,000 silver  |
| 4th              | 4,000 silver  | 10,000 silver |
| 6th              | 6,000 silver  | 21,000 silver |
| 12th             | 12,000 silver | 78,000 silver |

::callout{type="info"}
**If you give up your camp, you get no silver back.**
::

## Create a camp

1. Stand where your camp should be.
2. Type `/camp` and choose **Create camp**.
3. Check the border preview.
4. Choose **Create** or **Cancel**.

You cannot create a camp everywhere. The game tells you if it is not possible here.

## Distances

Measured horizontally from border to border.

- to spawn: at least 512 blocks
- to other camps: no minimum, camps may border each other directly
- to cities, outposts and sold plots: at least 64 blocks

Camps only exist in the Overworld.

## Show borders

- `/camp view` shows the borders.
- `/camp off` hides the borders.

Only you see the borders. If you move more than 64 blocks away, they hide.

If the result of a change is uncertain, repeat it with `/camp retry`.

## Co-residents

`/camp add <player>` adds a co-resident. Manage them under **Residents** in `/camp`. A camp has at most 10 co-residents.

## Rights

Under **Rights** you set what each group may do. The groups are:

- co-residents
- friends
- party members
- clan members
- visitors

Rights are sorted into groups, for example Building, Farming, Containers, Doors, Redstone, Animals and Spawners. For each group and each single right you choose **Standard**, **Allow** or **Deny**. In a camp, Standard means denied. All rights are listed under [Rights & plots](/smp/cities/rights).

- If several groups match, one with **Allow** is enough. So a co-resident keeps his rights even if he is also a friend and friends are denied.
- Under **Personal rights** you give single players their own rights or take them away. This comes before the groups, for **Allow** and for **Deny**.
- Under **Co-management** you add co-managers. They may edit rights and residents.
- Manage foreign graves on your land in the separate **Graves** menu.
- Changes only apply after saving and confirming.

## Land settings

Under **Land settings** you set TNT, PvP, environment and cosmetics. Only the owner may do this.

- **Allow TNT** and **Allow PvP:** both are off until you turn them on. TNT damage stays inside your camp. PvP across a border only works if both sides allow fights.
- **Environment:** whether bees, sheep, villagers and snow golems work, whether fire, lava and mobs hurt your animals, whether disruptive mobs change blocks, and whether redstone, hoppers, pistons, water and lava act across your border
- **Cosmetics:** whether auras, trails, pets and fireworks are visible on your land

The defaults are listed under [Rights & plots](/smp/cities/rights#land-settings).

## Animals and golems defend

In camps, cities and plots, wolves, iron and snow golems, axolotls and frogs fight monsters, even if they belong to nobody.

Piglins, piglin brutes and hoglins are protected there. But if they attack you, you may hit them on other players' land too. Your tamed animal may then defend you against them.

## Turn your camp into a city

In `/camp` choose **Found city**. Your camp becomes a city, and paid camp expansions count towards the founding price. See [Cities](/smp/cities#from-your-camp).

## Stuck

If you are stuck in someone else's camp, `/unstuck` takes you to the nearest free block after five seconds of standing still. See [Cities](/smp/cities#stuck).

## Abandon your camp

In `/camp` choose **Abandon camp**. The land becomes wilderness again.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/camp`: open your camp
- `/camp add <player>`: add a resident
- `/camp view`: show borders
- `/camp off`: hide borders
- `/camp retry`: repeat a change whose result is uncertain
- `/unstuck`: free yourself from someone else's land

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I protect my builds?

With a [camp](/smp/camps) or in a [city](/smp/cities). The wilderness has no protection.
