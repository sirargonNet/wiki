| Enchantment           | German                  | Levels | For                      | Effect                                                        | Found in                         |

| Wind Boost            | Wind Boost              | I–IV   | harnesses                | Your happy ghast flies faster                                 | enchanting table, anvil          |
| Aerial's Bane         | Nemesis der Haltlosen   | I–V    | melee weapons            | More damage against creatures in the air                      | lone citadel                     |
| Illagers Bane         | Nemesis der Illager     | I–V    | melee weapons            | 2.5 more damage per level against illagers                    | pale residence, woodland mansion |
| Spiteful              | Trotzig                 | I–III  | melee weapons            | More damage when you are low on health                        | shrines, pale residence          |
| Might                 | Macht                   | I–V    | crossbow                 | More damage with arrows                                       | lone citadel                     |
| Tri-Shot              | Dreifachschuss          | I      | bow                      | You shoot three arrows at once                                | toxic lair                       |
| Blowthrough           | Durchdringung           | I–IV   | bow                      | Arrows pass through one creature per level                    | toxic lair                       |
| Antidote              | Gegengift               | I      | chestplate               | Protects from poison and wither damage, costs durability      | toxic lair                       |
| Outreach              | Reichweite              | I–IV   | chestplate               | You reach blocks 0.5 blocks further per level                 | desert ruins                     |
