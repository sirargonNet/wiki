You also find these enchantments only as loot in the structures listed, not at the enchanting table. The structures have their own [advancements](/smp/advancements/nether).

| Enchantment   | German         | Levels | For                                 | Effect                                                                  | Found in                                |
| ------------- | -------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Ghasted       | Begeistert     | I–III  | Crossbow                            | Arrows turn into ghast fireballs                                        | Sealing Halls                           |
| Wither Coated | Verdorrung     | I–III  | Swords, spears, axes, trident, mace | Gives enemies the Wither effect at the cost of more durability          | Nether Keep, Sealing Halls, Nether Port |
| Hydro Veil    | Wasserschleier | I–IV   | Harnesses                           | Your Happy Ghast heals faster and is protected from fire and explosions | Nether Keep, Sealing Halls              |
| Swift Soar    | Flinker Flug   | I–III  | Harnesses                           | Your Happy Ghast flies faster while you sprint                          | Nether Keep, Nether Port, Sealing Halls |

- Ghasted does not go together with Multishot, Piercing, Might or Aerial's Bane.
- Wither Coated does not go together with Fire Aspect.
- Wither skeletons sometimes carry weapons with Wither Coated.
