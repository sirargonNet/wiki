## Placing

You place spawners:

- in your own camp
- on your own plot
- in a city with the right **Place spawners**

Not in the wilderness and not close to spawn.

## How they work

Spawners do not spawn mobs. They collect loot and XP in a storage. Each spawner gives half as much as a vanilla spawner.

A spawner works while its chunk is loaded and an active player is nearby:

- in the spawner's chunk or one of the 8 chunks around it
- at most 32 blocks above or below the spawner
- in Survival or Adventure mode

This can be any player; the owner does not need to be online. AFK players do not count. When the storage is full, the spawner collects nothing until you empty it.

## Stack

You stack identical spawners in one place:

- Right-click with a spawner in your hand adds one.
- Sneak and right-click adds all from your hand.

Only spawners of the same type with the same upgrades and the same unlocked colours stack. A stack holds up to 64 spawners. When you look at it, the game shows how full it is.

Every spawner in the stack adds one page of storage with 36 slots for loot and room for 10,000 XP.

Spawners with equal bought upgrades can stack even if their chosen wool colour or compactor settings differ. The existing stack keeps its settings. Picked-up items list their bought upgrades and can stack with matching spawner items.

After placing them anew, choose the wool colour and compacted drops again. Unlocks stay; only these settings return to their defaults.

## Menu

Right-click the stack to open the **Spawner stack** menu:

- **XP:** click takes XP up to the next level, shift-click takes everything.
- **Repair:** repairs items with Mending using the stored XP. Click repairs what you wear and hold, shift-click your whole inventory.
- **Loot:** take items out and filter by item.
- **Destroy drops:** delete new drops of an item right away.
- **Upgrades:** buy Looting, a compactor for suitable loot and the wool colour for sheep.

## Upgrades

Upgrades apply to the whole stack. The price is per spawner and is multiplied by the size of the stack. You see the calculation before you buy. Upgrades stay on the spawner, even when you break it.

### Compactor

The compactor stores suitable loot as blocks. It costs **1,000 Silver per spawner** in the stack.

1. Right-click your stack and open **Upgrades**.
2. Choose **Buy compactor** and confirm the total price.
3. Open **Compactor** and choose each drop you want to compact.

Every 9 items become 1 block. Remainders stay raw. You can reverse the recipe at a crafting table. Production and XP stay the same. The upgrade stays with the spawner when picked up.

| Spawner | Raw loot      | Stored block      |
| :------ | :------------ | :---------------- |
| Zombie  | iron ingots   | iron block        |
| Drowned | copper ingots | copper block      |
| Slime   | slimeballs    | slime block       |
| Witch   | redstone dust | block of redstone |

Only suitable drops appear in the menu. Bones are not turned into bone meal, and string is not turned into wool. You can keep each drop raw again without losing the upgrade.

### Wool colour

For a sheep spawner you choose under **Wool colour** which wool it collects:

- **Natural colours:** 1,000 Silver per spawner. You choose one of the natural sheep colours.
- **Own colours:** 3,000 Silver per spawner, after the natural colours. You unlock every further colour once for the stack, with dye of that colour. The menu shows how much you need.

## Breaking

- You need a pickaxe with Silk Touch.
- Normally you take one spawner, while sneaking the whole stack.
- Only the owner breaks spawners from their stack.
- You can take spawners as long as the loot fits into the remaining pages. The last spawner comes out only with empty loot and empty XP.

You can take a natural spawner from the world once as an item with Silk Touch, if you may break blocks there. Spawn eggs do not change its mob.

You cannot trade spawners.
