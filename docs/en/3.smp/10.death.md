On the SMP, PvP is allowed in the wilderness, and in camps and cities only when the owners switch it on. Killing is forbidden at spawn. When you die, your items go into a gravestone. `/graves` shows you where it is and how long it stays.

## PvP

- PvP is allowed in the wilderness.
- In camps and cities only if the owners turn it on.
- Across a territory border only if both sides allow fights.
- Killing at spawn is forbidden.

## Fighting and logging out

If you attack a player or get attacked, you are in combat for 30 seconds. During that time you cannot change servers. If you log out, your **combat-log NPC** stays behind. If it is killed, your inventory and experience drop there. So stay in game until the fight is over.

## Death message

The public death message shows how long the player lived. It counts their play time since their last death. If they set a new record, it says so:

- server record: longest life
- personal record: longest life
- personal record: shortest life

You hear a quiet thunder with every public death message. Turn off **Death thunder** in `/settings`.

## Gravestone

Whenever you die, however it happens, a gravestone appears: a head with your name and the time of death. Your items are inside.

- Every damageable item loses 10 % durability.
- XP and levels drop as usual.
- For 6 hours only you can open the gravestone.
- If a player killed you, they can usually take from it too during the first 5 minutes. Whoever is first gets the items.
- During those 6 hours you can share your graves, either with your friends or with your clan. Set it in `/settings network` under **Share graves**: **Off**, **Friends** or **Clan**, see [Settings](/guides/settings#network). Shared players may take items.
- After that, anyone can open it for 7 days. Then it disappears.

**Off** is the default. Choose either **Friends** or **Clan**, never both. There is no separate option for city members.

## Open a grave

Right-click the gravestone to open the grave:

- **Take all and equip** puts your armour on directly and the rest into your inventory.
- Click single stacks to take them.

## Your graves

`/graves` shows your graves, where they are and how long they remain.

## Graves on other players' land

Territory owners can turn off or send away other players' graves on their land. You then collect your grave via `/graves`.

## Commands

- `/graves`: your graves

## Frequently asked questions

### Where may I fight?

In the wilderness. In camps and cities only if the owners turned PvP on. Never at spawn.

### I died. Where are my items?

In your gravestone. `/graves` shows where it is. More under [Gravestone](/smp/death#gravestone).

### Why did my items disappear?

Some items despawn faster in the SMP than in vanilla. More under [Changed mechanics](/smp/mechanics#item-despawn).
