You can't fight other players everywhere on the SMP. When you die, your items stay in a grave.

## PvP

PvP is allowed in the wilderness. In camps and cities it only works if the owners turn it on, and across a territory border only if both sides allow fights. Killing at spawn is forbidden.

In the End, PvP is off for the first dragon until the dragon egg appears, and on after that. For all dragons summoned again later, PvP in the End is on the whole time. More under [dragon fight](/smp/realms#the-dragon-fight).

The [rules](/reference/rules) say every fight needs an RP reason (short for roleplay reason): something that came up in play, such as a dispute or a duel both sides agreed to. The [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg#fighting-for-the-egg) counts as an RP reason: whoever carries the egg, fights for it or helps its carrier may be attacked. While the egg is open in a camp or city, PvP is on for everyone there.

## Fighting and logging out

If you attack a player or get attacked, you're in combat for 30 seconds and can't change servers until it ends. If you log out, your **combat-log NPC** stays behind. If it is killed, your inventory and experience drop there. So stay in game until the fight is over.

## Death message

The public death message shows how long the player lived, counted as their play time since their last death. If they set a new record, the message says so:

- server record: longest life
- personal record: longest life
- personal record: shortest life

You hear a quiet rumble of thunder with every public death message. If it bothers you, turn off **Death thunder** in `/settings`.

## Gravestone

Normally, a gravestone appears after your death: a head with your name and the time of death, holding your items. Special [End rules](/smp/death#end) apply during dragon fights.

- Every damageable item loses 10 % durability.
- XP and levels drop as usual.
- For 6 hours only you can open the gravestone.
- If a player killed you, they can usually take from it as well during the first 5 minutes. Whoever is first gets the items.
- During those 6 hours you can share your graves, either with your friends or with your clan. Set it in `/settings network` under **Share graves**: **Off**, **Friends** or **Clan**, see [Settings](/guides/settings#network). Shared players may take items.
- After that, anyone can open it for 7 days. Then it disappears.

**Off** is the default. Choose either **Friends** or **Clan**, never both. There is no separate option for city members.

## Open a grave

Right-click the gravestone to open the grave:

- **Take all and equip** puts your armour on directly and the rest into your inventory.
- Click single stacks to take them.

## Your graves

`/graves` shows your graves, where they are and how long they remain.

## Graves on other players' land

Territory owners can turn off or send away other players' graves on their land. You then collect your grave through `/graves`.

## End

While a dragon lives in the End and the elytra stage is still closed, dying near the centre gives you a virtual grave. Near the centre means inside the fight area, the **300 × 300 block border square around 0/0**, at every height. That includes being killed by a player and dying in the void.

- Collect your items from anywhere by clicking your grave in `/graves`.
- Only you can open it. Your killer, friends and clan members have no access.
- Your items lose no durability when you die.
- It expires after the same time as a normal grave, but never becomes public.
- The [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg) doesn't go into the grave. Instead it can be claimed again.

Normal grave rules apply when no dragon is alive, you die outside that area or the elytra stage has opened.

## Commands

- `/graves`: your graves

## Frequently asked questions

### Where may I fight?

In the wilderness. In camps and cities only if the owners turned PvP on. Never at spawn.

### I died. Where are my items?

In your gravestone. `/graves` shows where it is. More under [Gravestone](/smp/death#gravestone).

### Why did my items disappear?

Some items despawn faster in the SMP than in vanilla. More under [Changed mechanics](/smp/mechanics#item-despawn).
