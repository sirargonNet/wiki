With `/image` you turn a picture from the internet into one or more maps that you hang on a wall in item frames. That way logos, signs or art end up in your builds without rebuilding them block by block. Large pictures are spread over up to 3 × 3 maps that you hang up side by side.

## How to make a picture

1. Upload the picture somewhere it gets a public address starting with `http://` or `https://`, and copy the link to the image file.
2. On the SMP, type `/image <address>`. For a bigger picture, add the size, for example `/image https://… 2x2` for two maps across and two down.
3. For your first picture you confirm the rules for pictures (see below).
4. A window shows the size, the price, how many pictures you can still make today and the address. **Buy** loads the picture.
5. The maps go into your inventory. If it is full, the rest land on the ground in front of you.

Hang the maps in item frames: the first map goes top left, then continue row by row. Every map says which part it is. With invisible item frames the picture hangs on the wall without a visible frame.

The whole picture is fitted onto the maps and nothing is cut off. If its aspect ratio does not match exactly, a transparent strip stays at the edge. Maps only know certain colours, so photos look a little grainier than the original.

## Values

| Rule             | Value                    |
| :--------------- | :----------------------- |
| Price            | 1,000 Silver per map     |
| Size             | 1 × 1 to 3 × 3 maps      |
| Without a size   | 1 map                    |
| Pictures per day | 5, reset at midnight     |
| Formats          | PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP  |
| File size        | up to 8 MB               |
| Image size       | up to 4096 × 4096 pixels |

So a 3 × 3 picture costs 9,000 Silver. A GIF shows up as a still image.

## Rules for pictures

Before your first picture you confirm in a window that you hold the rights to it and that it shows nothing sexualised, discriminating or otherwise forbidden. If these rules change, the server asks you again with your next picture.

Every picture stays linked to you: each map says who made it, and the server stores the address, the time and the maps. You can use pictures straight away. If one breaks the rules, the team can remove it and punish you, and its maps then turn blank everywhere. To report a picture that breaks the rules, use `/report` against the player named on the map, as described under [Report a player](/getting-started/help#report-a-player).

## Handing on and copying

You can give picture maps away or hand them to others. They cannot be copied: the cartography table, the crafting table and the crafter make nothing from a picture map. You cannot offer maps on the market.

## Commands

- `/image <address>`: makes a picture on one map.
- `/image <address> <width>x<height>`: makes a picture on several maps, up to `3x3`.

`<…>` is a placeholder: type the address or number without the brackets.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is my address refused?

The address has to lead straight to a public image file and start with `http://` or `https://`. Addresses in your home network or on unusual ports do not work. A link to a page that only embeds the picture usually fails too: open the picture on its own and copy that address.

### Do I get my Silver back if the picture fails?

If the picture does not load or is not a supported format, nothing is charged at all. You only pay once the picture is ready.

### I paid but got no maps. What now?

Now and then a picture is finished only after the server stopped waiting for it, or you leave the game before the maps arrive. You still paid only once, and the maps come on their own: usually after about a minute, at the latest the next time you join the SMP. So don't buy the picture again straight away.

### Why is my picture map suddenly blank?

The team removed the picture because it breaks the rules. Right after a server restart a map can also stay blank for a moment until the server has loaded its picture.
