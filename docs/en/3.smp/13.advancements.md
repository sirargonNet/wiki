On top of the vanilla advancements, the SMP has extra ones, and you earn almost all of them the normal way in survival. The additional [items](/smp/items) and [enchantments](/smp/enchantments) have pages of their own.

## Congratulations

When someone reaches an advancement on the SMP, it shows up in chat. Write **gz** or **w** in chat within 60 seconds and you get 5 network XP. **gratz**, **grats** and **glückwunsch** count too, but your message has to be that one word and nothing else. `/gz` types `gz` for you.

- You get no XP for your own success.
- Your congratulation counts once per advancement.
- Only a limited number of congratulations per day earn you XP.
- If you get no XP, chat tells you privately why.

If someone dies and the death message is public, you can pay respects with **f** or **o7** within 60 seconds. That earns you 1 network XP, and daily limits apply here too.

The announcement is a single chat line with a star and the player's name in their rank colour. The star is green, or purple for challenges. Hover over the advancement's name to read its description. Click **gz** to congratulate on exactly that advancement. If you type `gz` yourself, it goes to the newest open success.

## Not obtainable

Since [villagers](/smp/mechanics#villagers) don't trade on the SMP, you can't get advancements that need trades with cartographers or librarians. These include:

- **Networking**
- **Call for Adventure**
- **Charter to All**
- **Bookworm**
- the librarians' books
