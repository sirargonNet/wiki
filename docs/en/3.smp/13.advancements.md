Besides the vanilla advancements, the SMP has more advancements. You get almost all of them the normal way in survival. The additional [items](/smp/items) and [enchantments](/smp/enchantments) have their own pages.

## Congratulations

When someone reaches an advancement on the SMP, it shows in chat. Then write **gz** or **w** in chat within 60 seconds. **gratz**, **grats** and **glückwunsch** count too. The message must consist of that word alone. You get 5 network XP for it.

- Your own success gives no XP.
- Your congratulation counts once per advancement.
- Only a limited number of paid congratulations count per day.
- If there is no XP, the chat tells you privately why.

If someone dies and the death message is public, pay respects with **f** or **o7** within 60 seconds. That gives 1 network XP. Daily limits apply here too.

The announcement uses one chat line with a star and the name in the player’s rank colour. Hover to read its description. Click **gz** to congratulate that exact advancement. Typing `gz` yourself refers to the newest open success.

## Not obtainable

Villagers do not trade on the SMP, see [Villagers](/smp/mechanics#villagers). That is why you cannot get advancements that need trades with cartographers or librarians. For example:

- **Networking**
- **Call for Adventure**
- **Charter to All**
- **Bookworm**
- the librarians' books
