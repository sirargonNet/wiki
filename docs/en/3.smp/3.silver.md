Silver is the SMP currency. You need it for territories, trading, spawners and skills. Your silver is shown in the sidebar.

How you get silver:

- play actively
- complete [quests](/smp/quests)
- reach [milestones](/smp/milestones)
- sell and trade, see [Trading](/smp/trading)

AFK does not count.
