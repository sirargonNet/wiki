Silver is the SMP currency. You need it for territories, trading, spawners and skills. You earn it by playing actively, from [quests](/smp/quests) and [milestones](/smp/milestones), and by [trading](/smp/silver#trading). AFK does not count. Your silver is shown in the sidebar.

## Trading

### Offers

`/offers` opens the **Offers** market. `/ah`, `/auctionhouse` and `/market` work too.

- Filter by **Category**, **Search** and **Sort order**.
- Under **Status** you see all offers or only your own: running, sold, bought and expired.
- **Switch to buy orders** takes you to the [buy orders](#buy-orders).
- Your **Pickup** holds what you bought, got back or had delivered and what did not fit into your inventory.

Every item shows its **Market value** in its description when the market knows it. For bundles and shulker boxes, **Contents market value** is separate from the container’s value. If a price is missing or an item cannot be traded, the known contents value says **at least**. This is not a complete total.

#### Selling

1. Hold the item in your main hand.
2. Type `/offers sell` or click **Sell item in hand**. `/offers sell <price> <duration>` does it directly, with the duration `12h`, `24h`, `48h` or `7d`.
3. Enter the price for the whole stack and choose the duration: 12 hours, 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days.
4. Check the preview with price, offer fee and your proceeds and confirm.

A new offer first sits in the showcase for a short time. Only then can someone buy it.

- If your price differs strongly from recent sales, the game warns you.
- If an item is worth a lot, its offer needs a minimum price. The game tells you what it is.
- You can withdraw an offer at any time. The item comes back, the offer fee does not.
- You can have at most 15 running offers at once.

#### Buying

Click an offer and confirm. The silver is charged when you buy. The item goes into your inventory or, if there is no room, into your Pickup.

### Buy orders

With a buy order you look for an item.

- `/orders` shows all buy orders.
- `/orders create` creates a buy order.

How to create a buy order:

1. Choose the item from a list. It does not have to be in your inventory.
2. Optionally set minimum enchantment levels.
3. Enter the quantity, the price per item and the duration.

`/orders create <item> <quantity> <price> [duration] [enchantments]` does it directly. Write enchantments like this: `sharpness=5`.

The silver for the whole quantity is reserved when you create the order. Others supply your order from their main hand, also in parts. The items go into your Pickup.

- If an item is worth a lot, its buy order needs a minimum price per item.
- You can withdraw a buy order at any time. Unused silver comes back, the order fee does not.
- You can have at most 10 open buy orders at once.

### Fees

You only pay fees when you create something:

| Fee       | Amount                                    |
| :-------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| Offer fee | 5% of the price, at least 50 Silver       |
| Order fee | 5% of the total price, at least 50 Silver |

- If your price is below the market value, the fee follows the market value.
- You pay the order fee on top of the reserve.
- Fees are never refunded, not when you withdraw and not when something expires.
- Selling and supplying cost nothing. You get the full price.

### Trades

Trade directly with a player, without a fee. Trades are for handing things to a certain player, not the market. Using the market for that can get you blocked from the market automatically.

1. Type `/trade <player>`. You must be in the same world and at most 8 blocks apart.
2. The other player accepts the request within 20 seconds, for example with `/trade accept`. `/trade decline` declines it.
3. Click stacks in your inventory to offer them. You can also offer silver.
4. Click **Ready**. When both are ready, the trade completes after 3 seconds.

`/trade` opens a running trade.

### Not tradeable

You cannot trade filled containers, soulbound items or custom plugin items such as spawners.

Trading is paused during maintenance.

The team can send a spawner to your **Pickup** as compensation or a reward. Open `/offers`, choose **Pickup** and collect it as an item. Spawners still cannot be traded.

When the market refuses an item, it explains why. Renamed items cannot be sold there, but can be exchanged with `/trade`. Empty filled containers first.

### Market data on the website

On the SMP page of the [statistics](/getting-started/statistics), open **Market data**. In the **SMP market** you search items, choose categories, sort and page through the entries.

- Categories: Blocks, Equipment, Food, Materials and Other
- Sort: Most sales, Item name, Highest unit price or Price change
- **Period:** 7 days, 30 days or All. 7 days is the default.

The data comes from sold listings and shows Silver prices. Direct trades with `/trade` do not count. Buyer and seller names are not shown.

Select an entry for its details. The view shows the price line, the range of unit prices and the quantity sold, plus a table per day. It also shows aggregated **Active offers** and **Active buy orders**.

- **Average unit price** is weighted by the quantity sold. The range runs from the lowest to the highest unit price of a single sale.
- **24h change** compares the average unit price of the last 24 hours with the 24 hours before, as a percentage. A dash is shown when either period has no sale.
- With 7 and 30 days there is one point per day. When **All** covers more than 90 days, the history shows one point per week.
- Periods without a sale remain gaps in the price line.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/offers`: offers, also `/ah`, `/auctionhouse` or `/market`
- `/offers sell [price [12h|24h|48h|7d]]`: offer the item in your hand
- `/orders`: buy orders
- `/orders create [item quantity price [duration] [enchantments]]`: create a buy order
- `/trade <player>`: request a trade
- `/trade accept`: accept a trade request
- `/trade decline`: decline a trade request
- `/trade`: open a running trade

Open your **Pickup** from the `/offers` menu.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get silver?

Play actively, complete quests, reach milestones and trade. More under [Silver & trading](/smp/silver), [Quests](/smp/quests) and [Milestones](/smp/milestones).

### How do I trade with a player?

Stand at most 8 blocks from them and type `/trade <player>`. More under [Trades](/smp/silver#trades).
