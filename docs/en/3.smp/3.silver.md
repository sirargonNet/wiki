Silver is the SMP currency. You need it for territories, trading, spawners and skills, and you earn it by playing actively, completing [quests](/smp/quests), reaching [milestones](/smp/milestones) and [trading](/smp/silver#trading). Time spent AFK doesn't count. Your silver balance is shown in the sidebar.

## Trading

### Offers

`/offers` opens the **Offers** market, and so do `/ah`, `/auctionhouse` and `/market`.

- You can filter by **Category**, **Search** and **Sort order**.
- Under **Status** you can see all offers or only your own: running, sold, bought and expired.
- **Switch to buy orders** takes you to the [buy orders](#buy-orders).
- Your **Pickup** holds what you bought, got back or had delivered and what did not fit into your inventory.

If the market knows an item, its description shows the **Market value**. For bundles and shulker boxes, the **Contents market value** is listed separately from the container’s own value. If a price is missing or an item can't be traded, the known contents value says **at least**, so the number isn't a complete total.

#### Selling

1. Hold the item in your main hand.
2. Type `/offers sell` or click **Sell item in hand**. To do it in one go, use `/offers sell <price> <duration>` with `12h`, `24h`, `48h` or `7d` as the duration.
3. Enter the price for the whole stack and choose the duration: 12 hours, 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days.
4. Check the price, offer fee and your proceeds in the preview, then confirm.

A new offer sits in the showcase for a short while first, and only then can someone buy it.

- If your price is far off recent sales, the game warns you.
- If an item is worth a lot, its offer needs a minimum price, and the game tells you what it is.
- You can withdraw an offer at any time. You get the item back, but not the offer fee.
- You can have at most 15 running offers at once.

#### Buying

Click an offer and confirm. The silver is charged when you buy, and the item goes into your inventory or, if there's no room, into your Pickup.

### Buy orders

A buy order is how you ask for an item you're looking for.

- `/orders` shows all buy orders.
- `/orders create` creates a buy order.

How to create a buy order:

1. Choose the item from a list. It does not have to be in your inventory.
2. Optionally set minimum enchantment levels.
3. Enter the quantity, the price per item and the duration.

To do it in one go, use `/orders create <item> <quantity> <price> [duration] [enchantments]`. Enchantments are written like this: `sharpness=5`.

As soon as you create the order, the silver for the whole quantity is reserved. Other players fill it from their main hand, and they can also deliver only part of it. The items go into your Pickup.

- If an item is worth a lot, its buy order needs a minimum price per item.
- You can withdraw a buy order at any time. Any unused silver comes back to you, but the order fee doesn't.
- You can have at most 10 open buy orders at once.

### Fees

You only pay a fee when you create an offer or a buy order:

| Fee       | Amount                                    |
| :-------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| Offer fee | 5% of the price, at least 50 Silver       |
| Order fee | 5% of the total price, at least 50 Silver |

- If your price is below the market value, the fee follows the market value.
- You pay the order fee on top of the reserved silver.
- Fees are never refunded, not even when you withdraw something or it expires.
- Selling and supplying cost nothing, so you get the full price.

### Trades

A trade lets you swap items directly with another player, with no fee. If you want to hand something to a particular player, use a trade, not the market. Using the market for that can get you blocked from the market automatically.

1. Type `/trade <player>`. You both need to be in the same world and no more than 8 blocks apart.
2. The other player has 20 seconds to accept the request, for example with `/trade accept`, or can turn it down with `/trade decline`.
3. Click the stacks in your inventory that you want to offer. You can offer silver too.
4. Click **Ready**. Once you are both ready, the trade completes after 3 seconds.

Use `/trade` to open a running trade.

### Not tradeable

You cannot trade filled containers, soulbound items or custom plugin items such as spawners.

Trading is paused during maintenance.

The team can put a spawner in your **Pickup** as compensation or a reward. Open `/offers`, choose **Pickup** and take it out as an item. That doesn't make spawners tradeable.

When the market refuses an item, it tells you why. You can't sell renamed items there, but you can swap them directly with `/trade`. Empty a filled container before you list it.

### Market data on the website

On the SMP page of the [statistics](/getting-started/statistics), open **Market data**. In the **SMP market** you can search for items, pick categories, sort and page through the entries.

- Categories: Blocks, Equipment, Food, Materials and Other
- Sort: Most sales, Item name, Highest unit price or Price change
- **Period:** 7 days, 30 days or All. 7 days is the default.

The data comes from sold listings and shows prices in Silver. Direct trades with `/trade` aren't included, and buyer and seller names aren't shown.

Select an entry to see its details: the price line, the range of unit prices and the quantity sold, plus a table per day. You also see a summary of **Active offers** and **Active buy orders**.

- **Average unit price** is weighted by the quantity sold. The range runs from the lowest to the highest unit price of a single sale.
- **24h change** compares the average unit price of the last 24 hours with the 24 hours before, as a percentage. A dash is shown when either period has no sale.
- With 7 and 30 days there is one point per day. When **All** covers more than 90 days, the history shows one point per week.
- Periods without a sale remain gaps in the price line.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/offers`: offers, also `/ah`, `/auctionhouse` or `/market`
- `/offers sell [price [12h|24h|48h|7d]]`: offer the item in your hand
- `/orders`: buy orders
- `/orders create [item quantity price [duration] [enchantments]]`: create a buy order
- `/trade <player>`: request a trade
- `/trade accept`: accept a trade request
- `/trade decline`: decline a trade request
- `/trade`: open a running trade

Open your **Pickup** from the `/offers` menu.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get silver?

By playing actively, completing quests, reaching milestones and trading. More under [Silver & trading](/smp/silver), [Quests](/smp/quests) and [Milestones](/smp/milestones).

### How do I trade with a player?

Stand no more than 8 blocks away from them and type `/trade <player>`. More under [Trades](/smp/silver#trades).
