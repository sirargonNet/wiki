The **World Quest** is a shared task in the SMP, on top of your personal quests. Every player donates raw materials for the same goals in `/quests` and earns tickets for them. If every goal is full by the deadline, everyone with enough tickets gets a share of the silver pot. Donated items are never returned.

Unlike the weekly quests, the World Quest doesn't follow a weekly cycle; it runs until a fixed deadline. The current task, **Rail network**, lasts about a week and ends on 14 November 2026 at 00:00 German time, which is when Friday, 13 November, is over.

## How to take part

1. Gather the raw materials needed by the active task.
2. Open `/quests`. The **World Quest** sits directly below your personal quests.
3. Check the required amounts, your contribution, the minimum tickets and the deadline.
4. Click **Donate matching materials**.
5. The game takes every matching item that is still needed from your inventory and uses it up. Your contribution then shows as tickets.

**Put away anything you want to keep first.** The button donates more than the item in your hand. Anything beyond the amount still needed, and materials whose goals are already full, stay in your inventory. Renamed items and special plugin items aren't accepted.

You donate the raw materials, not the finished crafting target. Every raw material goal has to be full by the deadline, because extra of one material can't make up for another.

## Donate other forms

The game accepts some raw materials in other forms too. The menu lists them under "Also accepted".

| Raw material | Also accepted                                              |
| :----------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| Iron ingot   | block of iron and block of raw iron (9 each), raw iron (1) |
| Gold ingot   | block of gold and block of raw gold (9 each), raw gold (1) |
| Redstone     | block of redstone (9)                                      |
| Oak log      | every other log and stem, stripped too (1)                 |
| Coal         | block of coal (9), charcoal (1)                            |

The number in brackets is how many items one piece counts as. The game only takes whole blocks, so if only 3 redstone are still needed, for example, a redstone block stays in your inventory.

## Reward

- You only succeed together if every raw material goal is reached in time.
- To earn Silver, you need at least the displayed number of tickets. The menu shows **Minimum contribution reached** or how many tickets you still need.
- 40% of the pool is split equally among eligible players.
- 60% is split by those players' tickets.
- You can only donate up to your contribution limit. The menu shows how many tickets you can still contribute, and once you reach the limit, the button is greyed out.
- After success, your share is credited automatically as Silver, so there's nothing to collect. A **World Quest reward** message names the amount, your rank and your share of all tickets.
- If you contributed with several SMP profiles, your share is split between those profiles by their tickets.

On success, a global booster also starts for everyone. The menu shows you beforehand which one it is and how long it lasts. When it starts, chat announces **World Quest completed!** with the booster and its duration.

If the task fails, there is no reward. **Donated items are never returned, even on failure.** After completion, the menu shows your share and, where different, the current profile's share.

## Progress

The menu shows:

- shared progress and your contribution for each material
- your tickets and your share of all tickets
- your rank
- your estimated reward on success
- the **Top contributors**, up to 5 players
- the time left once less than 8 days remain

After each donation, a chat line tells you how many items you donated for how many tickets. Progress announcements in chat also name the players who contributed, and at 100% chat announces success.

## Website ranking

The SMP page of the [statistics](/getting-started/statistics) has a **World Quests** section. It shows each task with its amount, date range, progress, status (**Running**, **Completed** or **Failed**) and the shared tickets.

Choose **Show more players** to open a task's **Contributions**. Players are ranked by their tickets, 10 per page. **Show fewer players** collapses the list again.

Hidden players keep their place, but without a name or head, and their contribution shows as **Hidden**. Because the shared ticket count includes every contribution, you can still roughly tell how much hidden players contributed.

The ranking is for bragging rights and adds no reward.
