The **World Quest** is a shared task in the SMP, on top of your personal quests. It does not follow the weekly cycle of the weekly quests; it runs until a fixed deadline. The current task, **Rail network**, runs for about a week and ends on 14 November 2026 at 00:00 German time, which is the end of Friday, 13 November. Every player donates raw materials for the same goals in `/quests` and earns tickets for them. If every goal is full by the deadline, everyone with enough tickets gets a share of the silver pot. Donated items are never returned.

## How to take part

1. Gather the raw materials needed by the active task.
2. Open `/quests`. The **World Quest** sits directly below your personal quests.
3. Check the required amounts, your contribution, minimum tickets and deadline.
4. Click **Donate matching materials**.
5. The game takes all matching items still needed from your inventory. They are consumed. Your contribution is counted as tickets.

**Put away anything you want to keep first.** The button donates more than the item in your hand. Excess amounts and materials whose goals are already full stay in your inventory. Renamed items and special plugin items are not accepted.

Donate raw materials, not the finished crafting target. Every raw material goal must be full by the deadline. Extra amounts of one material cannot replace another.

## Donate other forms

The game accepts some raw materials in other forms too. The menu lists them under "Also accepted".

| Raw material | Also accepted                                              |
| :----------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| Iron ingot   | block of iron and block of raw iron (9 each), raw iron (1) |
| Gold ingot   | block of gold and block of raw gold (9 each), raw gold (1) |
| Redstone     | block of redstone (9)                                      |
| Oak log      | every other log and stem, stripped too (1)                 |
| Coal         | block of coal (9), charcoal (1)                            |

The number in brackets is how many items one piece counts as. The game takes whole blocks only. If just 3 redstone are still needed, for example, a redstone block stays in your inventory.

## Reward

- You succeed together only if every raw material goal is reached in time.
- To earn Silver, you need at least the displayed number of tickets. The menu shows **Minimum contribution reached** or how many tickets you still need.
- 40% of the pool is split equally among eligible players.
- 60% is split by those players' tickets.
- You can donate only up to your contribution limit. The menu shows how many tickets you can still contribute. Once you reach the limit, the button is greyed out.
- After success, your share is credited automatically as Silver. You do not need to collect it. A **World Quest reward** message names the amount, your rank and your share of all tickets.
- If you contributed with several SMP profiles, your share is split between those profiles by their tickets.

On success a global booster also starts for everyone. The menu shows beforehand which one and for how long. When it starts, chat announces **World Quest completed!** with the booster and its duration.

If the task fails, there is no reward. **Donated items are never returned, even on failure.** After completion, the menu shows your share and, where different, the current profile's share.

## Progress

The menu shows:

- shared progress and your contribution for each material
- your tickets and your share of all tickets
- your rank
- your estimated reward on success
- the **Top contributors**, up to 5 players
- the time left once less than 8 days remain

After each donation a chat line tells you how many items you donated for how many tickets. Progress announcements in chat also show the players who contributed. At 100%, chat announces success.

## Website ranking

The SMP page of the [statistics](/getting-started/statistics) has a **World Quests** section. It shows each task with its amount, date range, progress, status (**Running**, **Completed** or **Failed**) and the shared tickets.

Choose **Show more players** to open a task's **Contributions**. Players are ranked by their tickets, 10 per page. **Show fewer players** collapses the list again.

Hidden players keep their place without a name or head. Their contribution shows as **Hidden**. The shared ticket count includes every contribution, so it roughly shows how much hidden players contributed.

The ranking is for recognition. It adds no reward.
