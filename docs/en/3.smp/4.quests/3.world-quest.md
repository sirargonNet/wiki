The **World Quest** is a shared weekly task in the SMP. Everyone works towards the same raw material goals. It comes on top of your personal daily and weekly quests.

## How to take part

1. Gather the raw materials needed by the active task.
2. Open `/quests`. The **World Quest** sits directly below your personal quests.
3. Check the required amounts, your contribution, minimum tickets and deadline.
4. Click **Donate matching materials**.
5. The game takes all matching items still needed from your inventory. They are consumed. Your contribution is counted as tickets.

**Put away anything you want to keep first.** The button donates more than the item in your hand. Excess amounts and materials whose goals are already full stay in your inventory. Renamed items and special plugin items are not accepted.

Donate raw materials, not the finished crafting target. Every raw material goal must be full by the deadline. Extra amounts of one material cannot replace another.

## Donate blocks

You can donate these blocks too:

| Block             | Counts as       | Tickets per block |
| :---------------- | :-------------- | ----------------: |
| Block of redstone | 9 redstone dust |                18 |
| Block of quartz   | 4 nether quartz |                16 |

The game donates matching blocks first, then raw items. It takes whole blocks only. If just 3 redstone are still needed, for example, a redstone block stays in your inventory.

## Reward

- You succeed together only if every raw material goal is reached in time.
- To earn Silver, you need at least the displayed number of tickets.
- 20% of the pool is split equally among eligible players.
- 80% is split by those players’ tickets.
- This task has no fixed maximum per player. The full pool follows the distribution.
- After success, your share is credited automatically as Silver. You do not need to collect it.
- If you contributed with several SMP profiles, your share is split between those profiles by their tickets.

If the task fails, there is no reward. **Donated items are never returned, even on failure.** After completion, the menu shows your share and, where different, the current profile’s share.

## Progress

The menu shows shared progress, your contribution for each material and your tickets. Progress announcements in chat also show the players who contributed. At 100%, chat announces success.
