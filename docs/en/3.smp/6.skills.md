SMP XP raises your level and gives you skill points. You spend them in the skill tree to unlock abilities. How you collect XP and how much a level costs is under [Levels and XP](/smp/skills#levels-and-xp).

## Skill tree

In the skill tree you unlock stars. Every star costs one skill point. A star needs the tier before it. Higher tiers also need points in the same category, counted over all abilities of that category:

- abilities with 3 tiers: from tier III the first number of points
- abilities with 5 tiers: from tier III the first number of points, from tier IV the second
- abilities with 8 tiers: from tier IV the first number of points, from tier VI the second

Once every skill of an area is maxed, you unlock its [mastery](/smp/skill-tree#mastery) there. Its Diamond tiers cost 3, 4 and 5 skill points.

**Show as constellation** and **Show in columns** switch the view. The same choice is in `/settings`.

All abilities and their values are under [Skill tree](/smp/skill-tree).

## Mana

Abilities for larger areas only trigger while sneaking and cost mana. Some fishing abilities cost mana too. Your mana is shown above the hotbar. `/skills` shows it too.

- Without [Mana Storage](/smp/skill-tree#community-spirit-and-crafting) you have 50 mana. Every tier adds 25 mana, up to 250.
- Without Mana Flow your mana refills by 0.5 per second. Mana Flow makes it faster, up to 2.5 per second. Spirit Mastery refills even faster while you stand still and fills to 125% while meditating.
- Your mana is full again when you join the server, leave it or die.

| Ability        | Mana              |
| :------------- | :---------------- |
| Vein Miner     | 3 per extra block |
| Tunnel Borer   | 2 per block       |
| Tree Feller    | 2 per extra log   |
| Harvest Scythe | 1 per ripe crop   |
| Excavator      | 1 per block       |
| Sprint Boost   | 20                |
| Fast Bite      | 2 per cast        |
| Double Catch   | 3 per hit         |
| Cook Catch     | 1 per cooked fish |

The mana stars of a category lower these costs by 5% per tier. You pay the mana before each block. If a block is protected, you get its mana back. When your mana runs out, the ability stops. The blocks broken so far stay broken.

## Good to know

- Abilities only work in Survival mode.
- Abilities are off while you fight other players.
- Territory rights apply to every single block. When an ability reaches a protected block, it stops there.
- No ability breaks blocks you placed yourself.
- When your tool has 5 durability or less left, you only break single blocks. That way it does not break.
- An ability runs as long as you sneak and hold the same tool in the same slot. If you let go or switch, it stops. This also applies to Tree Feller.
- Every block must be at most 32 blocks away from you and loaded. Otherwise the ability stops.
- If you trigger an ability while another one runs, the new one waits. At most 4 wait at the same time. If you stop the running one yourself, the waiting ones are dropped.
- Use `/skills toggle` to turn off an ability that gets in your way.
- When you open `/skills`, the game checks your [milestones](/smp/milestones) right away, for example **First steps**.

## Resetting

`/skills reset` or **Reset skills** in `/skills` resets all stars, Diamond tiers included. You keep your XP and skill points. You need at least one star for it.

Your first reset is free. After that, every further reset costs 2,000 Silver more: 2,000, 4,000, 6,000 Silver and so on. You see the price before you confirm.

When we change the skills or the skill tree, you may reset once for free. After that, the prices start from the beginning. The game tells you in chat. The free reset does not expire. Until you reset, your chosen skills remain.

The fishing area and masteries changed the skill tree. That is why everyone who had already played on the SMP has a free reset.

## Levels and XP

### SMP XP

You get SMP XP when you do things in the SMP. The action bar shows you how much you are getting.

#### How it is counted

- While you play, the server counts your actions.
- They are booked every few seconds. That is why your XP arrives shortly after the action.
- AFK time does not count. Time on staff duty and during maintenance does not count either.
- If the server has a short hiccup, your actions are not lost. They are booked later.

#### Actions

| Action                                     | How much it gives                                                              |
| :----------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| playing actively                           | a little over time                                                             |
| mining ores                                | the rarer the ore, the more; diamond, emerald and ancient debris give the most |
| mining stone, dirt, sand and gravel        | a little per block                                                             |
| cutting down trees                         | some per log                                                                   |
| harvesting crops                           | a little per ripe crop; pumpkins and melons a bit more                         |
| fishing                                    | some per fish; treasure more, junk nothing                                     |
| defeating mobs                             | the stronger the mob, the more; elder guardian, warden and wither a lot        |
| taking items out of a furnace              | depends on the item's furnace XP                                               |
| discovering new chunks and biomes          | once per chunk and biome                                                       |
| being the first to enter a structure       | once per structure; the bigger, the more                                       |
| reaching an advancement for the first time | depends on the advancement type                                                |

- Blocks you placed yourself and unripe crops do not count. AFK does not count.
- If you farm the same kind of mob for a long time or smelt a lot at once, you get less XP for a while. Discovering new chunks also has a limit per hour.
- Mobs from trial chamber spawners give less XP.
- A mob goes to the player who dealt it the most damage. It must die shortly after your last hit. Fire, fall damage and other mobs do not count. This also applies to kill quests.
- An SMP XP booster from your [inventory](/guides/inventory) doubles your SMP XP.

### Levels

With enough SMP XP your SMP level rises. From level 2 you get one skill point per level. There is no maximum level.

From level 21, every level costs 20 XP more than the one before: level 21 costs 300 XP, level 22 costs 320 XP and so on.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

Instead of `/skills` you can use `/skill`, instead of `/skilltree` also `/skillstree`.

- `/skills`: abilities and mana
- `/skills tree` or `/skilltree`: skill tree
- `/skills toggle <ability>`: turn an ability on or off
- `/skills reset`: reset stars
