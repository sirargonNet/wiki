SMP XP raises your level and earns you skill points, which you spend in the skill tree to unlock abilities. How you collect XP and what a level costs is explained under [Levels and XP](/smp/skills#levels-and-xp).

## Skill tree

In the skill tree you unlock stars, and every star costs one skill point. You can only buy a star once you have the tier before it. Higher tiers also need points in the same category, added up across all of its abilities:

- abilities with 3 tiers: from tier III the first number of points
- abilities with 5 tiers: from tier III the first number of points, from tier IV the second
- abilities with 8 tiers: from tier IV the first number of points, from tier VI the second

Once every skill in an area is at its highest tier, you unlock its [mastery](/smp/skill-tree#mastery) there. Its Diamond tiers cost 3, 4 and 5 skill points.

Hover over a star you haven't bought yet and its tooltip shows how many skill points that tier costs. This works even while the star is locked or you're short of points.

**Show as constellation** and **Show in columns** switch the view. You can set the same thing in `/settings`.

The [Nether skill tree](/smp/skill-tree#nether) uses the same skill points, and its gathering, fighting and roaming areas have their own masteries.

All abilities and their values are under [Skill tree](/smp/skill-tree).

## Mana

Abilities for larger areas only trigger while you sneak, and they cost mana. Fishing abilities and [Chorus Harvest in the End](/smp/skill-tree#end), on the other hand, cost no mana. You can see your mana above the hotbar and in `/skills`.

- Without [Mana Storage](/smp/skill-tree#playing-together-spirit-and-crafting) you have 50 mana. Every tier adds 25 mana, up to 250.
- Without Mana Flow your mana refills by 0.5 per second; Mana Flow speeds that up to as much as 2.5 per second. Spirit Mastery refills it even faster while you stand still and fills it to 125% while you meditate.
- Your mana is full again when you join the server, leave it or die.

| Ability        | Mana              |
| :------------- | :---------------- |
| Vein Miner     | 3 per extra block |
| Tunnel Borer   | 2 per block       |
| Tree Feller    | 2 per extra log   |
| Harvest Scythe | 0.5 per ripe crop |
| Excavator      | 1 per block       |
| Sprint Boost   | 20                |

The mana stars of a category lower these costs by 5% per tier. Quick Shovel replaces Excavation Mana, so Excavator always costs 1 mana per block. Breeder does not lower mana costs, and Harvest Scythe costs a fixed 0.5 mana per crop.

You pay the mana before each block, and if a block is protected, you get its mana back. When your mana runs out, the ability stops, but whatever it has broken so far stays broken.

## Good to know

- Abilities only work in Survival mode.
- Abilities are off while you fight other players.
- Territory rights apply to every single block. When an ability reaches a protected block, it stops there.
- No ability breaks blocks you placed yourself.
- When your tool has 5 durability or less left, you only break single blocks, so the tool doesn't break.
- An ability runs as long as you sneak and hold the same tool in the same slot. If you let go or switch, it stops. Tree Feller is the one exception, see below.
- Every block has to be loaded and at most 32 blocks away from you, otherwise the ability stops.
- If you trigger an ability while another one runs, the new one waits its turn. At most 4 can wait at the same time. If you stop the running one yourself, the waiting ones are dropped.
- Use `/skills toggle` to turn off an ability that gets in your way.
- When you open `/skills`, the game checks your [milestones](/smp/milestones) in the background, for example **First steps**. Until that's done, you see the last known state. Progress from your actions still counts straight away.

### Tree Feller

With Tree Feller, sneaking with an axe only starts the felling. Once it has started, it keeps going even if you let go. If you switch tools, it only carries on with a tool that wears down when breaking blocks: axe, pickaxe, shovel, hoe, sword, shears, trident or mace. That tool then takes the wear.

A felling that has started ends when:

- the tree is down
- your tool has only 5 durability left
- you hold something else after a switch, for example armour, elytra, a shield, bow, crossbow, fishing rod, flint and steel, brush, a block, food or nothing
- your mana does not cover the next log
- the next log is protected, more than 32 blocks away or not loaded
- you switch the ability off or get into a fight with another player
- you change worlds or log out

## Resetting

`/skills reset` or **Reset skills** in `/skills` resets all your stars, Diamond tiers included. You keep your XP and skill points. You can only reset if you have at least one star.

Your first reset is free. After that, each one costs 2,000 Silver more than the last: 2,000, 4,000, 6,000 Silver and so on. You see the price before you confirm.

When we change the skills or the skill tree, the game lets you know in chat and you may reset once for free. After that, the prices start from the beginning again. The free reset doesn't expire, and your chosen skills stay as they are until you reset.

The End tree and the revised skills changed the skill tree together. If you had already played on the SMP before this change, you get one free reset for the combined update, covering the Overworld, Nether and End changes at once. Your chosen stars stay until you use it.

## Levels and XP

### SMP XP

You get SMP XP for doing things in the SMP, and the action bar shows you how much is coming in.

#### How it is counted

- While you play, the server keeps track of your actions and credits them every few seconds. That's why your XP arrives shortly after the action.
- AFK time doesn't count, and neither does time on staff duty or during maintenance.
- If the server has a short hiccup, your actions aren't lost; they're credited later.

#### Actions

| Action                                     | How much it gives                                                              |
| :----------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| playing actively                           | a little over time                                                             |
| mining ores                                | the rarer the ore, the more; diamond, emerald and ancient debris give the most |
| mining stone, dirt, sand and gravel        | a little per block                                                             |
| cutting down trees                         | some per log                                                                   |
| harvesting crops                           | a little per ripe crop; pumpkins and melons a bit more                         |
| fishing                                    | some per fish; treasure more, junk nothing                                     |
| defeating mobs                             | the stronger the mob, the more; elder guardian, warden and wither a lot        |
| taking items out of a furnace              | depends on the item's furnace XP                                               |
| discovering new chunks and biomes          | once per chunk and biome                                                       |
| being the first to enter a structure       | once per structure; the bigger, the more                                       |
| reaching an advancement for the first time | depends on the advancement type                                                |

- Blocks you placed yourself and unripe crops don't count.
- If you farm the same kind of mob for a long time or smelt a lot at once, you get less XP for a while. Discovering new chunks also has a limit per hour.
- Mobs from trial chamber spawners give less XP.
- A mob goes to the player who dealt it the most damage, and it has to die shortly after your last hit. Fire, fall damage and other mobs don't count. The same goes for kill quests.
- An SMP XP booster from your [inventory](/guides/inventory) doubles your SMP XP.

#### Dragon victories

When the End event's dragon fight is won, everyone in the End at that moment earns SMP XP, even if they never damaged the dragon.

Later dragon victories give less SMP XP, and only once per account and calendar day (Berlin time). Staff on duty and erased accounts earn no XP.

How the fight works and what rules apply is explained under [Realms](/smp/realms#end).

### Levels

With enough SMP XP your SMP level goes up, and every level gives you one skill point, including level 1. So your free points are your SMP level minus the cost of the stars you've bought. There is no maximum level.

From level 21, every level costs 20 XP more than the one before: level 21 costs 300 XP, level 22 costs 320 XP and so on.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

You can also type `/skill` instead of `/skills`, and `/skillstree` instead of `/skilltree`.

- `/skills`: abilities and mana
- `/skills tree` or `/skilltree`: skill tree
- `/skills toggle <ability>`: turn an ability on or off
- `/skills reset`: reset stars

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get skill points?

You get one skill point for every SMP level, including level 1. Every realm shares this pool. More under [Levels and XP](/smp/skills#levels-and-xp).
