The End is the SMP's third realm. The first time, it opens with the End event and its dragon, and after the first victory the dragon egg appears.

## Opening and Elytra stage

- The server opens access to the End at an announced stage, see [Realms](/smp/realms#end).
- You can glide with elytra anywhere, but until the [Elytra stage](/smp/realms#elytra-stage) rocket boosts in flight only work in the End.
- Xaero's minimap, world map and your own waypoints stay locked in the End until the Elytra stage, see [Map mods](/reference/mods#map-mods).

End portals in strongholds belong to everyone. Strongholds cannot be claimed, and nobody may block or occupy the portal, its entrances or corridors. Killing others at the End portal again and again is spawn camping, see [Camps](/smp/camps).

## Dragon fight

::callout{type="warning" title="Careful at the End event"}
If you don't want to die, stay away from the End event. Once the egg appears, it is often unclear who is fighting for it and who isn't, and players who aren't taking part can get hit too.

Your clan or city can drag you in as well: if your allies fight for the egg, you can become a target as their ally. You are only safe if you avoid the centre of the End around the exit portal after the victory, because that is where the egg appears.
::

- The End event's dragon is stronger than in vanilla and fights differently.
- For the first dragon, a border limits the End to 300 × 300 blocks around 0/0, and PvP stays off until the [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg) appears. After that PvP is on, and the border goes away with the victory. For every dragon summoned later, PvP is on the whole time. More under [Dragon fight](/smp/realms#the-dragon-fight).
- While a dragon is alive and the Elytra stage has not opened yet, special [grave rules](/smp/death#end) apply near the centre.
- The victory earns [SMP XP](/smp/skills#dragon-victories).

## Dragon egg

After the first dragon victory, whoever stands alone in the circle around the egg for five seconds gets it. The egg is an [RP reason](/smp/death#pvp) for PvP: whoever carries it, fights for it or helps its carrier may be attacked. Its carrier glows, and while the egg is carried or sits unsecured in a camp or city, PvP is on for everyone there. All the rules are under [Dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg).

## Quests, milestones and skills

The End's content unlocks the first time you play actively there:

- [End quests](/smp/quests#end): one hard daily quest and one hard weekly quest
- [End milestones](/smp/milestones#end): six one-time goals with silver
- [End skill tree](/smp/skill-tree#end): gathering, combat, travel and masteries

## Items, enchantments and advancements

- [Items](/smp/items#end): the End structures add no items of their own with special abilities.
- [Enchantments](/smp/enchantments#end): Gravity, Wax Wings and Aerial's Bane
- [Advancements](/smp/advancements#end): End lighthouse, End ship, End castle and Nullscape
