This page sums up everything about the End. The details are on the linked pages.

## Opening and Elytra stage

- The server opens access to the End at its announced stage. More under [Realms](/smp/realms#end).
- You can glide with elytra everywhere. Rocket boosts in flight only work in the End until the [Elytra stage](/smp/realms#elytra-stage).
- Xaero's minimap, world map and your own waypoints stay locked in the End until the Elytra stage. More under [Map mods](/reference/mods#map-mods).

## Dragon fight

::callout{type="warning" title="Careful at the End event"}
If you do not want to die, stay away from the End event. For the first dragon, PvP is off until the dragon egg appears on the exit portal. From then on, PvP is on. After that it is often unclear who is going for the egg and who is not. Players who are not fighting for the egg can get hit too.

Your clan or city can also make you a target. If your allies fight for the egg, you can become a target yourself as their ally. To stay safe, keep away from the centre of the End after the victory, that is from the area around the exit portal where the egg appears.
::

- The End event's dragon is stronger and fights differently than in vanilla.
- For the first dragon, a border limits the End to 300 × 300 blocks around 0/0. It disappears with the victory. More under [Realms](/smp/realms#the-dragon-fight).
- For the first dragon, PvP in the End is off until the [dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg) appears. From then on, PvP is on. For all dragons summoned again later, PvP is on the whole time. More under [Realms](/smp/realms#the-dragon-fight).
- While a dragon lives and the Elytra stage is still closed, special [grave rules](/smp/death#end) apply near the centre.
- The victory gives [SMP XP](/smp/skills#dragon-victories).

## Dragon egg

After the first dragon victory, the dragon egg appears on the exit portal. You get it through a pickup contest. The egg counts as an RP reason for PvP: whoever carries it, fights for it or helps its carrier may be attacked. Its carrier glows, and where it is open, PvP is on for everyone in camps and towns. All rules are under [Dragon egg](/smp/dragon-egg).

## Quests, milestones and skills

You unlock the End's content once you play actively there for the first time.

- [End quests](/smp/quests#end): one hard daily quest and one hard weekly quest
- [End milestones](/smp/milestones#end): six one-time goals with silver
- [End skill tree](/smp/skill-tree#end): gathering, combat, travel and masteries

## Items, enchantments and advancements

- [Items](/smp/items#end): the End structures add no items of their own with special abilities.
- [Enchantments](/smp/enchantments#end): Gravity, Wax Wings and Aerial's Bane
- [Advancements](/smp/advancements#end): End lighthouse, End ship, End castle and Nullscape
