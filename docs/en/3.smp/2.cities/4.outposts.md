An outpost is a territory of your city that does not border the city.

- `/city outpost` opens the outposts.
- Whoever has the **Manage outposts** permission creates new ones.

## Size and costs

- An outpost covers at most 4 chunks.
- A city has at most 2 outposts.
- Each outpost costs 25,000 silver from the treasury.
- Its chunks count towards the city's chunk limit and upkeep.

## Distances

Measured horizontally from border to border.

- to spawn: at least 512 blocks
- to cities, including your own: at least 384 blocks
- to other outposts: at least 384 blocks
- to sold plots: at least 384 blocks
- to camps: at least 64 blocks

## Dimensions

Outposts also work in the Nether and the End once they are open. There they are the only kind of territory. In the End an outpost must be at least 1000 blocks from the centre.
