An outpost is an area of your city in the Overworld or the Nether that does not border the city. You create one with `/city outpost` or under **Land management**. The city treasury pays for them. Your [tier](/smp/cities/tiers) decides how many you may have.

## Create and expand

Creating one needs the right **Manage outposts**.

1. Stand where the outpost should be.
2. Under **Land management**, choose **Create outpost**, or type `/city outpost`.
3. A new outpost covers exactly 2 × 2 chunks: your chunk and the chunks east, south and south-east of it. The preview shows them.
4. Check the price and confirm.

After that you expand the outpost one chunk at a time. This needs the right **Expand city**. Stand in a chunk that shares an edge with the outpost and choose **Add current chunk** under **Land management**.

| Realm     | New outpost (4 chunks) | Each further chunk |
| :-------- | ---------------------: | -----------------: |
| Overworld |          80,000 Silver |      20,000 Silver |
| Nether    |          96,000 Silver |      24,000 Silver |

It is paid from the city treasury. Every outpost is bought on its own. There is no upkeep.

## How many

How many outposts your city may have depends on its [tier](/smp/cities/tiers) and the realm:

| Tier | Name       | Overworld | Nether | Chunks per outpost |
| ---: | :--------- | --------: | -----: | -----------------: |
|    1 | Clearing   |         0 |      0 |                  – |
|    2 | Hamlet     |         1 |      0 |                  4 |
|    3 | Village    |         1 |      0 |                  4 |
|    4 | Small Town |         2 |      1 |                  4 |
|    5 | Town       |         2 |      1 |                  8 |
|    6 | City       |         3 |      1 |                  8 |
|    7 | Major City |         4 |      2 |                  8 |
|    8 | Metropolis |         4 |      2 |                  8 |

Outpost chunks count towards the city's chunk limit.

The End opens only after the release.

## Release

Stand in the chunk you want to give up. Under **Land management**, **Release chunk** gives it up. This needs the right **Manage outposts**.

- "No refund; new land costs the full price again."
- Land and spawners in the chunk are then unprotected in the wilderness.
- "A chunk containing a plot cannot be released."
- The rest of the outpost must still be connected afterwards.
- If you release the last chunk, the outpost is removed: "Final chunk: the outpost is removed." You confirm this separately.

## Distances

Distances are measured horizontally from border to border.

- to spawn: at least 512 blocks
- to cities, including your own: at least 384 blocks
- to other outposts: at least 384 blocks
- to sold plots: at least 384 blocks
- to camps: at least 64 blocks

## Nether

Outposts also work in the Nether. There they are the only kind of territory.

## Older cities

Cities that have not [migrated](/smp/cities#claims-20) to the tier system yet keep the old rules: at most 4 chunks per outpost and the old price of 25,000 Silver per new outpost. Their outpost chunks cannot be released one by one.

## Commands

- `/city outpost`: open the city menu and show the preview for a new outpost where you stand
