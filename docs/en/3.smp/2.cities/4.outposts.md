An outpost is an area of your city in the Overworld or the Nether that does not border the city. You create one with `/city outpost` or under **Land management**, and your [tier](/smp/cities/tiers) decides how many you may have.

## Create and expand

Creating one needs the right **Manage outposts**.

1. Stand where you want the outpost to be.
2. Under **Land management**, choose **Create outpost**, or type `/city outpost`.
3. The preview shows you the chunks. A new outpost covers exactly 2 × 2 chunks: your chunk and the ones east, south and south-east of it.
4. Check the price and confirm.

After that, you can expand the outpost one chunk at a time, which needs the right **Expand city**. Stand in a chunk that shares an edge with the outpost and choose **Add current chunk** under **Land management**.

| Realm     | New outpost (4 chunks) | Each further chunk |
| :-------- | ---------------------: | -----------------: |
| Overworld |          80,000 Silver |      20,000 Silver |
| Nether    |          96,000 Silver |      24,000 Silver |

The city treasury pays, and every outpost is bought on its own. There is no upkeep.

## How many

How many outposts your city may have depends on its [tier](/smp/cities/tiers) and the realm:

| Tier | Name       | Overworld | Nether | Chunks per outpost |
| ---: | :--------- | --------: | -----: | -----------------: |
|    1 | Clearing   |         0 |      0 |                  – |
|    2 | Hamlet     |         1 |      0 |                  4 |
|    3 | Village    |         1 |      0 |                  4 |
|    4 | Small Town |         2 |      1 |                  4 |
|    5 | Town       |         2 |      1 |                  8 |
|    6 | City       |         3 |      1 |                  8 |
|    7 | Major City |         4 |      2 |                  8 |
|    8 | Metropolis |         4 |      2 |                  8 |

Outpost chunks count towards the city's chunk limit.

The End opens only after the release.

## Release

To give up a chunk, stand in it and choose **Release chunk** under **Land management**. This needs the right **Manage outposts**.

- "No refund; new land costs the full price again."
- Land and spawners in the chunk are then left unprotected in the wilderness.
- "A chunk containing a plot cannot be released."
- The rest of the outpost must still be connected afterwards.
- If you release the last chunk, the outpost is removed: "Final chunk: the outpost is removed." You confirm this separately.

## Distances

An outpost has to keep these distances, measured horizontally from border to border:

- to spawn: at least 512 blocks
- to cities, including your own: at least 384 blocks
- to other outposts: at least 384 blocks
- to sold plots: at least 384 blocks
- to camps: at least 64 blocks

Strongholds belong to everyone. Nothing can be claimed on a stronghold or the chunk around it, whether camp, city or outpost. If your land was already there, it was released and the price you paid was refunded. Built blocks stay standing and become part of the wilderness.

## Nether

Outposts also work in the Nether, where they are in fact the only kind of territory.

## Older cities

Cities that have not [migrated](/smp/cities#claims-20) to the tier system yet keep the old rules: at most 4 chunks per outpost and the old price of 25,000 Silver per new outpost. Their outpost chunks cannot be released one by one.

## Commands

- `/city outpost`: open the city menu and show the preview for a new outpost where you stand
