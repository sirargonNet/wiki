Every city has a form: Dominion, Republic or City council. The form decides what your head is called and how your city votes. On top of the fixed ranks you can create your own, and your [tier](/smp/cities/tiers) decides how many.

## City forms

Here is how the three forms differ. Only the head can change the form, using **Change city form**.

| Form         | Head's title  | How the head takes office                                |
| :----------- | :------------ | :------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dominion     | Ruler         | founder, transfer or succession                          |
| Republic     | Mayor         | elected by the citizens                                  |
| City council | Council chair | council election, then chair election inside the council |

## Fixed ranks

Every city has these fixed ranks:

| Rank       | What it may do from the start                                                                                                                                                             |
| :--------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Head       | everything. The menu shows the title of the city form.                                                                                                                                    |
| Councillor | city council only: everything a resident may, plus manage plots, invite, remove and restrict residents, manage outposts, expand the city, set TNT and PvP and view the treasury history   |
| Resident   | build, break, harvest, use containers, doors, redstone, stations, beds, decoration, animals and vehicles; deposit, buy and rent plots, vote and deliver to the tier project. No spawners. |
| Guest      | nothing                                                                                                                                                                                   |

- The rights of the head and councillors cannot be changed: "This office has fixed permissions".
- You set the rights of residents and guests under **City ranks**. These ranks can't be renamed, deleted or moved, though.
- Guests never get the rights for graves or the tier project.
- Only the head may **Dissolve city**. That right can't be given to any rank.

## Custom ranks

How many custom ranks your city may have depends on its [tier](/smp/cities/tiers): 5 as a Clearing, 14 as a Metropolis. The fixed ranks don't count towards this.

To manage custom ranks, you need the right **Manage ranks**:

- **Create rank:** enter a name. A new custom rank starts without any permissions.
- **Rename rank** and **Delete rank:** anyone in a deleted rank becomes a Resident.
- **Move rank up** and **Move rank down:** the order matters for [succession](#dominion).
- **Assign rank:** pick a member under **Residents** and give them a rank.

Councillors only take office through an election, and the head only changes by transfer, election or succession. You can, however, also give a member the Guest rank.

The **Residents** list shows each member's city rank: custom ranks by their name, fixed ranks in your language, and the head with the title of the city form. A restricted member has "restricted" in front of their rank, and that label stays visible even with long rank names.

## Rights

The available rights are listed under [Rights & plots](/smp/cities/rights). With the right **Restrict residents** you can also restrict individual residents. They then become **Restricted residents**:

- They only have the rights of guests.
- They cannot vote and never move up as head.

The head can't be restricted.

## Elections

Under **Government and elections**, you set the term, the re-election and the days until voting rights in **Election rules**. This needs the right **Manage election rules**, which only the head has at the start.

| Rule                   | At the start | Allowed       |
| :--------------------- | -----------: | :------------ |
| Days before voting     |            7 | 0 to 365 days |
| Term in days           |      28 days | 4 to 365 days |
| Re-election after days |       3 days | 1 to 7 days   |

You may vote if you have the right **Vote in elections**, are not restricted and have been a member long enough. The same right lets you start an election with **Open election**. Members who can't vote yet are left off the ballot, but the election still starts. When a term ends, no election starts by itself.

An election runs for one day, and the menu shows when it ends. If it ends in a tie, a caretaker leads the city. After the days set in **Re-election after days**, the re-election then starts by itself. The caretaker can't change everything, for example neither the city form nor the election rules.

### City council

In a city council the city elects a council with several seats.

1. In **Elect council**, every eligible voter casts one vote.
2. The players with the most votes become councillors. On a tie, whoever has been a member longer wins.
3. Then **Elect chair** opens. Only the councillors see this election and vote.
4. The winner becomes head with the title Council chair. Here too, a tie goes to whoever has been a member longer.

You set the number of seats under **Election rules** in **Council seats**: 3, 5, 7 or 9 seats. It starts at 5, and a change applies from the next council election.

When the city switches to a city council, the councillor rank appears, and the first council election starts by itself. When it leaves the city council, councillors become residents again.

### Dominion

A dominion has no elections. Instead, the head hands leadership over to another resident with **Transfer headship**. This only works in a dominion.

**Uprising:** the citizens can overthrow their head.

1. An eligible voter chooses **Start uprising**.
2. Eligible voters choose **Sign uprising**, including whoever started it. **Withdraw signature** takes your signature back.
3. The game explains: "The uprising lasts 3 mode days. With at least 3 signatures and two thirds of eligible voters, the city becomes a republic with a new election."

If the uprising succeeds, the old head becomes a resident, and a caretaker leads the city until the election is decided. If it fails: "After a failed uprising, wait 14 mode days before starting another." You cannot sign in a frozen city.

**Succession:** if the head of a dominion has not been active for a long time, an active member takes over automatically. They come from the highest custom rank that has an active member; if there are several, whoever has been a member longest. If no custom rank has an active member, the longest-serving resident follows. Guests and restricted residents never move up.

## Commands

- `/city`: open the city menu. Ranks are under **City ranks**, elections under **Government and elections**.
