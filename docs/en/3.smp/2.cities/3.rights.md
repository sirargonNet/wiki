Rights decide who may do what on your land. They apply in cities, on plots and in [camps](/smp/camps), including cities that have not migrated to the tier system yet. The land settings also control TNT, PvP, environment and cosmetics, and further down you'll find how to buy, rent and manage a plot.

## Right groups

Rights are sorted into groups. The **Change the whole group** switch flips a whole group at once, and under **Fine rights** you set each right on its own. The button shows whether all, some or no rights are allowed.

| Group          | Rights                                                                           |
| :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Building       | Place blocks, Break blocks, Light fires, Water/lava buckets                      |
| Farming        | Harvest ripe crops, Sow and plant, Bonemeal and soil                             |
| Containers     | View containers, Take and insert, Furnaces, Brewing stands, Hoppers and droppers |
| Doors          | Doors, Trapdoors, Fence gates                                                    |
| Redstone       | Buttons and levers, Plates and tripwire, Configure redstone                      |
| Stations       | Anvils, Enchanting tables, Workstations, Lecterns                                |
| Living         | Beds, Bells and noteblocks                                                       |
| Decoration     | Item frames, Armour stands, Paintings, Flowerpots, Edit signs                    |
| Animals        | Feed and breed, Kill animals, Ride animals, Leash animals, Shear and milk        |
| Movement       | Place vehicles, Use vehicles, Throw ender pearls                                 |
| Spawners       | Place spawners, Use spawners                                                     |
| Graves         | Manage graves                                                                    |
| Administration | cities only, see below                                                           |

**Administration** covers: Create plots, Set plot prices, Repossess plots, Edit parcel rights, Invite residents, Remove residents, Restrict residents, Deposit into treasury, Withdraw from treasury, View treasury history, Manage ranks, Manage outposts, Expand city, Configure TNT and PvP, Manage election rules, Manage diplomacy, Buy and rent plots, Vote in elections and Deliver to tier project.

## In the city

In a city, you switch rights on or off for the city ranks. The available ranks are listed under [Government & ranks](/smp/cities/government).

### City hard locks

Under **Management** and **City hard locks**, the city can lock single rights for everyone. This needs the right **Edit parcel rights**.

An enabled lock blocks the right across the whole city, including personal plots and their owners. Even the head is bound by locks. A plot can't allow a locked right either: "Blocked by the city; a plot cannot allow this right."

### City zone

**Create city zone** creates a plot that the city owns itself and that can't be sold. This needs the right **Create plots**.

## On plots and in camps

On your plot and in your camp you choose **Standard**, **Allow** or **Deny** for each group and each right.

| Group         | Applies to                     |
| :------------ | :----------------------------- |
| Co-residents  | plot and camp                  |
| Friends       | plot and camp                  |
| Party members | plot and camp                  |
| Clan members  | plot and camp                  |
| Citizens      | plot only: members of the city |
| Visitors      | plot and camp                  |

- In a camp, **Standard** means denied. On a plot, Standard means the rights of the city rank apply, which for strangers is the Guest rank.
- You can't give out administration rights or graves here. You manage other players' graves on your land in the separate **Graves** menu instead.
- Plots and camps from before version 0.24.0 kept their old rights.

### Single players

Under **Personal rights**, use **Add personal rights** and enter a player's name to give them their own rights or take them away.

### Co-management

Under **Co-management**, **Add co-manager** adds a player, who may then edit rights and residents. Camps you co-manage are listed under **Managed camps**.

### What comes first?

The game sums it up like this: "City lock, person, role, city rank: the first setting wins. With several roles, one Allow is enough." Step by step:

1. A city lock always denies.
2. Next comes what you set for the single player, and that goes for **Allow** as much as for **Deny**.
3. Then the group, for example Friends. If several groups match, one with **Allow** is enough. Groups on **Standard** don't count here.
4. The city rank comes last.

## Land settings

Under **Land settings** you set TNT, PvP, environment and cosmetics. In a city this needs the right **Configure TNT and PvP**. On a plot and in a camp only the owner may do this.

### TNT and PvP

- **Allow TNT:** damage stays within this territory.
- **Allow PvP:** both sides must allow combat.

Both are off until you turn them on.

### Environment

Under **Environment** you set what animals, mobs and technology may do on your land.

| Group                    | Rule                      | What it controls                               | At the start |
| :----------------------- | :------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------- | :----------- |
| Animals & villagers      | Bees                      | Pollination, hives and honey                   | on           |
| Animals & villagers      | Sheep                     | Eating grass                                   | on           |
| Animals & villagers      | Villagers                 | Harvesting and planting                        | on           |
| Animals & villagers      | Snow golems               | Placing snow trails                            | on           |
| Animals & villagers      | Fire damage               | Fire, burning and lava hurt animals and golems | on           |
| Animals & villagers      | Mob attacks               | Hits and projectiles from mobs on animals      | on           |
| Disruptive mobs          | Endermen                  | Picking up and placing blocks                  | off          |
| Disruptive mobs          | Zombies                   | Breaking doors                                 | off          |
| Disruptive mobs          | Ravagers                  | Destroying crops                               | off          |
| Disruptive mobs          | Wither                    | Destroying blocks                              | off          |
| Disruptive mobs          | Mob explosions            | Creepers, wither skulls and ghasts             | off          |
| Technical transfers      | Redstone & observers      | Signals across boundaries                      | on           |
| Technical transfers      | Hoppers                   | Hoppers and hopper minecarts across boundaries | on           |

| Fluids across boundaries | Lava                      | Flowing across boundaries                      | off          |

- Anything crossing a boundary needs both sides to agree. The wilderness always agrees.
- On a plot, **Standard** inherits the city's setting.
- **Fire damage** and **Mob attacks** apply to animals, villagers and golems, for example in an iron farm. To protect your animals, turn them off.
- For **Mob attacks**, the area the animal stands in decides.
- These switches don't affect explosions, and players still need the **Kill animals** right.

### Cosmetics

Under **Cosmetics** you choose which visible effects are allowed on your land:

- Auras
- Trails
- Pets
- Fireworks

Everything is on until you turn it off, and the setting applies to members and visitors alike. A plot has its own setting, independent of the city. There's more about effects under [Account](/guides/inventory#effects).

## Plots

Cities divide their land into plots, which they can sell or rent out.

### Find a plot

- `/city plots` shows your city's free plots.
- A hologram on site shows the size and price.
- Right-click the hologram to open the purchase menu.

### Buy or rent

- **Buy:** the plot is yours. The city may neither rebuild it nor give it to someone else. You pay no rent, fees or levies for a bought plot.
- **Rent:** you pay every week.

You need the matching permission in the city to buy or rent.

### Return a plot

**Return plot** gives your bought or rented plot back to the city. You don't get any silver back for it.

### Manage your plot

On your plot you decide who may do what: for groups such as friends or citizens of the city, and for single players. You can also add co-managers. How all of this works is explained under [On plots and in camps](/smp/cities/rights#on-plots-and-in-camps).

- If you leave a right on **Standard**, the rights of the city rank apply.
- City hard locks always apply, to you as the owner too.
- Under **Land settings** you set TNT, PvP, environment and cosmetics for your plot.

### Create plots for the city

There are four plot rights: **Create plots**, **Set plot prices**, **Repossess plots** and **Edit parcel rights**. With them the city creates plots, changes their shape and offers them for sale or rent. The offer hologram can be moved.

- **End lease:** needs **Repossess plots**. This only works once the agreed end is reached or the rent has been overdue for 14 days. Time while the city is frozen doesn't count.
- **Buy back for city:** if an owner offers their bought plot for sale, the city can buy it back with silver from the treasury. You need the rights **Repossess plots** and **Withdraw from treasury**.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/plot`: open the plot you stand in; otherwise your city's free plots
- `/city plot`: same as `/plot`
- `/plot add <player>`: add a resident
- `/plot view`: show borders
- `/plot off`: hide borders
- `/plot retry`: repeat a change whose result is uncertain
- `/city plots`: your city's free plots
