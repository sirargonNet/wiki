An outpost is an area of your city that does not border the city.

- `/city outpost` opens the outposts.
- Anyone with the right **Manage outposts** creates new ones, expands them and releases chunks.

## Create and expand

1. Stand where the outpost should be.
2. Choose **Create outpost**. A new outpost covers exactly 2 × 2 chunks around you.
3. Check the price and confirm.

After that you expand the outpost one chunk at a time.

| Realm     | New outpost (4 chunks) | Each further chunk |
| :-------- | ---------------------: | -----------------: |
| Overworld |          80,000 Silver |      20,000 Silver |
| Nether    |          96,000 Silver |      24,000 Silver |

It is paid from the city treasury. Every outpost is bought on its own. There is no upkeep.

## How many

How many outposts your city may have depends on its [tier](/smp/cities/tiers) and the realm:

| Tier | Name       | Overworld | Nether | Chunks per outpost |
| ---: | :--------- | --------: | -----: | -----------------: |
|    1 | Clearing   |         0 |      0 |                  – |
|    2 | Hamlet     |         1 |      0 |                  4 |
|    3 | Village    |         1 |      0 |                  4 |
|    4 | Small Town |         2 |      1 |                  4 |
|    5 | Town       |         2 |      1 |                  8 |
|    6 | City       |         3 |      1 |                  8 |
|    7 | Major City |         4 |      2 |                  8 |
|    8 | Metropolis |         4 |      2 |                  8 |

Outpost chunks count towards the city's chunk limit.

From tier 6, outposts in the End are added. The End opens only after the release.

## Release

Under **Land management**, **Release chunk** gives up an outpost chunk. There is no refund. If you release the last chunk, the outpost is removed: "Final chunk: the outpost is removed."

## Distances

Distances are measured horizontally from border to border.

- to spawn: at least 512 blocks
- to cities, including your own: at least 384 blocks
- to other outposts: at least 384 blocks
- to sold plots: at least 384 blocks
- to camps: at least 64 blocks

## Realms

Outposts also work in the Nether. There they are the only kind of territory.

## Older cities

Cities that have not [migrated](/smp/cities/claims-2) to the tier system yet keep the old rules: at most 4 chunks per outpost and the old outpost prices. Their outpost chunks cannot be released one by one.
