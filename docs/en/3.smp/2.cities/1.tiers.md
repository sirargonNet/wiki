Every city rises through eight tiers, from Clearing to Metropolis. To rise, you collect **city hours** through your play time and deliver a **Tier project** together. Each tier brings more chunks, more [outposts](/smp/cities/outposts) and more custom [ranks](/smp/cities/government). A city never drops a tier.

## How your city rises

1. You collect **city hours**. They come from your members' active play time on the SMP, wherever they play.
2. Once the next tier's threshold is reached, its **Tier project** opens.
3. Together you deliver the materials for a building.
4. When everything is delivered, the city rises right away. The whole server hears about it: "{city} reached tier {tier} by completing its city project."

- Only time while someone is a member counts. If a member leaves the city, their city hours stay with the city.
- AFK time does not count.
- While the city is frozen, the project pauses.
- Projects have no deadline.
- If you already have enough city hours for the tier after next, its project opens right after you rise.

The city menu shows your tier as "Tier {tier} · {name}" and your progress as "City hours: {hours} / {goal}".

A **Metropolis** also needs at least 15 members, both to open and to complete the project. It requires the End. The End opens only after the release.

### What counts

| Material           | What you can deliver                                                                                                  |
| :----------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wood               | every log and stem, stripped too (1 each)                                                                             |
| Wool               | wool of any colour (1 each)                                                                                           |
| Crops              | wheat, carrot, potato, beetroot, melon slice, pumpkin, nether wart, cocoa beans (1 each); hay bale and melon (9 each) |
| Iron, Gold, Copper | ingot and raw metal (1 each); block and raw block (9 each)                                                            |
| Emeralds, Diamonds | the item (1); the block (9)                                                                                           |
| Quartz             | nether quartz (1); block of quartz (4)                                                                                |

For the other materials only that exact item counts.

### Deliver

1. Open `/city`, then **Management** and **Tier project**.
2. Check what is still missing.
3. Click **Deliver**. The game takes matching materials from your inventory, only as many as are still needed.

- Delivering needs the right **Deliver to tier project**. Residents and every higher rank have it from the start. Guests never get it.
- The game does not accept renamed items, filled containers or special plugin items.
- **Contributions** shows who delivered how much: "{n} materials delivered".
- Projects pay no Silver. The reward is the new tier.

If you carry nothing suitable, the game says: "You carry no matching materials that are still needed."
