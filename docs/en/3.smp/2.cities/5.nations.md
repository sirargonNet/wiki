In a nation, several cities band together, while an alliance is a pact between cities. Both are paused for now.

::callout{type="warning" title="Paused"}
Nations and alliances will come back reworked. The game says: "Nations and alliances are paused and will return with Claims 2.1."

- `/nation` and `/alliance` still open, but nothing you do inside them works. Elections in nations are on hold too.
- Rights from nations and alliances do not apply at the moment.
- Nations keep their data and treasury and pay no upkeep.

The sections below show how nations and alliances worked before the pause.
::

## Nations

There are two kinds of nation:

- **Empire**
- **Federation**

`/nation` opens your nation.

### Found

To found one, both city heads must agree, and you need network level 15 and 250,000 silver.

### In the nation

- The nation has a capital and its own treasury.
- It assigns nation ranks and names a successor.
- Cities are invited, join or leave the nation.

### Nation standards

The nation sets standards, for example for TNT and PvP. A city can reject them, but then it loses its vote in the nation.

## Alliances

`/alliance` opens your alliance. Cities are invited, join or leave it. Both sides must agree to found one.

## Diplomacy

Under **Diplomacy** you manage consent, membership and permissions between cities.

## Commands

- `/nation`: open your nation
- `/nation view`: show borders
- `/nation off`: hide borders
- `/nation retry`: repeat a change whose result is uncertain
- `/alliance`: open your alliance
- `/alliance view`: show borders
- `/alliance off`: hide borders
- `/alliance retry`: repeat a change whose result is uncertain
