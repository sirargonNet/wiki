## Nations

Several cities form a nation. There are two kinds:

- **Empire**
- **Federation**

`/nation` opens your nation.

### Found

Both city heads must agree. Founding needs network level 15 and 250,000 silver. The nation pays 100 silver upkeep from its treasury every week.

### In the nation

- The nation has a capital and its own treasury.
- It assigns nation ranks and names a successor.
- Cities are invited, join or leave the nation.

### Nation standards

The nation sets standards, for example for TNT and PvP. A city can reject them. It then loses its vote in the nation.

## Alliances

An alliance is a pact between cities.

`/alliance` opens your alliance. Cities are invited, join or leave it. Both sides must agree to found one.

## Diplomacy

Under **Diplomacy** you manage consent, membership and permissions between cities.

## Commands

- `/nation`: open your nation
- `/alliance`: open your alliance

For `/nation` and `/alliance`:

- `view`: show borders
- `off`: hide borders
- `retry`: repeat a change whose result is uncertain
