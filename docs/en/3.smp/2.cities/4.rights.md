Rights decide who may do what on your land. They apply in cities, on plots and in [camps](/smp/camps). This applies to every city, including cities that have not migrated to the tier system yet.

## Right groups

Rights are sorted into groups. Every group has a switch **Change the whole group**. **Fine rights** lets you set each right on its own. The button shows whether all, some or no rights are allowed.

| Group          | Rights                                                                           |
| :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Building       | Place blocks, Break blocks, Light fires, Water/lava buckets                      |
| Farming        | Harvest ripe crops, Sow and plant, Bonemeal and soil                             |
| Containers     | View containers, Take and insert, Furnaces, Brewing stands, Hoppers and droppers |
| Doors          | Doors, Trapdoors, Fence gates                                                    |
| Redstone       | Buttons and levers, Plates and tripwire, Configure redstone                      |
| Stations       | Anvils, Enchanting tables, Workstations, Lecterns                                |
| Living         | Beds, Bells and noteblocks                                                       |
| Decoration     | Item frames, Armour stands, Paintings, Flowerpots, Edit signs                    |
| Animals        | Feed and breed, Kill animals, Ride animals, Leash animals, Shear and milk        |
| Movement       | Place vehicles, Use vehicles, Throw ender pearls                                 |
| Spawners       | Place spawners, Use spawners                                                     |
| Graves         | Manage graves                                                                    |
| Administration | cities only, see below                                                           |

**Administration** covers: Create plots, Set plot prices, Repossess plots, Edit parcel rights, Invite residents, Remove residents, Restrict residents, Deposit into treasury, Withdraw from treasury, View treasury history, Manage ranks, Manage outposts, Expand city, Configure TNT and PvP, Manage election rules, Manage diplomacy, Buy and rent plots, Vote in elections and Deliver to tier project.

## In the city

City ranks get their rights with on/off switches. The available ranks are listed under [Government and ranks](/smp/cities/government).

### City hard locks

Under **Management** and **City hard locks**, the city locks single rights for everyone. This needs the right **Edit parcel rights**.

- An enabled lock blocks this right across the city, including personal plots and their owners.
- The head is bound by locks too.
- A plot cannot allow a locked right: "Blocked by the city; a plot cannot allow this right."

### City zone

**Create city zone** creates a city-owned plot that cannot be sold. This needs the right **Create plots**.

## On plots and in camps

On your plot and in your camp you choose **Standard**, **Allow** or **Deny** for each group and each right.

| Group         | Applies to                     |
| :------------ | :----------------------------- |
| Co-residents  | plot and camp                  |
| Friends       | plot and camp                  |
| Party members | plot and camp                  |
| Clan members  | plot and camp                  |
| Citizens      | plot only: members of the city |
| Visitors      | plot and camp                  |

- In a camp, **Standard** means denied. On a plot, Standard means the rights of the city rank apply. For strangers that is the Guest rank.
- You cannot give administration rights or graves here. Manage foreign graves on your land in the separate **Graves** menu.
- Plots and camps from before version 0.24.0 kept their old rights.

### Single players

Under **Personal rights**, **Add personal rights** gives one player their own rights or takes them away. Enter their name.

### Co-management

Under **Co-management**, **Add co-manager** adds a player. They may edit rights and residents. Camps you co-manage are listed under **Managed camps**.

### What comes first?

"City lock, person, role, city rank: the first setting wins. At the same level, Deny wins."

1. A city lock always denies.
2. Then what you set for the single player counts.
3. Then the group, for example Friends. If several groups match and one says **Deny**, Deny applies.
4. Last the city rank.

## Land settings

Under **Land settings** you set TNT, PvP, environment and cosmetics. In a city this needs the right **Configure TNT and PvP**. On a plot and in a camp only the owner may do this.

### TNT and PvP

- **Allow TNT:** damage stays within this territory.
- **Allow PvP:** both sides must allow combat.

Both are off until you turn them on.

### Environment

Under **Environment** you set what animals, mobs and technology may do on your land.

| Group                    | Rule                      | What it controls                               | At the start |
| :----------------------- | :------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------- | :----------- |
| Animals & villagers      | Bees                      | Pollination, hives and honey                   | on           |
| Animals & villagers      | Sheep                     | Eating grass                                   | on           |
| Animals & villagers      | Villagers                 | Harvesting and planting                        | on           |
| Animals & villagers      | Snow golems               | Placing snow trails                            | on           |
| Disruptive mobs          | Endermen                  | Picking up and placing blocks                  | off          |
| Disruptive mobs          | Zombies                   | Breaking doors                                 | off          |
| Disruptive mobs          | Ravagers                  | Destroying crops                               | off          |
| Disruptive mobs          | Wither                    | Destroying blocks                              | off          |
| Disruptive mobs          | Mob explosions            | Creepers, wither skulls and ghasts             | off          |
| Technical transfers      | Redstone & observers      | Signals across boundaries                      | on           |
| Technical transfers      | Hoppers                   | Hoppers and hopper minecarts across boundaries | on           |
| Technical transfers      | Pistons & flying machines | Moving blocks across boundaries                | on           |
| Fluids across boundaries | Water                     | Flowing across boundaries                      | off          |
| Fluids across boundaries | Lava                      | Flowing across boundaries                      | off          |

- Transfers across boundaries need both sides. The wilderness always allows them.
- On a plot, **Standard** inherits the city's setting.

### Cosmetics

Under **Cosmetics** you choose which visible effects are allowed on your land:

- Auras
- Trails
- Pets
- Fireworks

Everything is on until you turn it off. The setting applies to members and visitors. A plot has its own setting, independent of the city. More about effects under [Account](/guides/account#effects).
