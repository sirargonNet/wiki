This page describes cities in the tier system. Cities that have not migrated yet keep the old system, see [Older cities](#older-cities).

## City forms

Every city has a form. It decides the head's title and how elections work. Only the head changes the form with **Change city form**.

| Form         | Head's title  | How the head takes office                                |
| :----------- | :------------ | :------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dominion     | Ruler         | founder, transfer or succession                          |
| Republic     | Mayor         | elected by the citizens                                  |
| City council | Council chair | council election, then chair election inside the council |

## Fixed ranks

Every city in the tier system has these fixed ranks:

| Rank       | What it may do from the start                                                                                                                                                             |
| :--------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Head       | everything. The menu shows the title of the city form.                                                                                                                                    |
| Councillor | city council only: everything a resident may, plus manage plots, invite, remove and restrict residents, manage outposts, expand the city, set TNT and PvP and view the treasury history   |
| Resident   | build, break, harvest, use containers, doors, redstone, stations, beds, decoration, animals and vehicles; deposit, buy and rent plots, vote and deliver to the tier project. No spawners. |
| Guest      | nothing                                                                                                                                                                                   |

- The rights of head and councillor are fixed: "This office has fixed permissions".
- Set the rights of residents and guests under **City ranks**. These ranks cannot be renamed, deleted or moved.
- Guests never get the rights for graves or the tier project.
- Only the head may **Dissolve city**. No rank can get it.

## Custom ranks

::callout{type="info"}
After the next update: The **Residents** list shows each member’s actual city rank. Custom ranks appear by name, and fixed ranks use their translated titles. Restricted members have a **restricted** label before their rank. The label stays visible with long rank names.
::

How many custom ranks your city may have depends on its [tier](/smp/cities/tiers): 5 as a Clearing, 14 as a Metropolis. Fixed ranks do not count.

You need the right **Manage ranks**:

- **Create rank:** enter a name. Custom ranks start without permissions.
- **Rename rank** and **Delete rank:** members of a deleted rank become Residents.
- **Move rank up** and **Move rank down:** the order matters for [succession](#dominion).
- **Assign rank:** under **Residents**, choose a member and give them a rank.

Councillors take office only through an election. The head changes only by transfer, election or succession.

## Rights

The available rights are listed under [Rights and area settings](/smp/cities/rights). You can also restrict single residents. They then become **Restricted residents**.

## Elections

Under **Government and elections**, **Election rules** set the term, the re-election and the days until voting rights. This needs the right **Manage election rules**. At the start only the head has it.

| Rule                             | At the start |
| :------------------------------- | -----------: |
| Days of membership before voting |            7 |
| Term                             |      28 days |
| Interval until re-election       |       3 days |

- Anyone may vote who has the right **Vote in elections**, is not restricted and has been a member long enough.
- **Open election** starts an election. When a term ends, the election starts by itself.
- An election runs for one day. The menu shows when it ends.
- After a tie, a caretaker leads the city until the re-election decides. The caretaker cannot change everything.

### City council

In a city council the city elects a council with several seats.

1. In **Elect council**, every eligible voter casts one vote.
2. The players with the most votes become councillors.
3. Then **Elect chair** opens. Only the councillors see this election and vote.
4. The winner becomes head with the title Council chair.

Set the number of seats under **Election rules** in **Council seats**: 3, 5, 7 or 9 seats. At the start there are 5. Changes apply from the next council election.

When the city switches to a city council, the councillor rank appears. When it leaves the city council, councillors become residents again.

### Dominion

A dominion has no election. The head hands over leadership to another resident with **Transfer headship**. This only works in a dominion.

**Uprising:** the citizens can overthrow the head.

1. An eligible voter chooses **Start uprising**.
2. Other eligible voters choose **Sign uprising**. **Withdraw signature** takes your signature back.
3. "The uprising lasts 3 mode days. With at least 3 signatures and two thirds of eligible voters, the city becomes a republic with a new election."

If the uprising succeeds, the old head becomes a resident. A caretaker leads the city until the election is decided. "After a failed uprising, wait 14 mode days before starting another." You cannot sign in a frozen city.

**Succession:** if the head of a dominion has not been active for a long time, an active member takes over automatically. They come from the highest custom rank; on a tie, whoever has been a member longest. If nobody is there, a resident follows. Guests and restricted residents never move up.

## Older cities

Cities founded before version 0.24.0 that have not [migrated](/smp/cities/claims-2) yet keep the old system:

- base ranks: Head, Assistant, Honorary citizen, Citizen and Visitor
- at most ten city ranks in total
- In a dominion the head is called City lord. There is no uprising and no succession.
- In a city council the head is elected directly, without council seats.

Migrating to the tier system resets custom ranks and city rights.
