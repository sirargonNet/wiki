Besides the vanilla enchantments, the SMP has more enchantments, in the Overworld and the Nether. You find almost all of them only as loot in certain structures, not at the enchanting table. These structures have their own [advancements](/smp/advancements#structures).

## Overworld

Except for Wind Boost, you only find these enchantments as loot in the structures listed, not at the enchanting table.

| Enchantment           | Levels | For                      | Effect                                                        | Found in                         |
| --------------------- | ------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Wind Boost            | I–IV   | harnesses                | Your happy ghast flies faster                                 | enchanting table, anvil          |
| Aerial's Bane         | I–V    | melee weapons            | More damage against creatures in the air                      | lone citadel                     |
| Illagers Bane         | I–V    | melee weapons            | 2.5 more damage per level against illagers                    | pale residence, woodland mansion |
| Spiteful              | I–III  | melee weapons            | More damage when you are low on health                        | shrines, pale residence          |
| Might                 | I–V    | crossbow                 | More damage with arrows                                       | lone citadel                     |
| Tri-Shot              | I      | bow                      | You shoot three arrows at once                                | toxic lair                       |
| Blowthrough           | I–IV   | bow                      | Arrows pass through one creature per level                    | toxic lair                       |
| Antidote              | I      | chestplate               | Protects from poison and wither damage, costs durability      | toxic lair                       |
| Outreach              | I–IV   | chestplate               | You reach blocks 0.5 blocks further per level                 | desert ruins                     |
| Traveler              | I–III  | boots                    | While sprinting you step up higher and move a little faster   | shrines                          |
| Photosynthesis        | I      | anything with durability | Slowly repairs itself in light. Does not combine with Mending | jungle ruins                     |
| Curse of Conductivity | I      | metal armour and tools   | In a thunderstorm under open sky, lightning can strike you    | witch villa                      |

Tri-Shot and Blowthrough cannot go on the same bow.

## Nether

You also find these enchantments only as loot in the structures listed, not at the enchanting table. The structures have their own [advancements](/smp/advancements#nether).

| Enchantment   | Levels | For                                 | Effect                                                                  | Found in                                |
| ------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Ghasted       | I–III  | Crossbow                            | Arrows turn into ghast fireballs                                        | Sealing Halls                           |
| Wither Coated | I–III  | Swords, spears, axes, trident, mace | Gives enemies the Wither effect at the cost of more durability          | Nether Keep, Sealing Halls, Nether Port |
| Hydro Veil    | I–IV   | Harnesses                           | Your Happy Ghast heals faster and is protected from fire and explosions | Nether Keep, Sealing Halls              |
| Swift Soar    | I–III  | Harnesses                           | Your Happy Ghast flies faster while you sprint                          | Nether Keep, Nether Port, Sealing Halls |

- Ghasted does not go together with Multishot, Piercing, Might or Aerial's Bane.
- Wither Coated does not go together with Fire Aspect.
- Wither skeletons sometimes carry weapons with Wither Coated.
