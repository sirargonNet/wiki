With Simple Voice Chat you talk to others right in the game. The mod is included in the official modpack. You hear players near you, up to 48 blocks away. In a group you also hear your group on other servers. Voice chat works in the lobby, in the SMP and in Creative.

## Set up

Install the official modpack [Sirargons Mod-Mische](https://sirargon.net/modpack). Simple Voice Chat is included. Use a [supported Minecraft version](/getting-started/first-steps#connect).

Without the voice mod you play as usual.

## Proximity

You hear players near you: the closer, the louder. You hear others up to 48 blocks away. When you whisper, only players up to 24 blocks away hear you.

## Name tags

The name above a player shows their voice chat: no voice chat, turned off or in another group. You only see these marks if you have the voice mod yourself.

## Groups

Create a group with your friends in the voice chat's group menu.

- A group works across the whole network. You hear each other even on different servers.
- The group stays when you switch servers.
- The group menu also lists groups from other servers, and you can join them.
- Every group name exists only once. A group can have a password.
- If you leave the network, you leave the group after a short time.

## Where it works

Voice chat works in the lobby, the SMP and Creative. There is no voice chat while watching replays.

## Checklist

- [ ] Official modpack set up
- [ ] Connected with a supported version
- [ ] Microphone and speakers checked in the voice settings

The [rules](/reference/rules) apply in voice chat too. If you are muted in voice, others cannot hear you. If you are banned from voice, you cannot hear anyone or join a group.

## Commands

- `/voice status`: Shows whether your voice chat is connected and verified, the server and the last acknowledgement.
- `/voice reconnect`: Reconnects your voice chat without leaving the server. You can use it once every 30 seconds.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get voice chat?

With the official modpack. More under [Set up](/guides/voice-chat#set-up).

### My voice chat will not connect. What can I do?

Use `/voice status` to check whether the connection is verified. Use `/voice reconnect` to start it again. You can stay on the server.
