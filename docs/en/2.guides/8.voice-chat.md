With Simple Voice Chat you can talk to other players right in the game. You hear the players near you, and in a group you also hear your group on other servers.

## Set up

Install the official modpack [Sirargons Mod-Mische](https://sirargon.net/modpack), which already includes Simple Voice Chat, and use a [supported Minecraft version](/getting-started/first-steps#connect).

No voice mod? You can still play as usual.

## Proximity

The closer a player is, the louder you hear them, up to 48 blocks away. When you whisper, only players within 24 blocks can hear you.

## Name tags

The name above a player shows their voice chat status: they have no voice chat, have turned it off, or are in another group. You only see these marks if you have the voice mod yourself.

## Groups

Create a group with your friends in the voice chat's group menu.

- A group works across the whole network, so you hear each other even on different servers.
- The group stays together when you switch servers.
- The group menu also lists groups from other servers, and you can join them.
- Each group name can only exist once, and a group can have a password.
- If you leave the network, you leave the group after a short time.

## Where it works

Voice chat works in the lobby, the SMP and Creative, but not while you're watching replays.

## Checklist

- [ ] Official modpack set up
- [ ] Connected with a supported version
- [ ] Microphone and speakers checked in the voice settings

The [rules](/reference/rules) apply in voice chat too. If you're muted in voice, others can't hear you. If you're banned from voice, you can't hear anyone or join a group.

## Commands

- `/voice status`: shows whether your voice chat is connected and verified, plus the server and the last acknowledgement.
- `/voice reconnect`: reconnects your voice chat without you leaving the server. You can use it once every 30 seconds.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get voice chat?

It comes with the official modpack. [Set up](/guides/voice-chat#set-up) explains how.

### My voice chat will not connect. What can I do?

Check with `/voice status` whether the connection is verified, then use `/voice reconnect` to start it again. You can stay on the server while you do.
