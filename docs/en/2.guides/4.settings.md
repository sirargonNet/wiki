`/settings` opens your settings for chat, requests, privacy, lobby, network and language. Effect settings are in your inventory. Each of these categories has its own page, and you can also open it directly, for example with `/settings privacy`.

Some settings are only available with a paid rank. Without one, they show up locked.

## Chat

`/settings chat`

- **Name pings:** a notification sound when someone mentions your name in chat
- **Direct messages:** your consent to direct messages, see [Messages](/guides/chat#messages)
- **Join and leave notices:** all, selected or none
- **AFK notices:** all, selected or none
- **Announce levels:** others see your level milestones. With it off, only you get your level message.
- **Thank my purchases publicly:** when you buy a rank or someone gifts you one, chat thanks you by name

In this section, “selected” means your friends, your party, your clan, the team and supporters.

## Requests

`/settings requests`

- **Messages from:** everyone, friends or nobody
- **Reply access:** how long someone you wrote to may reply, for the session or a fixed time
- **Friend requests:** everyone, friends of friends or nobody
- **Party invitations:** everyone, friends or nobody
- **Clan invitations:** everyone, friends or nobody

## Privacy

`/settings privacy`

- **Statistics:** public, friends or only me, also on the website
- **Show online status**
- **Show current server**
- **Show last online**
- **Play time:** visible or hidden
- **SMP money:** silver on public leaderboards, Argonite only in profiles
- **Leaderboards:** with it off, your place stays anonymous
- **Show first join**
- **Allow following:** friends may follow you to your server

If your statistics are private or leaderboards are off, chat won't thank you publicly for purchases either.

## Lobby

`/settings lobby`

- **Lobby players:** all, selected or none. Here, “selected” means friends, party and clan.
- **Lobby return:** back to your last position or to spawn
- **Lobby flight:** rank only
- **Double jump:** on or off
- **Pick me up:** who may pick you up: everyone, friends or nobody

## Network

`/settings network`

- **Reconnect to:** on your next login, back to your last mode or to the lobby
- **Sidebar:** Normal, Compact or Minimal. Compact and Minimal are narrower and show shorter values.
- **Skill tree:** your SMP skill tree as a constellation or in columns
- **Skill feedback:** shows why a skill does not activate or stops early
- **Quest progress:** shows on the status line how far your quest has come
- **Show SMP XP:** adds up your recent SMP XP gains on the status line
- **Death thunder:** a quiet thunder with every public SMP death message
- **Share graves:** who may open your private graves while only you can open them. Pick one of **Off**, **Friends** or **Clan**; the default is **Off**. What counts is your current friends or your current clan in the same mode. How graves work is explained under [PvP and death](/smp/death#gravestone).
- **Show optional announcements:** rank only. Turning it off hides only optional notices, such as shop and wiki tips. Important notices such as maintenance stay visible.

## Effects

The effect settings are in `/inventory`. Open **Effects** or **Gadgets** there and click **Effect settings**.

- **Show effects:** All, Simple only or None for other players' effects. You still see your own effects. For cheers and respects, though, the filters apply to your own words too.
- Turn off individual kinds such as auras, trails, gadgets or **Reaction effects**.
- **Chat animations:** on by default. With them off, animated cheers and respects show only their first colour and no sparkle characters.
- **Allow gadget hits:** whether other players may push you with their gadgets.

Right-click an effect to choose its **Allowed contexts**, the places where your own effect may be active. In the SMP you can turn it off separately for your own area, foreign areas and the wilderness. More in [Inventory](/guides/inventory).

## Language

`/settings language`

Pick your language yourself, or go with your Minecraft client's language.

| Language      | Status |
| :------------ | :----- |
| Deutsch       |        |
| English (UK)  |        |
| Français      | Beta   |
| Italiano      | Beta   |
| Rumantsch     | Beta   |
| Deutsch (AT)  | Beta   |
| Deutsch (CH)  | Beta   |
| Français (CH) | Beta   |
| Italiano (CH) | Beta   |

In the menu, every language shows its flag and its own name. Beta languages have a red **Beta** after the name, because their translation is still being reviewed. When you pick one, chat tells you once.

Your language choice applies in game and to your account on the [website](/guides/account#website) at the same time. You can also set it there under **Language**, and **Automatic** follows your client or browser.

The wiki is available in German and English.

## Reset

**Reset everything** puts every setting back to its default. Your consent to direct messages is kept.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/settings`: open settings
- `/settings chat|requests|privacy|lobby|network|language`: open a page directly
- `/settings dm`: consent to direct messages

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I hide my statistics?

Under `/settings privacy`, set **Statistics** to friends or only you.

### How do I change the language?

Under `/settings language`. More under [Language](/guides/settings#language).

### Why can't I pick my city for grave sharing any more?

Your city is no longer one of the options. If you had Clan turned on before, **Clan** now applies. Otherwise, if you had Friends turned on, **Friends** applies.
