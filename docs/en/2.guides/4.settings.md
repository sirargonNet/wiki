`/settings` opens your settings. Here you choose your chat, requests, privacy, lobby, network, effects and language options. Each category has its own page, which you can also open directly, for example with `/settings privacy`.

Some settings are only available with a paid rank. Without one, you see them locked.

## Chat

`/settings chat`

- **Name pings:** a notification sound when your name is mentioned in chat
- **Direct messages:** your consent, see [Messages](/guides/chat#messages)
- **Join and leave notices:** all, selected or none
- **AFK notices:** all, selected or none
- **Announce levels:** others see your level milestones. When off, only you get your level message.
- **Thank my purchases publicly:** when you buy or are gifted a rank, chat thanks you by name

“Selected” here means your friends, your party, your clan, the team and supporters.

## Requests

`/settings requests`

- **Messages from:** everyone, friends or nobody
- **Reply access:** how long someone you wrote to may reply, for the session or a fixed time
- **Friend requests:** everyone, friends of friends or nobody
- **Party invitations:** everyone, friends or nobody
- **Clan invitations:** everyone, friends or nobody

## Privacy

`/settings privacy`

- **Statistics:** public, friends or only me, also on the website
- **Show online status**
- **Show current server**
- **Show last online**
- **Play time:** visible or hidden
- **SMP money:** silver on public leaderboards, Argonite only in profiles
- **Leaderboards:** when off, your place stays anonymous
- **Show first join**
- **Allow following:** friends may follow you to your server

Private statistics or leaderboards turned off also prevent the public thanks for purchases.

## Lobby

`/settings lobby`

- **Lobby players:** all, selected or none. Selected here means friends, party and clan.
- **Lobby return:** back to your last position or to spawn
- **Lobby flight:** rank only
- **Double jump:** on or off
- **Pick me up:** who may pick you up: everyone, friends or nobody

## Network

`/settings network`

- **Reconnect to:** on your next login, back to your last mode or to the lobby
- **Sidebar:** Normal, Compact or Minimal. Compact and Minimal are narrower and show shorter values.
- **Skill tree:** your SMP skill tree as a constellation or in columns
- **Skill feedback:** shows why a skill does not activate or stops early
- **Quest progress:** shows on the status line how far your quest has come
- **Show SMP XP:** shows recent SMP XP gains together on the status line
- **Death thunder:** a quiet thunder with every public SMP death message
- **Share graves:** who may open your private graves while only you can open them. Choose one: **Off**, **Friends** or **Clan**. Your current friends or your current clan in the same mode count. The default is **Off**. You can no longer choose your city. If you had Clan turned on before, **Clan** applies; otherwise, with Friends on, **Friends**. See [PvP and death](/smp/death#gravestone).
- **Show optional announcements:** rank only. When off, it hides only optional notices, such as shop and wiki tips. Important notices such as maintenance stay visible.

## Effects

Find these settings in `/inventory`. Open **Effects** or **Gadgets** and click **Effect settings**.

- **Show effects:** All, Simple only or None for other players' effects. You still see your own effects.
- Turn individual kinds such as auras, trails, gadgets or **Reaction effects** off.
- **Allow gadget hits:** whether other players may push you with their gadgets.

Right-click an effect to choose its **Allowed contexts**. In the SMP you can disable your own area, foreign areas and wilderness separately. See [Inventory](/guides/inventory).

## Language

`/settings language`

Choose your language. Or use your Minecraft client's language.

| Italiano      | Beta   |
| Rumantsch     | Beta   |
| Deutsch (AT)  | Beta   |
| Deutsch (CH)  | Beta   |
| Français (CH) | Beta   |

Every language appears in the menu with its flag and its own name. Beta languages have a red **Beta** after the name. Their translation is still being reviewed. When you choose one, chat tells you once.

Your language choice applies in game and to your account on the [website](/guides/account#website) at the same time. You can also set it there under **Language**. **Automatic** follows your client or browser.

The wiki is available in German and English.

## Reset

**Reset everything** sets every setting back to default. Your consent to direct messages stays.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/settings`: open settings
- `/settings chat|requests|privacy|lobby|network|language`: open a page directly
- `/settings dm`: consent to direct messages

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I hide my statistics?

Under `/settings privacy`, set **Statistics** to friends or only you.

### How do I change the language?

Under `/settings language`. More under [Language](/guides/settings#language).
