## Your account in game

`/account` opens your account. Find your profile, friends, party, requests, blocked players and messages here. `/profile` opens your own profile.

Use `/profile <player>` to open another profile where available. Profiles are accessible for friends, party and clan members, blocked players and pending requests.

## Levels and XP

Your profile shows your level. The network, SMP and Fishing have separate levels and XP displays. You can also see how much XP you need for the next level.

## Currencies

| Currency | Scope                           |
| :------- | :------------------------------ |
| Argonite | Virtual currency of the network |
| Silver   | Currency of the SMP             |
| Tokens   | Currency of Fishing             |

Your account shows your balances.

## Settings

Use `/settings` to open your settings: chat, requests, privacy, lobby and language. For example, choose who can contact you and which details others can see about you.

Manage your direct message consent under `/settings dm`. Lobby settings include player visibility and your return point; some features require permission.
