Use `/clan` to open your clan. Use `/clans` to discover other clans.

A clan has a name, a tag and a colour. You have your own clan chat, can invite players, accept applications and assign roles. Each clan level makes room for more members.

- `/clans list` shows the clan directory.
- `/clans search <text>` searches for a clan.
- `/clans info <clan>` shows information.
- `/clan members` shows your members.
- `/clan apply <clan>` applies to a clan.
- `/clan accept <clan>` accepts an invitation.
- `/clanchat <message>` writes in your clan chat.

The actions you can take depend on your clan role. The clan menu guides you through the available steps.
