`/friends` opens your friends overview, where you can see who's online, AFK or offline right now, and on which server. If a friend allows it, you can follow them.

`/party` starts a party. It keeps your group together and comes with its own chat.

## Add friends

1. Type `/friends add <player>`.
2. The other player gets “Request from …:” in chat with the buttons **Accept** and **Decline**.
3. If they accept, you are friends.

When someone sends you a request, click **Accept** or **Decline**, or type `/friends accept <player>` or `/friends decline <player>`. `/friends requests` shows which requests are still open. Changed your mind about a request you sent? Take it back with **Cancel** or `/friends cancel <player>`. A friend request expires after 7 days.

## Follow a friend

You can follow a friend if they allow it, their online status is visible and their server still has room. You decide whether others may follow you under `/settings privacy`.

## Who may send you requests

Under `/settings requests` you choose who may send you friend requests: everyone, friends of friends or nobody.

## Limits

How many friends you can have and how big your party can get depends on your rank. The party leader counts towards the party size.

| Rank    | Friends | Party |
| :------ | ------: | ----: |
| no rank |      16 |     4 |
| Prime   |      32 |     8 |
| Elite   |      64 |    16 |
| Legend  |     128 |    32 |

If either of you has already hit the friend limit, you can't become friends. Your party can only be as big as the party leader's limit allows.

Playing together? Create a [party](/guides/friends#party).

## Party

`/party` opens your party.

### Create a party and invite

1. Type `/party invite <player>`. If you don't have a party yet, this creates one and makes you the party leader.
2. The other player gets “Request from …:” in chat with the buttons **Accept** and **Decline**.
3. If they accept, they are in your party.

When you get an invitation, click **Accept** or type `/party accept <player>`. `/party requests` shows which invitations are still open. You decide who may invite you under `/settings requests` with **Party invitations**: everyone, friends or nobody.

- You can only invite players who are online right now.
- A party invitation expires after 60 seconds.
- A party has a [member limit](/guides/friends#limits). If it is full, the game tells you.

### Follow a member

`/party join <player>` takes you to a member's server. You can also do this from the party menu, which `/party` opens.

### Party leader

Only the party leader can:

- invite players: `/party invite <player>`
- remove members: `/party kick <player>`
- hand over the lead: `/party leader <player>`
- disband the party: `/party disband`

If the party leader leaves, the member who has been in the party longest and is online right now takes over. If nobody else is online, or only one member is left, the party disbands.

### Leave and go offline

`/party leave` takes you out of the party, and you can only come back with a new invitation. If you leave the network, you stay in the party for another 5 minutes. If you're not back by then, you're out automatically.

### Party chat

`/partychat <message>` or `/pc <message>` sends a message to party chat. Leave the message out and party chat becomes your default chat. [Chat](/guides/chat) explains how the chat channels work.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

You can also type `/friend` instead of `/friends`, `/parties` instead of `/party` and `deny` instead of `decline`.

- `/friends`: open friends
- `/friends list`: show friends
- `/friends add <player>`: send a request
- `/friends requests`: show requests
- `/friends accept <player>`: accept a request
- `/friends decline <player>`: decline a request
- `/friends cancel <player>`: withdraw a request
- `/friends remove <player>`: end a friendship
- `/friends join <player>`: follow a friend
- `/friends invite <player>`: invite to your party
- `/friends block <player>`: block a player
- `/friends unblock <player>`: unblock a player
- `/friends blocks`: show blocked players
- `/friends help`: all actions
- `/party`: open your party
- `/party create`: create a party
- `/party invite <player>`: invite
- `/party accept <player>`: accept an invitation
- `/party decline <player>`: decline an invitation
- `/party requests`: show open invitations
- `/party cancel <player>`: withdraw an invitation
- `/party list`: show members
- `/party join <player>`: follow a member
- `/party leave`: leave the party
- `/party kick <player>`: remove a member
- `/party leader <player>`: hand over the lead
- `/party disband`: disband the party
- `/party help`: all actions

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I play with friends?

Use `/friends` for your [friends](/guides/friends) and `/party` for your [party](/guides/friends#party).

### Why can I not follow a friend?

They have to allow following, their online status has to be visible, and their server needs room for you.
