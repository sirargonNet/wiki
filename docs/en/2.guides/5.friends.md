`/friends` opens your friends. You see who is online, AFK or offline, and on which server. You can follow friends if they allow it.

With `/party` you start a party. It keeps your group together and has its own chat. How many friends and party members you can have depends on your rank.

## Add friends

1. Type `/friends add <player>`.
2. The other player gets “Request from …:” in chat with the buttons **Accept** and **Decline**.
3. If they accept, you are friends.

If you get a request yourself, click **Accept** or **Decline**. Or type `/friends accept <player>` or `/friends decline <player>`. `/friends requests` shows open requests. You withdraw your own request with **Cancel** or `/friends cancel <player>`. A friend request expires after 7 days.

## Follow a friend

You can follow a friend if they allow it, their online status is visible and their server has room. Whether others may follow you is set under `/settings privacy`.

## Who may send you requests

Under `/settings requests` you choose who may send you friend requests: everyone, friends of friends or nobody.

## Limits

How many friends you can have and how big your party may get depends on your rank. The party leader counts too.

| Rank    | Friends | Party |
| :------ | ------: | ----: |
| no rank |      16 |     4 |
| Prime   |      32 |     8 |
| Elite   |      64 |    16 |
| Legend  |     128 |    32 |

If one of you has reached the friend limit, no new friendship is possible. For a party, the leader's limit counts.

Playing together? Create a [party](/guides/friends#party).

## Party

`/party` opens your party. A party keeps your group together.

### Create a party and invite

1. Type `/party invite <player>`. If you have no party yet, this creates one. You are then the party leader.
2. The other player gets “Request from …:” in chat with the buttons **Accept** and **Decline**.
3. If they accept, they are in your party.

If you get an invitation, click **Accept** or type `/party accept <player>`. `/party requests` shows open invitations. Who may invite you is set under `/settings requests` with **Party invitations**: everyone, friends or nobody.

- You can only invite players who are online.
- A party invitation expires after 60 seconds.
- A party has a [member limit](/guides/friends#limits). If it is full, the game tells you.

### Follow a member

`/party join <player>` follows a member to their server. You can also do this in the party menu with `/party`.

### Party leader

Only the party leader can:

- invite players: `/party invite <player>`
- remove members: `/party kick <player>`
- hand over the lead: `/party leader <player>`
- disband the party: `/party disband`

If the party leader leaves, the member who has been in the party longest and is online takes over. If nobody else is online, the party disbands. If only one member is left, the party disbands too.

### Leave and go offline

`/party leave` leaves the party. You can only come back with a new invitation. If you leave the network, you stay in the party for 5 minutes. If you are not back by then, you leave it automatically.

### Party chat

`/partychat <message>` or `/pc <message>` writes in the party chat. Without a message, party chat becomes your default chat. More under [Chat](/guides/chat).

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

Instead of `/friends` you can use `/friend`. Instead of `/party` you can use `/parties`. Instead of `decline` you can use `deny`.

- `/friends`: open friends
- `/friends list`: show friends
- `/friends add <player>`: send a request
- `/friends requests`: show requests
- `/friends accept <player>`: accept a request
- `/friends decline <player>`: decline a request
- `/friends cancel <player>`: withdraw a request
- `/friends remove <player>`: end a friendship
- `/friends join <player>`: follow a friend
- `/friends invite <player>`: invite to your party
- `/friends block <player>`: block a player
- `/friends unblock <player>`: unblock a player
- `/friends blocks`: show blocked players
- `/friends help`: all actions
- `/party`: open your party
- `/party create`: create a party
- `/party invite <player>`: invite
- `/party accept <player>`: accept an invitation
- `/party decline <player>`: decline an invitation
- `/party requests`: show open invitations
- `/party cancel <player>`: withdraw an invitation
- `/party list`: show members
- `/party join <player>`: follow a member
- `/party leave`: leave the party
- `/party kick <player>`: remove a member
- `/party leader <player>`: hand over the lead
- `/party disband`: disband the party
- `/party help`: all actions

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I play with friends?

`/friends` manages your [friends](/guides/friends), `/party` your [party](/guides/friends#party).

### Why can I not follow a friend?

They must allow following, their online status must be visible and their server must have room.
