With replays you can watch recordings right in the game. `/replays` opens **My recordings**, and you start a replay from the lobby with **Watch in game**. When it's over, you're back in the lobby.

## My recordings

`/replays` shows your highlights and saved rounds, each with its date and status: **Recording**, **Preparing**, **Ready** or **Incomplete**. Click a recording to see its length too. You can only play recordings that are **Ready**. **Remove** takes a recording off your list.

## Start a replay

1. Go to the lobby, because that's the only place you can start a replay from. If you're in a queue, leave it first.
2. Open `/replays` and choose a recording that is **Ready**.
3. Click **Watch in game**.

Stay in the lobby until your replay starts. If you switch servers, the start is cancelled. If the game can estimate the wait reliably, it shows you that estimate. Otherwise it only tells you that your replay is being prepared. Don't want to wait after all? `/replays cancel` cancels the start.

## Controls

While watching, you're a spectator and can fly freely through the recording, but you can't change anything in it. `/replay` opens the playback controls, and ESC closes them again.

- **Pause** and **Resume**
- **Change speed:** 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×, 2× or 4×
- **Free camera** or **Player view:** in player view you choose a recorded player and see their view.
- **End playback:** you are back in the lobby afterwards.

There's no [voice chat](/guides/voice-chat) while you're watching replays.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/replays`: your recordings
- `/replays cancel`: cancel the start
- `/replay`: controls while watching
- `/replay pause` and `/replay resume`: pause and continue
- `/replay speed <value>`: speed, for example `0.5` or `2`
- `/replay free`: free camera
- `/replay follow`: a player's view
- `/replay end`: end
