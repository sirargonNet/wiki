Replays let you watch recordings right in the game. `/replays` opens **My recordings**. Start a replay from the lobby with **Watch in game**. While watching, `/replay` opens the controls, and when it ends you are back in the lobby.

## My recordings

`/replays` shows your highlights and saved rounds with date and status: **Recording**, **Preparing**, **Ready** or **Incomplete**. Click a recording to also see its duration. You can only play recordings that are **Ready**. **Remove** takes a recording off your list.

## Start a replay

1. Go to the lobby. You can only start a replay from there. If you are in a queue, leave it first.
2. Open `/replays` and choose a recording that is **Ready**.
3. Click **Watch in game**.

Stay in the lobby while your replay starts. The game shows an estimated wait when a reliable estimate is available. Otherwise it only says that your replay is being prepared. Use `/replays cancel` to cancel the start.

## Controls

While watching, you are a spectator and fly freely through the recording. You cannot change anything. `/replay` opens the playback controls, and ESC closes them.

- **Pause** and **Resume**
- **Change speed:** 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×, 2× or 4×
- **Free camera** or **Player view:** in player view you choose a recorded player and see their view.
- **End playback:** you are back in the lobby afterwards.

There is no [voice chat](/guides/voice-chat) while watching replays.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/replays`: your recordings
- `/replays cancel`: cancel the start
- `/replay`: controls while watching
- `/replay pause` and `/replay resume`: pause and continue
- `/replay speed <value>`: speed, for example `0.5` or `2`
- `/replay free`: free camera
- `/replay follow`: a player's view
- `/replay end`: end
