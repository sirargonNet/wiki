`/account` opens your account. In the lobby you can also get there by clicking your head in the hotbar. That's where your profile, statistics, friends, clan, messages and settings live.

Around your account, you can also link Discord, the website and Twitch, request your data and pick your skin. Without a Discord link, you can't enter the SMP.

## Your account in game

- Profile
- Statistics
- Friends
- Clan
- Party
- Requests
- Messages
- Blocked
- Settings

You can open single pages directly with a command, for example `/account friends` or `/account messages`.

`/account punishments` opens the **Punishments** page with your points and your cases.

Your wallets with Argonite and Silver are under [Currencies & streak](/guides/currencies#wallets).

## Profile

`/profile` shows your profile with your level, balances, rank, play time and first join. With `/profile <player>` you can look at the profiles of friends, party and clan members, blocked players and players from open requests.

## Messages

`/account messages` collects the messages waiting for you:

- messages someone left while you were offline
- answers to your clan applications
- changes to your clan role
- confirmations and team replies for your bug reports
- market sales and deliveries for your buy orders
- Argonite and items you received

You can mark them as read and delete them.

When the team replies to a bug report, the current title appears next to the code. Only you, as the reporter, see that title, and long titles are shortened. If the report or your account has been deleted, only the code is left.

## Discord

Here's how to link your account with Discord:

1. Join the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).
2. Type `/minecraft link` there. You get a private code.
3. In game, type `/discord link <code>`.

Each code works only once, and it expires after a while. If it's unknown, expired or already used, get a new one on the Discord.

`/discord` tells you where you stand:

- **linked and membership confirmed:** all done, you can enter the SMP
- **linked, membership not yet confirmed:** join the Discord
- **not linked:** the game shows you the three steps

### If you leave the Discord

Being linked isn't enough for the SMP on its own: you also have to stay on the Discord.

- If you leave the Discord while you're in the SMP, you get a warning with a countdown. Rejoin before it runs out and you can stay; otherwise the game sends you to the lobby.
- If you're not on the Discord, you can't enter the SMP. Your link stays in place, though, so all you need to do is rejoin.

### Unlink

`/discord unlink` removes the link. After that, you have to wait a while before you can link again. To link a different Discord account, unlink the old one first.

## Website

How to sign in on [sirargon.net](https://sirargon.net):

1. Type `/web` in game.
2. Click **Web login** in chat.
3. Confirm your account on the website.

The link is valid for five minutes and one sign-in. Never share it with anyone. If you request too many links, you'll have to wait a moment.

`/web logout-all` signs you out everywhere on the website, and any sign-in links still open stop working too.

The website is where you'll find your statistics, your bug reports and the data export.

## Twitch

Do you stream on Twitch? Then the Twitch extension can show your statistics to your viewers.

1. Open the extension's configuration on Twitch to get a code.
2. Type `/twitch link <code>` in game.
3. **Link Twitch channel** opens. The extension shows your Minecraft name, UUID and skin publicly, even if your statistics are private.
4. Click **Link**.

If the code is unknown or expired, request a new one in the extension.

`/twitch unlink` removes the connection, and the extension stops showing your statistics.

## Your data

`/exportdata` requests an export of your data. You can check its status and download it on the website under **My data export**. Get to the website with `/web`; you don't need a separate account or an email address. You won't get an email or an in-game message when the export is ready. After requesting one, you have to wait a while before you can request another.

Your export also includes the stored measurements of SMP XP from your activities, before and after skill bonuses.

If your data is larger than the automatic limit, the team checks your export and delivers it to you in several parts. Both `/exportdata` and **My data export** tell you so, and you don't need to request it again. Once the parts are ready, you download them there one by one with **Download part 1 of 3** and so on. Just like a normal export, each download link only works for five minutes, and the parts stay available for one day at most, so download them soon.

## Skin

`/skin` picks the skin you'll have at your next login: `original`, `steve`, `alex` or `backward_steve`.

## Sharing statistics

Below every `/stats` result you'll see **Share**. It posts the result in the public chat, in the form everyone is allowed to see. If a result isn't visible to everyone, you can't share it. Everyone reads a shared result in their own language. You have to wait 60 seconds between two shared results. **Share** below `/stats deaths` posts your death count in the public chat too.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/account`: open your account
- `/account profile|friends|party|requests|blocks|messages|punishments`: open a page directly
- `/profile [player]`: open a profile
- `/stats <statistic> [sub-statistic] [me|player <player>|server|top|clans|clan [tag]]`: statistics, only in the SMP and Creative. Without arguments, `/stats` shows the usage.
- `/discord`: show your Discord link
- `/discord link <code>`: link Discord
- `/discord unlink`: remove the link
- `/web`: website sign-in link
- `/web logout-all`: sign out everywhere on the website
- `/twitch link <code>`: link Twitch
- `/twitch unlink`: disconnect Twitch
- `/skin [original|steve|alex|backward_steve]`: skin for your next login
- `/exportdata`: request a data export

The streak calendar (`/streak`) and `/gz` are under [Currencies & streak](/guides/currencies#commands), the inventory (`/inventory`) under [Inventory & cosmetics](/guides/inventory#commands).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I link Discord?

Type `/minecraft link` on the Discord and `/discord link <code>` in game. More under [Discord](/guides/account#discord).

### How do I sign in on the website?

Type `/web` and open the link. It works once and only for a short time. More under [Website](/guides/account#website).
