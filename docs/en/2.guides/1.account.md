## Your account in game

`/account` opens your account. In the lobby you can also click your head in the hotbar.

- Profile
- Statistics
- Friends
- Clan
- Party
- Requests
- Messages
- Blocked
- Settings

You can open single pages directly, for example with `/account friends` or `/account messages`.

`/account punishments` opens the **Punishments** page: your points and your cases.

## Profile

`/profile` opens your profile: level, balances, rank, play time and first join. `/profile <player>` opens the profile of friends, party and clan members, blocked players and open requests.

## Messages

`/account messages` holds messages waiting for you:

- messages someone left while you were offline
- answers to your clan applications
- changes to your clan role
- confirmations and team replies for your bug reports
- market sales and deliveries for your buy orders
- Argonite and items you received

You can mark them as read and delete them.

## Levels and XP

You collect XP while you play. There are two levels:

- **Network level** for the whole network
- **SMP level** in the SMP, see [Skill levels](/smp/skills/levels)

Your profile shows your level and how much XP you need for the next level.

### Network XP

You mainly earn network XP by playing actively. While you are AFK, you earn no XP. Small bonuses come on top:

- **Congratulation:** 5 network XP, see [Congratulations](#congratulations)
- **Paying respects:** 1 network XP

A [network booster](#boosters) doubles the XP from active play. Congratulations and respects do not count double.

### Level milestones

When you reach network level 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150 or 200, the whole network sees a message. Every other level is only shown to you. In the [settings](/guides/settings#chat) you can turn off **Announce levels**. Then only you get the message.

## Congratulations

When someone reaches a level, a milestone or an advancement in the SMP, you can congratulate them. Within 60 seconds, type only `gz` or `w` in chat. `gratz`, `grats` and `glückwunsch` count too. `/gz` types `gz` for you.

- A congratulation gives you 5 network XP.
- `f` or `o7` pays respects to someone who died and gives you 1 XP.
- There is only limited XP for this within 24 hours.

If there is no XP, the chat tells you why. For example, you were too late, already congratulated or reached your limit. Your own success gives no XP either.

Clicking **gz** on an advancement congratulates that exact success. Typing the word yourself refers to the newest open success.

## Currencies

Your balance is shown in your profile and in the sidebar.

### Argonite

Argonite is the currency of the whole network. You get it, for example:

- as a [streak reward](#streak)
- as thanks for helpful [bug reports](/getting-started/help)
- during team events

You spend Argonite on [boosters](#boosters), [auras and trails](#auras-and-trails), and [gadgets](#gadgets). When you receive Argonite, a message lands in `/account messages`.

### Silver

Silver is the SMP currency. See [SMP](/smp).

## Streak

`/streak` opens your streak calendar. In the lobby you can also click the clock in your hotbar.

- If you play actively enough on a day, the day counts towards your streak. The calendar shows how much active time you still need today.
- A day runs on German time, from midnight to midnight.
- Days with maintenance do not count against you.
- The team can set outage days. They count towards your streak.

The calendar shows your current and your longest streak. Use **Previous week** and **Next week** to browse.

### Rewards

Daily rewards run in a cycle of 7 days. After day 7 it starts again at day 1. Long streaks also give milestone rewards. The calendar shows what each day gives. Rewards can be Argonite or boosters, for example.

Collect reached rewards with **Collect rewards**. They do not expire. You can also collect missed rewards later.

### Ice

**Ice** protects your streak when you miss a day.

- You get 1 Ice for every third completed day.
- Collect waiting Ice with **Collect Ice**.
- Stock and waiting Ice together: up to 2.
- Every missed day uses 1 Ice. Stock first, then waiting Ice.
- Without Ice, your streak starts over.

## Inventory

`/inventory` opens your inventory. In the lobby you can also click the chest in your hotbar. It holds boosters, auras, trails and gadgets. It opens straight on the network booster tab. Use the tabs above to switch to SMP and Clan boosters, gadgets, trails and auras. The titles and pets sections are still empty.

Auras, trails and gadgets are **In development**. You can already use them, but their values and effects may change.

### Boosters

There are three kinds:

- **Network XP Booster:** double network XP from active play
- **SMP XP Booster:** double SMP XP. You can only activate it in the SMP.
- **Clan XP Booster:** double Clan XP for every clan on the network. You can buy and activate it without a clan of your own.

You get boosters from your streak or buy them for Argonite:

| Duration | Network XP   | SMP XP       | Clan XP      |
| :------- | :----------- | :----------- | :----------- |
| 30 min   | 250 Argonite | 300 Argonite | 200 Argonite |
| 60 min   | 450 Argonite | 550 Argonite | 350 Argonite |

Every offer doubles its kind of XP. Boosters you already bought keep their duration and effect. The menu lists the name, effect and duration separately.

To buy and activate a booster:

1. Open `/inventory` and choose network, SMP or Clan XP boosters.
2. Click **+ Buy** at the bottom right. Choose 30 or 60 minutes and confirm the displayed price.
3. Click your bought booster and confirm activation.

A bought booster waits in your inventory until you activate it.

An active booster works for all players. If one of the same kind is already running, yours joins the queue. It starts as soon as the previous one ends. Factors do not stack: two boosters never make 4×. An activation cannot be undone.

A bar at the top of the screen shows running boosters. It names the booster, who activated it and the time left. When more boosters wait, it also shows how many and how long the whole chain still runs.

### Auras and trails

Auras show particles around you. Trails follow your steps while you walk on the ground.

1. Open `/inventory` and choose **Auras** or **Trails**.
2. Click **+ Buy**, choose an effect and confirm its price in Argonite. The tab shows your bought effects and those unlocked by your rank.
3. Click the unlocked effect to equip it. Click again to unequip it. **No aura** or **No trail** also removes the effect of that kind.

You can equip one aura, one trail and one gadget at a time. Purchases are permanent. Rank effects need no purchase. You can use them while the required rank is active.

| Kind  | Effect           | Price or rank  |
| :---- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| Aura  | Sparks           | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura  | Hearts           | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura  | Notes            | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura  | Fire aura        | Prime or above |
| Aura  | Rain cloud       | Elite or above |
| Trail | Dust             | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail | Leaves           | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail | Snowflakes       | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail | Flame trail      | Prime or above |
| Trail | Soul flame trail | Elite or above |

These effects work in the lobby, on Creative plots where you may build, and outside combat in the SMP. In the SMP they work in your own area, foreign areas and the wilderness.

Right-click an effect to choose where it may be active. **Effect settings** controls which effects from other players you see. See [Settings](/guides/settings#effects).

### Gadgets

1. Open `/inventory` and choose **Gadgets**.
2. Buy a gadget through **+ Buy**, or choose one unlocked by your rank. Click it to equip it.
3. In the lobby it appears in the third hotbar slot. Right-click to use it.

| Gadget            | Price or rank  | Effect                                                                           | Cooldown    |
| :---------------- | :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------- |
| Confetti cannon   | 200 Argonite   | colourful confetti with a bang in the direction you look; moves and hurts nobody | 1.5 seconds |
| Snowball Launcher | 250 Argonite   | a snowball with a gentle push and a snow effect                                  | 1 second    |
| TNT Push          | 350 Argonite   | throws TNT; after 1.5 seconds, pushes players within 5 blocks without damage     | 4 seconds   |
| Jump boost        | Prime or above | jump upwards and slightly forwards                                               | 2.5 seconds |
| Grappling hook    | Elite or above | pulls you towards the block it hits, with a range of up to 25 blocks             | 2 seconds   |

The cooldown appears above your hotbar. When it ends, the game tells you that your gadget is ready.

The grappling hook pulls you towards a block and holds you there. Sneak, switch hotbar slots or throw again to let go. Missing a block within range does not start a cooldown.

All gadgets work only in the lobby. Gadgets cannot be used during parkour. Gadgets only push other players who have **Allow gadget hits** enabled. They do not hit you during parkour or while AFK.

## Discord

Link your account with Discord. The SMP requires it.

1. Join the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).
2. Type `/minecraft link` there. You get a private code.
3. In game, type `/discord link <code>`.

The code works only once and only for a while. If it is unknown, expired or already used, get a new one in Discord.

`/discord` shows your status:

- **linked and membership confirmed:** all done, you can enter the SMP
- **linked, membership not yet confirmed:** join the Discord
- **not linked:** the game shows you the three steps

### If you leave the Discord

For the SMP you must be linked and stay on the Discord.

- If you leave the Discord while you are in the SMP, you get a warning with a countdown. Rejoin within that time and you may stay. Otherwise the game takes you to the lobby.
- If you are not on the Discord, you cannot enter the SMP. Your link stays, though. Just rejoin.

### Unlink

`/discord unlink` removes the link. Afterwards there is a waiting time before you can link again. To link a different Discord account, unlink the old one first.

## Website

How to sign in on [sirargon.net](https://sirargon.net):

1. Type `/web` in game.
2. Click **Web login** in chat.
3. Confirm your account on the website.

The link is valid for five minutes and one sign-in. Never share it. If you request too many links, you have to wait a moment.

`/web logout-all` signs you out everywhere on the website. Open sign-in links stop working too.

On the website you find your statistics, your bug reports and the data export.

## Twitch

Do you stream on Twitch? The Twitch extension shows your viewers your statistics.

1. Open the extension's configuration on Twitch. You get a code there.
2. Type `/twitch link <code>` in game.
3. **Link Twitch channel** opens. Your Minecraft name, UUID and skin are shown publicly in the extension, even when your statistics are private.
4. Click **Link**.

If the code is unknown or expired, request a new one in the extension. `/twitch unlink` removes the connection. The extension then no longer shows your statistics.

## Your data

`/exportdata` requests an export of your data. You find the status and the download on the website under **My data export**. Open it with `/web`. You need no separate account and no email address. There is no email or in-game message when it is ready. You can request a new export only after a waiting time.

## Skin

`/skin` sets your skin for your next login: `original`, `steve`, `alex` or `backward_steve`.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/account`: open your account
- `/account profile|friends|party|requests|blocks|messages|punishments`: open a page directly
- `/profile [player]`: open a profile
- `/stats <statistic> [sub-statistic] [me|player <player>|server|top|clans|clan [tag]]`: statistics, only in the SMP and Creative. Without arguments, `/stats` shows the usage. **Share** below a result posts it in the public chat.
- `/streak`: streak calendar
- `/inventory`: boosters, auras, trails and gadgets
- `/gz`: congratulate, types `gz` in chat
- `/discord`: show your Discord link
- `/discord link <code>`: link Discord
- `/discord unlink`: remove the link
- `/web`: website sign-in link
- `/web logout-all`: sign out everywhere on the website
- `/twitch link <code>`: link Twitch
- `/twitch unlink`: disconnect Twitch
- `/skin [original|steve|alex|backward_steve]`: skin for your next login
- `/exportdata`: request a data export
