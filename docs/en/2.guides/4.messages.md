Use `/msg <player> <message>` to message a player directly. `/r <message>` replies to your last conversation while a reply is still possible.

## When someone is offline

You can leave a message for their next login. Follow the prompt in game: an offline message is only stored after you confirm.

## Consent and privacy

Direct messages require your consent. Open `/settings dm` to read the information and manage your consent. Under `/settings`, you can also choose who may contact you.

- `/block <player>` blocks a player.
- `/unblock <player>` unblocks them.
- `/blocked` shows your blocked players.

Use `/account messages` to open your stored messages.
