In chat you write in channels for everyone, for your party and for your clan. Under [Messages](/guides/chat#messages) you write to a player directly. A message has at most 256 characters. You choose who may write to you and what you see in chat.

## Channels

- **General:** `/allchat` or `/g`
- **Party:** `/partychat` or `/pc`
- **Clan:** `/clanchat` or `/cc`
- **Officers:** `/officerchat` or `/oc`, only for a clan's founder and officers

## How it works

- With a message: you write once in that channel, for example `/pc Hello!`.
- Without a message: the channel becomes your default. Everything you type normally goes there.

`/chat` shows your current channel. `/chat <channel>` selects one: `community` for general chat, `party`, `clan` or `officer`. Tab completion only suggests channels you may write in.

## Chat rules of thumb

- A message has at most 256 characters.
- Do not write too fast. Chat slows down repeats and lots of capitals. The same message twice in a row is not sent.
- Links only go to approved sites. Other links and blocked words are not sent.
- When someone writes your name, you hear a notification sound. Set this with **Name pings**.
- In the waiting room public chat, party chat and clan chat are paused. Direct messages still work.
- When an [update](/getting-started/lobby#server-switch-during-updates) moves you to a new server, the game shows you the chat from before again.

## Typed a command into chat by mistake?

If you type a known command with `7` instead of `/`, the game holds the message back. For example, `7friends` does not go straight into chat.

1. Read the chat notice.
2. If you meant a command, enter it again with `/`.
3. To send the text as a message anyway, click **YES**.

The confirmation sends the original text to its original destination, including direct messages. If it has expired, write the text again.

## Chat settings

Under `/settings chat` you choose:

- **Name pings:** whether you hear a notification sound when your name is mentioned
- **Join and leave notices** and **AFK notices:** all, selected or none. Selected means friends, party, clan, team and supporters.
- **Announce levels:** whether others see your level milestones
- **Thank my purchases publicly:** whether chat thanks you by name for a rank

If you turned off **Show online status** under `/settings privacy`, chat does not announce your joining and leaving. With a rank you can choose your own [arrival and departure message](/guides/inventory#arrival-and-departure).

All settings are under [Settings](/guides/settings).

How to report rule breaks in chat is explained under [Help](/getting-started/help).

## Messages

### Consent

You can only send direct messages once you consent. Open `/settings dm` for this. Direct messages are stored for 24 hours so that rule breaks can be reported. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Without consent, the game holds your message back briefly. Once you consent, you can still send or discard it.

### When someone is offline

If you write to a player who is offline, the game asks whether to leave the message. If you confirm, they see it on their next visit under `/account messages`.

If the recipient is AFK, your message is still delivered. The game tells you they may read it later.

### Who may write to you

Under `/settings requests` you choose who may send you direct messages: everyone, friends or nobody. Anyone you write to can reply for a while, even if you otherwise only allow friends or nobody. How long is set under **Reply access**: **Session** (until you leave the network) or 24, 12, 6, 3, 2 or 1 hour, 30 or 15 minutes. A fixed time restarts with every direct message you send.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/chat [community|party|clan|officer] [message]`: show or choose a channel, or write in it once
- `/allchat [message]`: general chat, also `/all`, `/ac`, `/g`, `/global`, `/localchat`, `/lc` and `/community`
- `/partychat [message]`: party chat, also `/pc`
- `/clanchat [message]`: clan chat, also `/cc`
- `/officerchat [message]`: officer chat, also `/oc`
- `/msg <player> <message>`: direct message, also `/dm`, `/w`, `/tell`, `/whisper`
- `/r <message>`: reply, also `/reply`
- `/block <player>`: block, also `/ignore`
- `/unblock <player>`: unblock, also `/unignore`
- `/blocked`: show blocked players, also `/ignored`

The player does not need to be online to be blocked.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can I not send a direct message?

Consent under `/settings dm`. The recipient's settings and blocks can also stop messages. More under [Messages](/guides/chat#messages).

### Can I write to offline players?

Yes. The game asks whether to leave the message. It arrives on their next visit.
