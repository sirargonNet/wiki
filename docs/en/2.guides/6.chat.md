Chat has channels for everyone, for your party and for your clan, and with [messages](/guides/chat#messages) you can write to a single player directly. You decide who may write to you and what shows up in your chat.

## Channels

- **General:** `/allchat` or `/g`
- **Party:** `/partychat` or `/pc`
- **Clan:** `/clanchat` or `/cc`
- **Officers:** `/officerchat` or `/oc`, only for a clan's founder and officers

## How it works

Add a message to the command and only that one message goes to the channel, for example `/pc Hello!`. Leave it out and the channel becomes your default, so everything you type normally goes there.

`/chat` shows which channel you're in. `/chat <channel>` picks one: `community` for general chat, `party`, `clan` or `officer`. Tab completion only suggests channels you're allowed to write in.

## Chat rules of thumb

- A message has at most 256 characters.
- Don't type too fast. If you repeat yourself or use lots of capitals, chat slows you down, and the same message twice in a row isn't sent at all.
- Links only work to approved sites; a message with any other link isn't sent. Blocked words are replaced with `***`.
- When someone writes your name, you hear a notification sound. You can switch it on or off with **Name pings**.
- In the waiting room, public chat, party chat and clan chat are paused, but direct messages still work.
- When an [update](/getting-started/lobby#server-switch-during-updates) moves you to a new server, you see the chat from before again there.

## Typed a command into chat by mistake?

If you type a known command with `7` instead of `/`, the game holds the message back first, so something like `7friends` doesn't go straight into chat.

1. Read the chat notice.
2. If you meant a command, enter it again with `/`.
3. To send the text as a message anyway, click **YES**.

Confirming sends your original text exactly where you meant it to go, direct messages included. If the confirmation has expired, you'll have to write the text again.

## Chat settings

Under `/settings chat` you choose:

- **Name pings:** whether you hear a notification sound when your name is mentioned
- **Join and leave notices** and **AFK notices:** all, selected or none. Selected means friends, party, clan, team and supporters.
- **Announce levels:** whether others see your level milestones
- **Thank my purchases publicly:** whether chat thanks you by name for a rank

If you've turned off **Show online status** under `/settings privacy`, chat doesn't announce when you join or leave. With a rank you can pick your own [arrival and departure message](/guides/inventory#arrival-and-departure).

Every setting is under [Settings](/guides/settings). [Help](/getting-started/help) explains how to report rule breaks in chat.

## Messages

### Consent

You can only send direct messages after you consent under `/settings dm`. They are stored for 24 hours so that rule breaks can be reported, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

If you write one without consenting, the game holds it back briefly. Consent then, and you can still send it or throw it away.

### When someone is offline

If you write to a player who is offline, the game asks whether to leave the message for them. Confirm, and they'll find it under `/account messages` on their next visit.

If the recipient is AFK, your message is still delivered, and the game tells you they may not read it until later.

### Who may write to you

Under `/settings requests` you choose who may send you direct messages: everyone, friends or nobody. Anyone you write to can reply for a while, even if you otherwise only allow friends or nobody. You set how long under **Reply access**: **Session** (until you leave the network) or 24, 12, 6, 3, 2 or 1 hour, 30 or 15 minutes. A fixed time starts over with every direct message you send.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

- `/chat [community|party|clan|officer] [message]`: show or choose a channel, or write in it once
- `/allchat [message]`: general chat, also `/all`, `/ac`, `/g`, `/global`, `/localchat`, `/lc` and `/community`
- `/partychat [message]`: party chat, also `/pc`
- `/clanchat [message]`: clan chat, also `/cc`
- `/officerchat [message]`: officer chat, also `/oc`
- `/msg <player> <message>`: direct message, also `/dm`, `/w`, `/tell`, `/whisper`
- `/r <message>`: reply, also `/reply`
- `/block <player>`: block, also `/ignore`
- `/unblock <player>`: unblock, also `/unignore`
- `/blocked`: show blocked players, also `/ignored`

You can block a player even when they're not online.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can I not send a direct message?

You need to consent under `/settings dm`. The recipient's settings and blocks can also stop messages. More under [Messages](/guides/chat#messages).

### Can I write to offline players?

Yes. The game asks whether to leave the message, and it reaches them on their next visit.
