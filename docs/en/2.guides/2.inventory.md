`/inventory` opens your inventory, and in the lobby the chest in your hotbar gets you there too. This is where you buy and activate boosters for double XP and equip effects, gadgets and chat cosmetics. You pay with Argonite, and some items come with a rank.

## Overview

At the top of the inventory you pick one of five areas:

- **Boosters:** your Network, SMP and Clan XP boosters, split across the tabs **Network**, **SMP** and **Clan**
- **Effects:** auras, trails and fireworks, which you browse with **Previous types** and **Next types**
- **Gadgets**
- **Pets:** **Coming soon**
- **Chat:** arrival, departure, cheers and respects, again, browse with **Previous types** and **Next types**

The inventory opens straight on **Boosters** and **Network**.

Below the areas you pick a kind. Each kind is a wide button with its short name in the middle, for example **Network**, **Auras** or **Arrival**, and no item picture. The areas above do show their pictures. The kind you picked is highlighted. A kind's tooltip shows the full name, and for boosters it also shows how many you have (**In stock**).

Auras, trails and gadgets are **In development**. You can already use them, but their values and effects may change.

## Boosters

There are three kinds:

- **Network XP Booster:** double network XP from active play
- **SMP XP Booster:** double SMP XP. You can only activate it in the SMP.
- **Clan XP Booster:** double Clan XP for every clan on the network. You can buy and activate it even without a clan of your own.

You get boosters from your streak, or you buy them for Argonite:

| Duration | Network XP   | SMP XP       | Clan XP      |
| :------- | :----------- | :----------- | :----------- |
| 30 min   | 250 Argonite | 300 Argonite | 200 Argonite |
| 60 min   | 450 Argonite | 550 Argonite | 350 Argonite |

Every offer doubles its kind of XP. Boosters you've already bought keep their base duration and XP effect, though a duration event can make them actually run longer. The menu lists the name, effect and duration separately.

To buy and activate a booster:

1. Open `/inventory`, choose **Boosters** and then **Network**, **SMP** or **Clan**.
2. Click **+ Buy** at the bottom right, choose 30 or 60 minutes and confirm the price shown.
3. Click the booster you bought and confirm the activation.

Until you activate it, a bought booster waits in your inventory.

An active booster works for all players. If one of the same kind is already running, yours joins the queue and starts as soon as the previous one ends. Boosts don't stack, so two boosters never make 4×. Once activated, a booster can't be taken back.

A bar at the top of your screen shows running boosters: which booster it is, who activated it and how much time is left. If more boosters are waiting, it also shows how many and how long the whole chain still runs.

### Team boosters

The team can put boosters straight at the end of the global queue. A team booster belongs to no player, but like every booster it doubles the XP of its kind for everyone. Instead of a player name, it shows a short text from the team or no name at all. If it starts during a booster duration event for its kind, it lasts twice as long.

The team can also take waiting boosters out of the queue. Yours then goes back into your inventory unused, and the boosters behind it move up. A running booster is never cancelled, and the order stays the same otherwise.

### Booster duration events

During an event, selected booster kinds last twice as long: 30 minutes become 60, and one hour becomes two. The XP effect stays at 2×.

What counts is when a booster starts in the global queue, not when it was bought or activated. That goes for purchased boosters, rewards and boosters granted by the team. If your booster starts before the event, its duration stays the same. If it starts during the event, it keeps the double duration even after the event is over. Overlapping events don't extend it again.

The game announces an event about a day ahead and again when it starts. During the event, the booster bar shows the event's remaining time separately from the booster's. `/booster` shows which duration events are active and how long they have left. When there's no event, the command points you to `/inventory`.

## Effects

Auras show particles around you, and trails follow your steps while you walk on the ground. On top of that there are level-up fireworks.

1. Open `/inventory`, choose **Effects** and then a kind, for example **Auras** or **Trails**. Each page holds two kinds: Auras and Trails first, then Fireworks.
2. Click **+ Buy**, choose an effect and confirm its price in Argonite. The kind shows the effects you bought and those your rank unlocks.
3. Click an unlocked effect to equip it, and click it again to unequip it. **No aura**, **No trail** or **No firework** also removes the effect of that kind.

You can equip one effect of every kind, plus one gadget. Purchases are permanent. Rank effects don't need buying: you can use them as long as the required rank is active.

| Kind      | Effect           | Price or rank  |
| :-------- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| Aura      | Sparks           | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura      | Hearts           | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura      | Notes            | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura      | Fire aura        | Prime or above |
| Aura      | Rain cloud       | Elite or above |
| Trail     | Dust             | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail     | Leaves           | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail     | Snowflakes       | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail     | Flame trail      | Prime or above |
| Trail     | Soul flame trail | Elite or above |
| Fireworks | Rank firework    | Prime or above |

With the **Rank firework**, fireworks light up in your rank colour on every network and SMP level-up. They're only particles, and players within 32 blocks see them.

Auras and trails work in the lobby, on Creative plots where you may build, and in the SMP outside combat. In the SMP they work in your own area, foreign areas and the wilderness. Cities, plots and camps can turn off auras, trails and fireworks on their land, as explained under [Rights and area settings](/smp/cities/rights#cosmetics).

Right-click an effect to choose under **Allowed contexts** where it may be active. Which effects from other players you see is up to you under **Effect settings**. See [Settings](/guides/settings#effects).

## Arrival and departure

In the **Chat** area of `/inventory` you can pick a message of your own that others see when you join or leave the network. It replaces the ordinary line.

| Kind      | Message           | Rank           | What it looks like                                   |
| :-------- | :---------------- | :------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrival   | Crystal arrival   | Prime or above | "The crystals hum, {player} is back."                |
| Departure | Crystal departure | Prime or above | "The crystals fall silent, {player} is gone."        |
| Arrival   | Portal arrival    | Elite or above | "A portal flickers open and {player} steps through." |
| Departure | Portal departure  | Elite or above | "The portal closes behind {player}."                 |

Like the ordinary line, it starts with `[+]` when you join and `[-]` when you leave, on your very first join too. `{player}` stands for your name. **Ordinary arrival message** or **Ordinary departure message** switches you back to the ordinary line. Players who have turned off other players' effects see the ordinary line.

## Cheers and respects

You'll find **Cheer** and **Respect** under **Chat** by browsing there with **Previous types** and **Next types**.

An equipped celebration effect styles your words **gz**, **w**, **gratz**, **grats** and **glückwunsch**, and a respect effect styles **f** and **o7**. Upper or lower case doesn't matter. In longer messages the word is styled too, but only the word itself. Everyone who receives your message sees it, no matter how far away they are. No extra line is added.

Click an unlocked variant to equip it. You equip celebration and respect separately, and **No celebration effect** or **No respect effect** removes that kind. With **+ Buy** you purchase each colour variant on its own, permanently, for Argonite.

### Rank set

- **Rank colour:** from Prime, static in the colour of your highest role.
- **Rank colour, animated:** from Elite, with a bright shine across your rank colour.

VIP, Media and team roles count too, by their rank weight. Rank sets can't be bought, and they only work while the matching rank is active. Any celebration and respect effects you had equipped before stay equipped as **Rank colour**.

### Purchasable variants

Each variant belongs to its own kind only. Gradients blend smoothly from letter to letter.

| Kind    | Appearance          | Variants                                              | Price per variant |
| :------ | :------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------- | :---------------- |
| Cheer   | Solid colour        | Gold, Emerald, Sky, Rose, Lavender, Orange, Ice, Ruby | 100 Argonite      |
| Cheer   | Gradient            | Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Aurora, Fire, Night            | 300 Argonite      |
| Cheer   | Simple animation    | Gold sparkle, Ice sparkle, Rose pulse, Sky pulse      | 500 Argonite      |
| Cheer   | Elaborate animation | Rainbow, Gold wave, Ocean wave, Gold shine            | 750 Argonite      |
| Respect | Solid colour        | Stone, Silver, Candlelight, Soul blue                 | 100 Argonite      |
| Respect | Gradient            | Moonlight, Ash, Soul fire                             | 300 Argonite      |
| Respect | Simple animation    | Silver fade, Candlelight fade                         | 500 Argonite      |
| Respect | Elaborate animation | Silver shine, Soul sparkle                            | 750 Argonite      |

Sparkle adds one changing character on each side. Pulse slowly changes the brightness, while Fade stays calm. Rainbow cycles through hues, Wave moves the letters one after another, and Shine sweeps a bright stripe across the word. You don't need to install anything extra for this.

Turn off **Reaction effects** under **Effect settings** and you see the ordinary word. **Simple only** shows solid colours, gradients and simple animations, and keeps elaborate animations hidden. **Chat animations** is on by default. Turn it off and animated variants show only their first colour, without sparkle characters. Shader mods can also leave the word static, but it stays readable.

Your [allowed contexts](/guides/settings#effects) restrict your equipped effect further. If you've limited it to certain territories in the SMP, chat hides it in all your SMP messages as a precaution. The styling doesn't change the XP for [congratulations and respects](/guides/currencies#congratulations), and a styled word doesn't by itself mean the reaction counts.

## Gadgets

1. Open `/inventory` and choose **Gadgets**.
2. Buy a gadget with **+ Buy**, or choose one your rank unlocks. Then click it to equip it.
3. In the lobby it sits in the third slot of your hotbar. Right-click to use it.

| Gadget            | Price or rank  | Effect                                                                           | Cooldown    |
| :---------------- | :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------- |
| Confetti cannon   | 200 Argonite   | colourful confetti with a bang in the direction you look; moves and hurts nobody | 1.5 seconds |
| Snowball Launcher | 250 Argonite   | a snowball with a gentle push and a snow effect                                  | 1 second    |
| TNT Push          | 350 Argonite   | throws TNT; after 1.5 seconds, pushes players within 5 blocks without damage     | 4 seconds   |
| Jump boost        | Prime or above | jump upwards and slightly forwards                                               | 2.5 seconds |
| Grappling hook    | Elite or above | pulls you towards the block it hits, with a range of up to 25 blocks             | 2 seconds   |

The cooldown shows above your hotbar, and once it's over, the game tells you your gadget is ready.

The grappling hook pulls you towards a block and holds you there. To let go, sneak, switch hotbar slots or throw again. If you miss every block within range, no cooldown starts.

All gadgets work only in the lobby, and you can't use them during parkour. They only push other players who have **Allow gadget hits** turned on. They don't hit you during parkour or while you're AFK.

## Commands

- `/booster`: active booster duration events and their remaining time
- `/inventory`: boosters, effects, gadgets and arrival and departure messages
