`/inventory` opens your inventory. In the lobby you can also click the chest in your hotbar. Here you buy and activate boosters for double XP. You also equip effects, gadgets and chat cosmetics. You pay with Argonite, and some items come with a rank.

## Overview

At the top of the inventory you choose one of five areas:

- **Boosters:** your Network, SMP and Clan XP boosters, with the tabs **Network**, **SMP** and **Clan**
- **Effects:** auras, trails and fireworks. Use **Previous types** and **Next types** to browse.
- **Gadgets**
- **Pets:** **Coming soon**
- **Chat:** arrival, departure, cheers and respects. Here too you browse with **Previous types** and **Next types**.

The inventory opens straight on **Boosters** and **Network**.

Below the areas you choose a kind. Each kind is a wide button with its short name, for example **Network**, **Auras** or **Arrival**. The name is centred, without an item picture. The areas above show their pictures. The chosen kind is highlighted. The tooltip shows the full name. For boosters it also shows how many you have (**In stock**).

Auras, trails and gadgets are **In development**. You can already use them, but their values and effects may change.

## Boosters

There are three kinds:

- **Network XP Booster:** double network XP from active play
- **SMP XP Booster:** double SMP XP. You can only activate it in the SMP.
- **Clan XP Booster:** double Clan XP for every clan on the network. You can buy and activate it without a clan of your own.

You get boosters from your streak or buy them for Argonite:

| Duration | Network XP   | SMP XP       | Clan XP      |
| :------- | :----------- | :----------- | :----------- |
| 30 min   | 250 Argonite | 300 Argonite | 200 Argonite |
| 60 min   | 450 Argonite | 550 Argonite | 350 Argonite |

Every offer doubles its kind of XP. Boosters you already bought keep their base duration and XP effect. A duration event can extend their actual running time. The menu lists the name, effect and duration separately.

To buy and activate a booster:

1. Open `/inventory`, choose **Boosters** and then **Network**, **SMP** or **Clan**.
2. Click **+ Buy** at the bottom right. Choose 30 or 60 minutes and confirm the displayed price.
3. Click your bought booster and confirm activation.

A bought booster waits in your inventory until you activate it.

An active booster works for all players. If one of the same kind is already running, yours joins the queue. It starts as soon as the previous one ends. Factors do not stack: two boosters never make 4×. An activation cannot be undone.

A bar at the top of the screen shows running boosters. It names the booster, who activated it and the time left. When more boosters wait, it also shows how many and how long the whole chain still runs.

### Team boosters

The team can put boosters straight at the end of the global queue. A team booster belongs to no player. Like every booster, it doubles the XP of its kind for everyone. Instead of a player name, it shows a short text from the team or no name at all. If it starts during a booster duration event for its kind, it lasts twice as long.

The team can take waiting boosters out of the queue. Your booster then returns unused to your inventory. The boosters behind it move up. A running booster is never cancelled, and the order does not change otherwise.

### Booster duration events

During an event, selected booster kinds last twice as long: 30 minutes become 60, and one hour becomes two. Their XP effect stays at 2×.

The start in the global queue counts, rather than the purchase or activation time. This applies to purchased boosters, rewards and boosters granted by the team. A booster that starts before the event keeps its usual duration. A booster that starts during the event keeps its double duration after the event ends. Overlapping events do not extend it again.

Ingame notices announce the event about a day ahead and when it starts. During the event, the booster bar shows the event’s remaining time separately from the booster’s. Use `/booster` to see active duration events and their remaining time. When no event is active, the command points you to `/inventory`.

## Effects

Auras show particles around you. Trails follow your steps while you walk on the ground. Level-up fireworks come on top.

1. Open `/inventory` and choose **Effects**. Then choose a kind, for example **Auras** or **Trails**. Each page holds two kinds: Auras and Trails, then Fireworks.
2. Click **+ Buy**, choose an effect and confirm its price in Argonite. The kind shows your bought effects and those unlocked by your rank.
3. Click the unlocked effect to equip it. Click again to unequip it. **No aura**, **No trail**, **No firework** also removes the effect of that kind.

You can equip one effect of every kind, plus one gadget. Purchases are permanent. Rank effects need no purchase. You can use them while the required rank is active.

| Kind      | Effect           | Price or rank  |
| :-------- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| Aura      | Sparks           | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura      | Hearts           | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura      | Notes            | 300 Argonite   |
| Aura      | Fire aura        | Prime or above |
| Aura      | Rain cloud       | Elite or above |
| Trail     | Dust             | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail     | Leaves           | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail     | Snowflakes       | 250 Argonite   |
| Trail     | Flame trail      | Prime or above |
| Trail     | Soul flame trail | Elite or above |
| Fireworks | Rank firework    | Prime or above |

- **Rank firework:** on every network and SMP level-up, fireworks light up in your rank colour. It is particles only. Players within 32 blocks see it.

Auras and trails work in the lobby, on Creative plots where you may build, and outside combat in the SMP. In the SMP they work in your own area, foreign areas and the wilderness. Cities, plots and camps can turn off auras, trails and fireworks on their land, see [Rights and area settings](/smp/cities/rights#cosmetics).

Right-click an effect to choose under **Allowed contexts** where it may be active. **Effect settings** controls which effects from other players you see. See [Settings](/guides/settings#effects).

## Arrival and departure

In the **Chat** area of `/inventory` you choose a special message that others see when you join or leave the network. It replaces the ordinary line.

| Kind      | Message           | Rank           | What it looks like                                   |
| :-------- | :---------------- | :------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrival   | Crystal arrival   | Prime or above | "The crystals hum, {player} is back."                |
| Departure | Crystal departure | Prime or above | "The crystals fall silent, {player} is gone."        |
| Arrival   | Portal arrival    | Elite or above | "A portal flickers open and {player} steps through." |
| Departure | Portal departure  | Elite or above | "The portal closes behind {player}."                 |

Like the ordinary line, it starts with `[+]` when you join and `[-]` when you leave, on your first join too. `{player}` stands for your name. **Ordinary arrival message** or **Ordinary departure message** takes you back to the ordinary line. Players who turned off other players' effects see the ordinary line.

## Cheers and respects

Find **Cheer** and **Respect** under **Chat**. Browse there with **Previous types** and **Next types**.

An equipped celebration effect styles your words **gz**, **w**, **gratz**, **grats** and **glückwunsch**. Respect styles **f** and **o7**. Letter case does not matter. In longer messages, only the individual word changes. Everyone receiving your message sees it, regardless of their distance from the recipient. There is no extra line.

Click an unlocked variant to equip it. **No celebration effect** and **No respect effect** remove that kind. Equip celebration and respect separately. Use **+ Buy** to purchase each colour variant individually and permanently with Argonite.

### Rank set

- **Rank colour:** from Prime, static in the colour of your highest role.
- **Rank colour, animated:** from Elite, with a bright shine across your rank colour.

VIP, Media and team roles also count according to their rank weight. Rank sets cannot be bought. They work while the matching rank is active. Your previously equipped celebration and respect effects remain equipped as **Rank colour**.

### Purchasable variants

Each variant belongs to its own kind. Gradients blend smoothly from letter to letter.

| Kind    | Appearance          | Variants                                              | Price per variant |
| :------ | :------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------- | :---------------- |
| Cheer   | Solid colour        | Gold, Emerald, Sky, Rose, Lavender, Orange, Ice, Ruby | 100 Argonite      |
| Cheer   | Gradient            | Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Aurora, Fire, Night            | 300 Argonite      |
| Cheer   | Simple animation    | Gold sparkle, Ice sparkle, Rose pulse, Sky pulse      | 500 Argonite      |
| Cheer   | Elaborate animation | Rainbow, Gold wave, Ocean wave, Gold shine            | 750 Argonite      |
| Respect | Solid colour        | Stone, Silver, Candlelight, Soul blue                 | 100 Argonite      |
| Respect | Gradient            | Moonlight, Ash, Soul fire                             | 300 Argonite      |
| Respect | Simple animation    | Silver fade, Candlelight fade                         | 500 Argonite      |
| Respect | Elaborate animation | Silver shine, Soul sparkle                            | 750 Argonite      |

Sparkle adds one changing character on each side. Pulse changes brightness slowly, while Fade stays gentle. Rainbow cycles through hues, Wave moves letters one after another, and Shine sweeps a bright stripe across the word. No additional installation is needed.

Under **Effect settings**, turn **Reaction effects** off to see ordinary words. **Simple only** shows solid colours, gradients and simple animations. Elaborate animations stay hidden. **Chat animations** is on by default. Turn it off to see only an animated effect's first colour, without sparkle characters. Shader mods may also leave words static; they remain readable.

[Allowed contexts](/guides/settings#effects) further restrict your equipped effect. In the SMP, a restricted territory choice hides the chat effect as a precaution. XP for [congratulations and respects](/guides/currencies#congratulations) stays the same. Styling alone does not mean a reaction counts.

## Gadgets

1. Open `/inventory` and choose **Gadgets**.
2. Buy a gadget through **+ Buy**, or choose one unlocked by your rank. Click it to equip it.
3. In the lobby it appears in the third hotbar slot. Right-click to use it.

| Gadget            | Price or rank  | Effect                                                                           | Cooldown    |
| :---------------- | :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------- |
| Confetti cannon   | 200 Argonite   | colourful confetti with a bang in the direction you look; moves and hurts nobody | 1.5 seconds |
| Snowball Launcher | 250 Argonite   | a snowball with a gentle push and a snow effect                                  | 1 second    |
| TNT Push          | 350 Argonite   | throws TNT; after 1.5 seconds, pushes players within 5 blocks without damage     | 4 seconds   |
| Jump boost        | Prime or above | jump upwards and slightly forwards                                               | 2.5 seconds |
| Grappling hook    | Elite or above | pulls you towards the block it hits, with a range of up to 25 blocks             | 2 seconds   |

The cooldown appears above your hotbar. When it ends, the game tells you that your gadget is ready.

The grappling hook pulls you towards a block and holds you there. Sneak, switch hotbar slots or throw again to let go. Missing a block within range does not start a cooldown.

All gadgets work only in the lobby. Gadgets cannot be used during parkour. Gadgets only push other players who have **Allow gadget hits** enabled. They do not hit you during parkour or while AFK.

## Commands

- `/booster`: active booster duration events and their remaining time
- `/inventory`: boosters, effects, gadgets and arrival and departure messages
