## SMP

One survival world for everyone: find your spot, build your home and trade with your neighbours.

## Creative

Your plot, every block. Try out ideas or build what you do not have the materials for in survival. Creative offers personal plots.

## Minigames

::callout{type="info" title="In development"}
Minigames are in development. The website introduces Knockout, Bedwars, Fishing and Challenges, among others. This does not promise that they are already playable.
::

Check the [website](https://sirargon.net/en-GB#modes) for the current status.
