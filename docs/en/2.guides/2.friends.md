description: 'Friendships and requests directly in game.'

---

Use `/friends` to open your friends. See who is online and which server they are on, where their privacy settings allow it.

- `/friends add <player>` sends a request.
- `/friends requests` shows pending requests.
- `/friends accept <player>` accepts a request.
- `/friends decline <player>` declines it.
- `/friends remove <player>` removes a friendship.
- `/friends join <player>` follows a friend when allowed and possible.

`/friend` is an alias for `/friends`. Find more actions with `/friends help`.

Want to join a round together? Create a [party](/guides/party).
