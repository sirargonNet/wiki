The network has two currencies: **Argonite** for the whole network and **Silver** in the SMP. You can see your balance in your [profile](/guides/account#profile) and in the sidebar.

While you play, you also earn XP for your network level, for example by congratulating others in chat. And if you play actively every day, your streak earns you rewards.

## Argonite

Argonite is the currency of the whole network. You get it, for example:

- as a [streak reward](/guides/currencies#streak)
- as thanks for helpful [bug reports](/getting-started/help)
- during team events

You spend it on [boosters](/guides/inventory#boosters), [auras and trails](/guides/inventory#effects), [gadgets](/guides/inventory#gadgets) and variants for [cheers and respects](/guides/inventory#cheers-and-respects). When you receive Argonite, a message lands in `/account messages`.

## Silver

Silver is the SMP currency. See [SMP](/smp).

## Wallets

**Wallets** in `/account` shows your Argonite and, on an SMP profile, your Silver too.

### Wallet history

Click a wallet to open its history, for example **Argonite history**. It lists your transactions, newest first, 20 per page, and the arrows take you through the pages. If you have several wallets, choose one under **Wallet**.

Each line shows the amount, currency and reason, plus the time and, if there is one, the other party: a player, a city or a nation.

If the history can't load, you see an error. Click **Retry** to load it again.

## Levels and XP

You collect XP while you play, and there are two levels:

- **Network level** for the whole network
- **SMP level** in the SMP, see [Skill levels](/smp/skills#levels-and-xp)

Your profile shows your level and how much XP you still need for the next one.

### Network XP

You mainly earn network XP by playing actively. While you're AFK, you earn none. You also get small bonuses for [congratulating and paying respects](/guides/currencies#congratulations).

A [network booster](/guides/inventory#boosters) doubles the XP from active play, but congratulations and respects don't count double.

### Level milestones

When you reach network level 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150 or 200, the whole network sees a message. Every other level is shown only to you. Turn off **Announce levels** in the [settings](/guides/settings#chat) and only you get the message.

## Congratulations

When someone reaches a level, a milestone or an advancement in the SMP, you can congratulate them: within 60 seconds, type only `gz` or `w` in chat. `gratz`, `grats`, `glückwunsch` and `glueckwunsch` count too, and `/gz` types `gz` for you.

- A congratulation gives you 5 network XP.
- `f` or `o7` pays respects to someone who died and gives you 1 network XP.
- There's only a limited amount of XP for this within 24 hours.

If you get no XP, chat tells you why: maybe you were too late, had already congratulated, or reached your limit. Your own success gives no XP either.

Click **gz** on an advancement and you congratulate exactly that success. If you type the word yourself, it goes to the newest open success.

You choose how your congratulation looks in chat under [Cheers and respects](/guides/inventory#cheers-and-respects).

## Streak

`/streak` opens your streak calendar, and in the lobby so does the clock in your hotbar.

- If you play actively enough on a day, that day counts towards your streak. The calendar shows how much active time you still need today.
- A day runs on German time, from midnight to midnight.
- Days with maintenance don't count against you.
- The team can set outage days, and they count towards your streak.

The calendar shows your current and your longest streak. Use **Previous week** and **Next week** to browse.

### Rewards

Daily rewards run in a 7-day cycle: after day 7 it starts again at day 1. The calendar shows what each day gives, for example Argonite or boosters.

On top of that, every streak day gives you Argonite. How much depends on your purchased rank:

| Rank    | Argonite per streak day |
| :------ | ----------------------: |
| no rank |                       1 |
| Prime   |                       5 |
| Elite   |                      10 |
| Legend  |                      15 |

Your highest purchased rank counts. With the role Administrator, Staff, Moderator, Helper, VIP or Media you get at least as much as with Elite, so 10 Argonite. A higher purchased rank such as Legend keeps its 15. Outage days only give the Argonite when they belong to a running streak, and days bridged by Ice give none.

Long streaks also earn milestone rewards:

| Milestone | Argonite |
| --------: | -------: |
|    7 days |       10 |
|   14 days |       20 |
|   30 days |       50 |
|   60 days |       75 |
|   90 days |      100 |
|  180 days |      200 |
|  365 days |      500 |

A milestone counts the days of one unbroken streak. Active days and outage days count; days bridged by Ice don't.

Collect the rewards you've reached with **Collect rewards**. They don't expire, and you can collect missed rewards later too, with **Claim missed rewards**.

### Backpay

Daily Argonite and milestones have existed since version 0.24.0. For the time before that, you get them paid out once:

- For every earlier streak day you get the daily Argonite, based on the rank you held that day.
- Outage days set by the team count if they belonged to a streak that was running at the time.
- Milestones are paid for all earlier streaks, including ones that have since ended.
- Rewards already paid out and the 7-day cycle stay as they are.
- If you held one of the roles above on an earlier streak day but only got the Argonite for no rank or for Prime, you get the difference up to 10 Argonite paid once. This also applies to days you already collected. Milestones don't change.

The backpay isn't credited automatically, so collect it in `/streak` with **Collect rewards**. Days of your current streak appear in the calendar, and rewards from ended streaks are in the list to collect. They don't expire.

### Ice

**Ice** protects your streak when you miss a day.

- You get 1 Ice for every third completed day.
- Collect waiting Ice with **Collect Ice**.
- Your stock and waiting Ice together come to at most 2.
- Every missed day uses 1 Ice, from your stock first, then waiting Ice.
- Once you're out of Ice, your streak starts over.
