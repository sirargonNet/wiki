## Set up

Install the official [Sirargons Mod-Mische](https://modrinth.com/modpack/sirargons-mod-mische) modpack. Simple Voice Chat is included. Use a Minecraft version that matches the network; see [Connecting](/getting-started/connect) for supported versions.

## Proximity and groups

Hear nearby players: the closer they are, the louder they sound. You can create a group with your friends. It stays together when you switch servers.

You can play normally without the voice mod. Voice chat is optional.
