## Where are my balance and my level?

Open `/account`. More under [Account](/guides/account).

## How do I link Discord?

Type `/minecraft link` on the Discord and `/discord link <code>` in game. More under [Account](/guides/account#discord).

## How do I sign in on the website?

Type `/web` and open the link. It works once and only for a short time.

## How do I play with friends?

`/friends` manages your [friends](/guides/friends), `/party` your [party](/guides/party).

## Why can I not follow a friend?

They must allow following, their online status must be visible and their server must have room.

## Why can I not send a direct message?

Consent under `/settings dm`. The recipient's settings and blocks can also stop messages. More under [Messages](/guides/messages).

## Can I write to offline players?

Yes. The game asks whether to leave the message. It arrives on their next visit.

## How do I hide my statistics?

Under `/settings privacy`, set **Statistics** to friends or only you.

## How do I change the language?

Under `/settings language`. More under [Settings](/guides/settings#language).

## How do I get voice chat?

With the official modpack. More under [Voice chat](/guides/voice-chat).
