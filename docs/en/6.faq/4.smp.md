## Why can I not join the SMP?

You need a [Discord link](/guides/account#discord) and Discord membership. Also accept the resource pack and confirm the rules.

## Are there homes or teleports?

There are no homes or warps. You travel on foot, by horse, boat or minecart. New players without a camp, city or plot can use `/rtp` to reach a safe place in the wilderness. More under [Starter teleport](/smp#starter-teleport).

## How do I protect my builds?

With a [camp](/smp/camps) or in a [city](/smp/cities). The wilderness has no protection.

## Where may I fight?

In the wilderness. In camps and cities only if the owners turned PvP on. Never at spawn.

## I died. Where are my items?

In your gravestone. `/graves` shows where it is. More under [PvP and death](/smp/death).

## Why did my items disappear?

Some items despawn faster in the SMP than in vanilla. More under [Changed mechanics](/smp/mechanics#item-despawn).

## How do I get silver?

Play actively, complete quests, reach milestones and trade. More under [Silver](/smp/silver), [Quests](/smp/quests) and [Milestones](/smp/milestones).

## Why do villagers not trade?

So that good finds keep their value. Where you get everything instead is under [Villagers](/smp/villagers).

## How do I trade with a player?

Stand at most 8 blocks from them and type `/trade <player>`. More under [Trading](/smp/trading).

## Why does my spawner produce nothing?

Spawners only work while an active player is nearby. More under [Spawners](/smp/spawners).

## How do I get skill points?

From SMP level 2 you get one per level. More under [Skills](/smp/skills).

## Is the Nether open?

Yes. Build a Nether portal and step through. More under [Realms](/smp/realms).

## Why do I not see any Nether quests?

Nether quests, milestones and the Nether skill tree appear once you have entered the Nether. Before that, `/quests`, `/milestones` and the skill tree do not show the Nether. When you first enter it, the chat tells you what is now unlocked. More under [Realms](/smp/realms).

## My farm runs slower than in vanilla.

Some values differ, for example for mobs, hoppers and the activation range. More under [Changed mechanics](/smp/mechanics).

## May I use Litematica?

Yes. Schematics, the preview, material lists and the verifier are allowed. Automatic placement is forbidden: the Easy Place mode, printer mods and add-ons and any other mod that places blocks by itself. How to turn both off: [Turn off Easy Place and printers](/reference/mods#turn-off-easy-place-and-printers).
