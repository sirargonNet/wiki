## Which address do I use?

`sirargon.net`. The steps are under [Connect](/getting-started/connect).

## Which Minecraft version do I need?

Java Edition 26.3. 26.2 is supported as well.

## Do I need mods?

No. For voice chat, use the official [modpack](https://modrinth.com/modpack/sirargons-mod-mische). Allowed mods are listed under [Mods](/reference/mods).

## May I use my own client?

Yes, if it is listed under [Clients](/reference/mods/clients) and you follow the rules for [mods](/reference/mods).

## Why can I not enter an area?

Accept the resource pack. Set **Server Resource Packs** to **Enabled** in the server list.

## All lobbies are full. What now?

You wait in a queue. The game shows your position. `/leavequeue` leaves it.

## Why am I in the waiting room?

You were AFK while it was busy, or no lobby was free. `/lobby` brings you back.

## Is AFK allowed?

Simply standing AFK, yes. Automated play with macros, autoclickers or bots, no.

## Where are the rules?

In game with `/rules` and under [Rules](/reference/rules).

## I found a bug.

Report it with `/bug`. More under [Help](/getting-started/help).

## Someone is breaking the rules.

Report them with `/report <player>`. More under [Help](/getting-started/help#report-a-player).

## Where do I get help?

In game with `/guide` and on the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).
