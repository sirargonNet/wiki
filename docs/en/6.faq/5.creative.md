## How do I get a plot?

With `/plot auto`. More under [Plots](/creative/plots).

## How do I let friends build with me?

With `/plot add <player>` while you are online, or `/plot trust <player>` even while you are offline.

## May I use WorldEdit?

Yes, on your own plot. More under [WorldEdit](/creative/worldedit).

## Can I download my plot or save it as a schematic?

No.

## Are there monsters or PvP?

No. It is always noon and there is no weather. With flags you set time and weather for your plot.

## May I use Litematica?

Yes, Litematica is allowed in Creative.
