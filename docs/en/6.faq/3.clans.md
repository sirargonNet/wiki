## How do I found a clan?

With `/clan found <tag> <name>`. You need a certain network level. More under [Clans](/guides/clans).

## How do I join a clan?

Depending on the clan: accept an invitation with `/clan accept <clan>`, apply with `/clan apply <clan>` or join directly with `/clan join <clan>`.

## How do I find a clan?

`/clans` opens the directory. `/clans search <text>` searches.

## Why can I not leave the clan?

As founder, first hand over founding with `/clan transfer <player>` or disband the clan with `/clan disband`.

## Who may do what in the clan?

Members can leave the clan. Officers invite, decide applications, remove members, manage clan bans and set the announcement. The founder may do everything. More under [Clans](/guides/clans#roles).

## How does my clan get more room?

With every clan level, more members fit in. Your clan gains experience while you play actively.

## How do I change the colour or tag?

As founder with `/clan colour <colour>` and `/clan tag <tag>`. There are 10 colours: 4 are free, 6 are unlocked by the founder's rank, from Prime. More under [Clans](/guides/clans#colour).
