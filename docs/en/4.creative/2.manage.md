## Co-builders and bans

Per plot you can add 4 players with `add` and 4 with `trust`, and ban 8 players.

## Flags

`/plot flag set <flag> <value>` changes your plot's settings, for example:

- time and weather
- greeting and farewell
- music
- whether visitors may use things
- redstone, water and lava

`/plot flag list` shows all flags. The flags for growth, melting and decay have no effect on Creative. Plants do not grow by themselves here, and ice and snow do not melt.

## Appearance

`/plot set floor|wall|border|middle` changes the floor, wall, border or the block in the middle.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

Instead of `/plot` you can use `/p`.

- `/plot add <player>`: let someone build while you are online
- `/plot trust <player>`: let someone build even while you are offline
- `/plot remove <player>`: take permissions away
- `/plot deny <player>`: ban from your plot
- `/plot kick <player>`: kick off your plot
- `/plot flag set <flag> <value>`: set a flag
- `/plot flag list`: list flags
- `/plot set floor|wall|border|middle`: change the look
- `/plot clear`: reset
- `/plot delete`: give up
