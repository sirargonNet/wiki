::infobox{title="Plots"}

:::infobox-row{label="Command" key="command"}
`/plot auto`
:::

::

On Creative you build on your own plots. `/plot auto` gets you the next free plot. Without a rank you have 2 plots, with a rank up to 8. You can merge your own plots, let friends build with you and set up your plot with flags.

## How many plots?

| Rank               | Plots |
| ------------------ | ----: |
| no rank            |     2 |
| Prime              |     4 |
| Elite              |     6 |
| Legend, VIP, Media |     8 |

## Merge plots

- `/plot merge` merges adjacent plots of your own into one area. The road between them becomes part of it.
- You can merge as many of your own plots as you may have: 2 with no rank, 4 with Prime, 6 with Elite and 8 with Legend, VIP or Media.
- Merging is free and never happens automatically.
- `/plot unlink` splits them again.

You cannot merge other players' plots with yours.

## Manage your plot

### Co-builders and bans

Per plot you can add 4 players with `add` and 4 with `trust`, and ban 8 players.

### Flags

`/plot flag set <flag> <value>` changes your plot's settings, for example:

- time and weather
- greeting and farewell
- music
- whether visitors may use things
- redstone, water and lava

`/plot flag list` shows all flags. The flags for growth, melting and decay have no effect on Creative. Plants do not grow by themselves here, and ice and snow do not melt.

### Appearance

`/plot set floor|wall|border|middle` changes the floor, wall, border or the block in the middle.

## Commands

`<…>` and `[…]` are placeholders; `[…]` is optional, `a|b` means one of them.

Instead of `/plot` you can use `/p`.

- `/plot auto`: get the next free plot
- `/plot claim`: claim the plot you stand on
- `/plot home`: go to your plot
- `/plot visit <player>`: visit a plot
- `/plot list`: list plots
- `/plot info`: plot information
- `/plot middle`: go to the middle
- `/plot merge`: merge adjacent plots of your own
- `/plot unlink`: split merged plots again
- `/plot like` and `/plot dislike`: rate
- `/plot add <player>`: let someone build while you are online
- `/plot trust <player>`: let someone build even while you are offline
- `/plot remove <player>`: take permissions away
- `/plot deny <player>`: ban from your plot
- `/plot kick <player>`: kick off your plot
- `/plot flag set <flag> <value>`: set a flag
- `/plot flag list`: list flags
- `/plot set floor|wall|border|middle`: change the look
- `/plot clear`: reset
- `/plot delete`: give up

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get a plot?

With `/plot auto`. More under [Commands](/creative/plots#commands).

### How do I let friends build with me?

With `/plot add <player>` while you are online, or `/plot trust <player>` even while you are offline.

### Can I download my plot or save it as a schematic?

No.
