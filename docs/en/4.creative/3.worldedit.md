You can use WorldEdit on your own plot.

## Select

- With the axe: left-click and right-click set the corners.
- `//pos1` and `//pos2` set the corners at your position.
- `//expand`, `//contract` and `//shift` change the selection.

## Edit

- `//set <block>` fills the selection.
- `//replace <old> <new>` replaces blocks.
- `//walls <block>` builds walls.
- `//overlay <block>` adds a layer on top.
- `//stack` and `//move` repeat or move.
- `//smooth` smooths terrain.

## Brushes

Brushes exist for sphere, cylinder and smoothing. `/size`, `/mask`, `/material` and `/range` set up the brush in your hand.

## Moving

`/ascend` and `/descend` take you one level up or down. `/thru` takes you through a wall, `/jumpto` to the spot you look at. `/unstuck` gets you out of blocks.

There are no schematics, plot downloads or plot saving.

## Commands

- `//wand`: get the selection axe, `/toggleeditwand` turns it off and on
- `//pos1` and `//pos2`: set corners at your position
- `//hpos1` and `//hpos2`: set corners at the block you look at
- `//expand`, `//contract`, `//shift`, `//outset`, `//inset`: change the selection
- `//chunk`: select a whole chunk
- `//size`: show the selection size
- `//set`, `//replace`, `//walls`, `//faces`, `//overlay`, `//center`, `//hollow`: edit
- `//line`, `//curve`: line or curve between the points
- `//naturalize`: make terrain look natural
- `//copy`, `//cut`, `//paste`, `//rotate`, `//flip`: copy
- `/clearclipboard`: clear the clipboard
- `//stack`, `//move`, `//smooth`: change
- `//sphere`, `//cyl`, `//pyramid`: shapes, hollow with `//hsphere`, `//hcyl`, `//hpyramid`
- `//fill`, `//drain`: fill a hole or remove liquid
- `/fixwater`, `/fixlava`: level water or lava
- `/brush sphere|cylinder|smooth`: brushes, also `/br`
- `/size`, `/mask`, `/material`, `/range`: set up the brush
- `/ascend`, `/descend`, `/ceiling`, `/up`: up or down
- `/thru`, `/jumpto`, `/unstuck`: through walls, to a target, out of blocks
- `//undo` and `//redo`: undo and redo
