## Which address do I use?

`sirargon.net`. See [Connecting](/getting-started/connect) for the steps.

## Which Minecraft version do I need?

Java Edition 26.3; 26.2 is also supported.

## Do I need mods?

You do not need the voice mod to play. For voice chat, use the official [modpack](https://modrinth.com/modpack/sirargons-mod-mische).

## How do I play with friends?

Manage friends with `/friends` and your group with `/party`. Read more about [parties](/guides/party).

## Where are my balance and level?

Open `/account` to find your [profile and currencies](/guides/account).

## Why is a direct message not arriving?

Check your consent under `/settings dm`. Privacy settings and blocks can prevent messages. For offline players, follow the prompt to store the message. Read more about [messages](/guides/messages).

## Are all minigames playable yet?

Minigames are in development. Check the [website](https://sirargon.net/en-GB#modes) for the status.

## Where can I get help?

The team and community help new players on the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).

## Where are the server rules?

The [rules page](/reference/rules) is a work in progress. No approved server rules have been published here yet.
