You may use the mods in the official modpack [Sirargons Mod-Mische](https://sirargon.net/modpack) everywhere. This page shows which other mods and clients are allowed and which are forbidden. Forbidden everywhere are, for example, X-ray, freecam, autoclickers and hacked clients, and in the SMP also mods that place blocks by themselves. The [rules](/reference/rules) apply as well.

## Forbidden everywhere

- Freecam and other free cameras
- X-ray mods and X-ray resource packs
- Radar and player tracking in map mods
- Cave mode in map mods, except in the vanilla Nether
- Autoclickers, macros, bots and auto-fishing
- World downloaders
- Hacked clients

This applies to every name, version and fork.

## Forbidden in the SMP

- Automatic placement: Litematica's Easy Place mode
- Printer mods and add-ons, for example Litematica Printer
- Any other mod that places blocks by itself

Litematica itself is allowed in the SMP: load schematics, show them as a preview, use material lists and check your build with the verifier. Place every block yourself.

## Turn off Easy Place and printers

1. Press `M` to open the Litematica menu and click **Configuration**. Or press `M` + `C`.
2. In the **Generic** tab, set `easyPlaceMode` to **false**.
3. In the **Hotkeys** tab, clear the binding of `easyPlaceToggle`. This way you do not turn Easy Place back on by accident.
4. Delete printer mods and add-ons from your `mods` folder. Turning them off is not enough; they must not be installed in the SMP.

## Map mods

- Surface map: allowed
- Waypoints: allowed
- Cave mode of the Xaero maps: allowed only in the vanilla Nether
- Radar and player tracking: forbidden

### Transfer your Xaero map and waypoints

You can keep your map and waypoints when moving to a new modpack instance:

1. Close Minecraft in both instances. Keep the old instance until you have checked the transfer.
2. Open the old game folder. In the Modrinth App, choose **Open folder**. In Prism, right-click the instance, choose **Folder**, then open `.minecraft` or `minecraft`.
3. Copy `xaero/world-map` and `xaero/minimap`, including their contents, into the new game folder.
4. If the old folders are named `XaeroWorldMap` and `XaeroWaypoints`, create `xaero` in the new game folder. Copy them into it and rename them to `world-map` and `minimap`.
5. Launch the new instance, connect and check your map and waypoints.

The [modpack page](https://sirargon.net/modpack) explains the steps for both launchers.
