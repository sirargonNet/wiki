You may use the mods in the official modpack [Sirargons Mod-Mische](https://modrinth.com/modpack/sirargons-mod-mische) everywhere. The [rules](/reference/rules) apply as well.

## Forbidden everywhere

- Freecam and other free cameras
- X-ray mods and X-ray resource packs
- Radar and player tracking in map mods
- Cave mode in map mods, except in the vanilla Nether
- Autoclickers, macros, bots and auto-fishing
- World downloaders
- Hacked clients

This applies to every name, version and fork.

## Forbidden in the SMP

- Automatic placement: Litematica's Easy Place mode
- Printer mods and add-ons, for example Litematica Printer
- Any other mod that places blocks by itself

Litematica itself is allowed in the SMP: load schematics, show them as a preview, use material lists and check your build with the verifier. Place every block yourself.

## Turn off Easy Place and printers

1. Press `M` to open the Litematica menu and click **Configuration**. Or press `M` + `C`.
2. In the **Generic** tab, set `easyPlaceMode` to **false**.
3. In the **Hotkeys** tab, clear the binding of `easyPlaceToggle`. This way you do not turn Easy Place back on by accident.
4. Delete printer mods and add-ons from your `mods` folder. Turning them off is not enough; they must not be installed in the SMP.

## Map mods

- Surface map: allowed
- Waypoints: allowed
- Cave mode of the Xaero maps: allowed only in the vanilla Nether
- Radar and player tracking: forbidden

## Not listed

Ask first on the [official Discord](https://discord.sirargon.net).
